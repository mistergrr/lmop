# Setting the Scene
- **Atmosphere:** Describe the oppressive stench, ankle-deep muck, and flickering torchlight. Water drips constantly. Strange echoes make direction hard to judge.
- **Hazards:** Narrow walkways, broken ladders, and sudden drops. Make simple navigation a challenge—perfect for Perception and Acrobatics checks.
- **Signs of the Gang:** Graffiti (a rat skull), chewed corpses, discarded syringes, torn scraps of black cloth. Maybe an ambush site with blood trails leading deeper in.
# Rooms
1. Entrance. Graffiti "Death-death to man-tings"
2. **Mush Room**. The stone walls are dry and flaking, the floor coated in a fine layer of pale dust. A cluster of five large mushrooms grows from cracks in the floor, their caps emitting a faint teal glow that makes the air shimmer. **DC 14 PER check** to notice the faint tremble of the mushrooms before they release spores.
	- **Sporeburst Hazard**
		- **Trigger.** A creature approaches within 5 feet of a glowing mushroom.
		- **Effect.** The mushroom releases a 5-foot-radius cloud of spores. Each creature in the area must succeed on a **DC 13 Constitution saving throw** or take **7 (2d6) poison damage** and be **poisoned** for 1 minute (save end on turn end). While poisoned in this way, the creature’s vision swims with phosphorescent light—creatures and objects within 10 feet emit a faint glow, but everything beyond appears hazy and indistinct.
		- **Persistence.** The cloud lingers for **1 minute** before settling into a thin dusting of glowing residue.
		- **Countermeasures.** The spores can be neutralised with **fire** (burning the mushrooms), **gusts of wind**, or **water** to wash them down cracks in the floor.
