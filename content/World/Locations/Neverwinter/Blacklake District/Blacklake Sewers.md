A honeycomb network of sewers beneath the [[Blacklake District]]. The [[Dead Rats]] have set up their home here. Gang members dissuade trespassers with traps, portcullises, tripwires near beds of spikes, and so on. Secure in their holes, the Rats steal, sabotage, and generally make life in Neverwinter a trial.

# Inhabitants
Besides the [[Dead Rats]], the sewers are also home to any creatures that might be found in a sewer complex, such as otyughs and carrion crawlers. Spiders, shambling mounds (of questionable substance), oozes (made of questionable substance), giant roaches, Troglodytes are good here. They wouldn't mind the smell at all. Maybe a gibbering mouther.
