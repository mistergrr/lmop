# Day 7
[[Grrurika]] returns to [[Neverwinter]] and head's straight to [[Smith]]'s to see about putting the ruby into [[Wolfsbane]]. It costs 150gp and will take 3 days to complete.

She then checks in at the [[Steel Wolves' HQ]] and sees [[Captain Thorne Blackwood|Captain Thorne]]. The captain awards her a promotion to corporal. This will come with a salary increase to 240sp per week. It also means she can bring 2 sidekicks with her on missions (provided they are a lower rank than her).

Her first mission is to investigate the wererat attack. [[Captain Thorne Blackwood|Captain Thorne]] is convinced that with one attempt on the Lord Protector's life, there will be more until they succeed or are wiped out completely.

[[Mission — Fix That Rat]]

[[Grrurika]] goes to meet her fellow soldiers and finds them hanging out together. [[Schuul Snobb|Schuul]] is showing off his corporal badge to the others and notices [[Grrurika]] has one, too. He issues a friendly challenge to see who can make it up the ranks faster from here.

[[Grrurika]] takes [[Perts Dangling]] and [[Eihr Zissyu]] and briefs them on the mission. Everyone kits up with silvered weapons and heads out.

The first place they go to is the scene of the wererat attack, near [[World/Locations/Neverwinter/Castle Never|Castle Never]]. [[Eihr Zissyu]] manages to find tracks that match a humanoid rat's and shrink down to a rat's. They follow the tracks into a quiet alley where they lose the rat's tracks amongst a mess of other rats.

Further investigation around ratholes reveals a symbol carved into the nearby stone wall. [[Perts Dangling|Perts]] mentions that he's overheard talks of a new gang called the [[Dead Rats]]. He thinks this was the symbol associated with them, but doesn't know any more.

[[Grrurika]] decides to check in on [[Babbelbell]] to see if he knows anything. They find him and she gives him some food. [[Babbelbell]] says there are tonnes of gangs around, but he knows that the [[Dead Rats]] have been recruiting homeless and the disenfranchised in the area. They infect new recruits with wererat lycanthropy to keep them loyal. He's been tempted to join for the money and support, but has been on the fence. [[Grrurika]] urges him not to and to seek her out if he starts getting desperate.

[[Babbelbell]] says the [[Dead Rats]] carve their symbol to mark their territory and to find their way in the dark. He takes them to a sewer entrance with one such mark.

## Into the Sewers
The party head down into the sewer. In the first chamber, they come across someone hiding amongst the filth. They only sit and stare but relax when [[Grrurika]] offers them a drink of water. The mysterious character refuses to speak but nods and shakes their head in response to her questions.

Through the stranger, the party learn that the [[Dead Rats]] use this entrance semi-frequently. They continue deeper and [[Grrurika]] starts mapping the dungeon. It's a maze of corridors and long tunnels. The first chamber they find is a tiny one — it's just a crossroads, but the words "Death to humans" is painted on the walls. One of the paths brings them to a storeroom.

The storeroom is a mix of junk and somewhat useful items — the most valuable being a greatsword with a wavy blade. [[Grrurika]] is uninterested in taking anything, so [[Perts Dangling|Perts]] nabs the sword. It isn't silvered, so [[Grrurika]] makes him sheath it and continue using the silvered weapon.

The next door out of here is heavy and locked. A couple of kicks fails to break it down so [[Grrurika]] takes a run up and smashes into it. Her momentum carries her too far and she's unable to react to the false floor trap she stumbles into. The trap is a 10ft drop into a pit. [[Eihr Zissyu|Eihr]] and [[Perts Dangling|Perts]] help her out. This room has a couple of exits back out to the top, as well as another path back into the sewers.

They take the path into the sewers and find they've completed a loop. Wayfinding unexplored paths eventually leads them to a solid metal door with a lever to open it.

## The Puzzle Room
A few things of note stand out about this 10ft high room. There's a half-rotted body near the door clutching a [[Potion of Water Breathing]] and a [[Dead Rats]] calling card. There are four stone pillars in each corner, 6ft high. Each has a carved symbol (lock, wave, flame, eye) on each side. Scorch marks are on the wall next to one. In the centre of the room is a grate that's sealed shut. This room is more damp than any of the previous ones.

The last thing of note is a locked metal door on the far end with a lever next to it. [[Grrurika]] is cautious and inspects the dead body as best as she can. She notes that they died of drowning and she takes the [[Potion of Water Breathing]] off them. She also notices circular scratch marks at the bottom of each pillar and realises they can be rotated.

Starting with the pillar with the scorch marks, she orients that so the flame points toward the centre of the room. She turns the pillar near the body to "water" and the remaining one near the locked door to "lock." By a process of elimination, she orients the last pillar to "eye."

Nothing of note happens immediately and [[Perts Dangling|Perts]] wonders if they have to pull the lever. [[Eihr Zissyu|Eihr]] doesn't like that idea and thinks they'll spring the trap they're sure this room is loaded with. He props the dead body in the open door they entered through just in case.

Unsure of what else to do, [[Grrurika]] decides to go for it and orders [[Perts Dangling|Perts]] to pull the lever.

The chamber echoes with scraping and clicking noises. The grate at the centre opens fully and the locked metal door opens with a click.

Further into the sewers they head.
