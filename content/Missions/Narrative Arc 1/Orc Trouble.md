# Location
[[Wyvern Tor]]

> The faint smell of smoke hangs on the air as you ascend a rugged ridge on the lower slopes of the hill. 150ft away, a cave mouth opens up at the bottom of a ravine. Hunkered down by a boulder 60ft outside the cave, keeping watch, is a single orc

If the characters stealth kill the lookout, they have a chance to surprise the remaining enemies. If the sentry spot the characters or it isn't silenced in the surprise round, they retreat to the cave to warn the others.
