# Mission
Find a way into [[Harbin Wester]]'s home and find any incriminating evidence against him. Feel free to loot the place, but if they take too much, he'll know. Infiltrate and avoid killing. The key is discretion.
# Location
[[Phandalin|Phandalin]]
# Encounters
- Personal guard — Thinks guarding a corrupt official is an easy gig but will flee if outmatched.
- "Banana Muffin:" A Loud, Yappy Dog — Not a trained guard dog, just a little thing that makes a lot of noise if someone breaks in.

## Entry Points & Obstacles
- **Front Door** – Locked, but Harbin isn't rich enough for an expert lock (_DC 13 Thieves’ Tools_).
- **Windows** – Easy to force open (_DC 10_), but risk alerting Banana Muffin.
- **Chimney** – Risky but possible—if someone’s willing to squeeze through.

## Guard & Dog Response
- **Banana Muffin Barks** → Alert countdown begins (1-2 minutes before guard reacts).
- **Lazy Guard Investigates** → He’ll check but is hesitant to fight. If outnumbered, **he runs**.

## The Safe & Lockbox
- In Harbin’s office.
- **Breaking In** – Heavy lock (_DC 15_), or **find the key** (hidden in a desk, under his mattress, etc.).
- **Inside** – Gold, the location of [[Harbin's Lockbox]], and [[Harbin's Letter]].

# Secrets and Clues
- [[Harbin Wester|Harbin]] has an escape plan should he need to flee. It includes fake travel papers and a secret cache stashed somewhere just outside of town.

# Consequences
- **If They’re Caught** – The guard informs [[Harbin Wester|Harbin]], who will head straight to his secret cache and flee town.
- **If Harbin Is Removed** – Does [[Halia Thornton|Halia]] take control of the town?
- **If They Steal Too Much** – [[Harbin Wester|Harbin]] will try pressing charges.
