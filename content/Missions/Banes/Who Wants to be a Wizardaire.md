# The Quiz
Run like "Who Want to be a Millionaire." At the start, a bound **book thief** is presented. They failed to return library book — regarded as stealing. Each round, [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] must choose a spell from her _learned spells_ list and cast it into a vessel of *Spell Storing*. Does not use a spell slot and automatically hits. Once a spell is cast, it cannot be chosen again for 3 rounds.

- With every **correct question**, the chosen spell is cast on the mage. 

# Lifelines
Three Lifelines. Each can only be used once.
- **50/50**
- **Ask the Audience** – Quick poll. Still up to the contestant whether or not to go with the results.
- **Phone a Friend** – 30-second call.
# Safe Havens
More correct answers, more rewards.
- **5 Questions**: **Library Membership**: Tome to fix bane:*Where's My Scryglass?: A Guide to Remembering Things for Scholars Who Read A Lot*".
- Access to [[Shadow Codex]] and other books.
- **10 Questions**: **Well Learned**: Permanent +1 increase to Intelligence; Gain proficiency in Arcana and History. If already have proficiency, gain expertise.
- **15 Questions**: **"Wait a minute, I know this"** (1/day): Recall any previously learned detail, fact, or event with perfect accuracy.
