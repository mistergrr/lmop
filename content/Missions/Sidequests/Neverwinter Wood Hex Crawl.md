# Party Travel Speed (3-mile forest hexes)
## Before Meeting the Ranger (Unaided Travel):
**3 hexes per day**
Each time the party enters a new hex:
> “Who’s leading the way today? Roll a **Survival check** to stay on course through the forest.”
- **DC 12**: clear weather
- **DC 14–15**: bad visibility (rain, blight, fog)
- On failure: the party moves into a random adjacent hex. Ranger encounter on failure.

## After Meeting the Ranger (Guided Travel):
- **4 hexes per day**
- **No Survival checks needed**
