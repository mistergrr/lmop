# Map
![[Starmetal-Hills-hex.jpg]]

- Path through hills is **overgrown** and difficult to follow at times.
- Shrines with offerings of **survival supplies** spread along route.
- Village accessed eastwards and westwards via **mountain passes**. Lies by a clear, still **lake**, _Lake Glorfindar_ ("_the lake of fair fortune_"), fed by a **river** from the north.
- **Stone and wood buildings** cluster around shore. Odour of **fish**. **Chapel** 60ft tall. Building with **waterwheel** on the river. Across the river, a giant **telescope** sits in a semi-covered hut.
