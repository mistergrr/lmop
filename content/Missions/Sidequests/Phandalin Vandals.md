> Vandels in Phandalin! Our beutiful town has been conflicted with a wave of troublemakers and ne'er-do-wells. 50 gp reward to whomsoever brings the perpertraitors to justice. See Sherif Richtor Helmsley III for more details.

Someone has been on a vandalism spree around [[Phandalin|Phandalin]], but all the graffiti has been specifically targeting [[Richtor Helmsley III]].
# Clues
## Dancing Lights
- Witnesses have seen four lamp lights around the statue at night.
- The lights twinkle on a one minute oscillation.
- The lights look like dim torches.
## Perpetrator
- A stray cat that lives at the statue gets cosy with Thia and no one else.
- Elegant handwriting.
- [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] always has magic ink and paint on her sleeves.
## Magic Paint
- "SherifF SildAR's simp the Turd."
- The paints have magic components, making _Prestidigitation_ ineffective at removing it.
## Time of Crime
- [[Ziggywix Tinkerton|Ziggywix]] goes home at 2am and the statue is always fine.
- The baker wakes up at 4am and the statue is always desecrated.

# How
[[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] wakes up at 2am every night and goes on a vandalism spree, painting [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor's]] statue and leaving murals around town. She uses magic paint, which makes _Prestidigitation_ ineffective at removing it. She lights her scene with _Dancing Lights_. Graffiti sites include the statue, [[The Wee Inn]], and the townmaster's hall.
