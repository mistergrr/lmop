A merchant by the road asks the party for help. Says their horse got spooked and ran off road into the **swamp**. The horse got stuck in the muck and sent him flying. **Monsters** crawled out of the bog and ate his horse, hooves and all.

# Objectives
Retrieve the lost merchandise, specifically a small box that was on the cart. 20 silver reward.

# Characters
**Merchant**. Wears a short sword, longbow and leather armour.
- *Scout who killed the real merchant, but was chased off by the monsters.*
- *Wants to bring health potions back to poor hamlet, where the elderly are sick and dying without aid.*

# The Crash Site
>Humanoid **monsters** roam about, waiting to drag victims into the swamp to be consumed. A **cart** lies overturned in the muck. The bodies of a **horse** and a **human** lie nearby.

- **Monsters**. Eight **drowners**. Hungry for human flesh.
- **Cart**. Contents strewn about. Couple of **arrows** in the front, three in the back.
	- **Treasure**. Iron ore, flowers, rations, 3 flasks of lamp oil, locked **strongbox**.
	- **Strongbox**. DC 13 to open. Contain 5 health potions.
- **Horse**. Two **arrows** in the throat and side. Body untouched otherwise.
- **Human**. Dressed in loose clothing. Half eaten by the monsters.
	- **Arrow** in the throat.

# The Return
**If the party return the box**:
- 20 silver reward.

**If the party confronts them**:
- Will try to distract, then hop on horse to flee. [[#Skill Challenge]] to catch.

# Skill Challenge
>Tracked individually. Fail limit = CON mod. Success target: 3/4/5 (easy/med/hard). DC = Quarry's roll.
- Obstacle 1: Getting off the mark.
- Obstacle 2: Jump over log.
- Obstacle 3: Round a sharp corner.
- Obstacle 4: Straight sprint

**Challenge successful**; Brigand gets knocked off horse. They'll reveal themselves as Hock Greenson, a hunter who wants to bring aid back to their hamlet, where the elderly are sick and dying. Begs to be aided/spared.

**Challenge failed**: Leads to [[#An Ambush]]

# An Ambush
>The brigand leads the party to a straight stretch of road flanked by tree cover before stopping and turning, sword drawn. Four other **humans** start shooting from the trees.

- **Humans** (Scout stat block). On foot. Use the trees for cover.
	- **Loot**. 104 silver, 113 copper, 7 rations; Leather armour, longbow, shortsword.
