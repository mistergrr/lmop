A villager has been casting curses on other locals. They write the victim's name and a curse down (e.g. "I bind the feet of...") and throw it together with an offering down a nearby dry well. Within a week, those cursed suffer an ailment of sorts.
# Secrets
- A {banshee or other creature} uses the curses to choose their next victim.
- The perpetrator has been let down by local authorities and turned to curses for justice.
