# The Temple of the Dawning Light
## Opening Scene
- They enter the [[Temple of the Dawning Light]].
- A skeletal giant elk leaps out from within, its horns dripping with blood.
- “**Life flows only through sacrifice**.” Healing spells no longer create hit points—they *transfer them*. Resurrection, if attempted, would require someone else to trade their life.
- **Health potions nullified** — While in the Temple, only the gods or mortals gifted by the gods can create life.
## Mission
Rekindle the [[Hand of Redemption]]

# Trial 1: This is My Body
## NPCs
- Priest of [[Lathander]]: mortally wounded by the skeletal giant elk.
## Opening Scene
> The chamber is covered in rust and vine-choked brass. Thorny brambles cover the floor. There's a blood trail that leads to a man bleeding on the floor. The man is dressed in robes with the symbol of [[Lathander]] on the front—Road traveling into a sunrise
- The priest had approached the skeletal giant elk, thinking it was [[Lathander]], but the undead beast attacked them, mortally wounding them.
- They ask to be carried to the altar.
- They're bleeding out continually. To keep them alive, the cleric must siphon HP from someone.
#### Twist:
- The path is blocked by thorns. Every step costs HP to anyone barefoot.
- Divine healing is the only way to sustain the burden—but every spell they cast takes life from someone else.
#### Mechanics:
- DC 12 STR checks to carry the weight (movement speed halved)
- The figure loses HP every round.
- Choice each round:
    - Spend HP from someone to keep the burden alive
    - Let the figure weaken, risking trial failure
- If the priest dies, they must carry the corpse to the altar—completing the physical act, but failing the moral test (trial 2 consequence)
### The Hand of Redemption:
- Above the altar floats an artifact—the [[Hand of Redemption]], a slightly cracked, radiant-but-dimming hand-shaped relic. It looks like it's reaching out. There is also a [[Scripture of Lathander]] here.
	- **If alive**, the priest will siphon life from it, staunching his bleeding.
	- **If the priest died**, when touched, the hand will move on to the next room.
- The hand will fly through a small opening to the next room, which is sealed by a large stone boulder.

### The Dawn Engine
> Near the boulder lies a **massive wheel** inset in the floor. The wheel is cracked, stiff with decay and the tangle of vines. Gears connect the wheel to an aperture in the ceiling. A sliver of light shine through the barely open gap aperture. 
**The Task**:  
Use the Dawn Engine to illuminate [[Lathander]]'s murals once more.
**Trial Elements**:
1. **Seized Mechanism**
	> The wheel and lens arms are rusted, jammed, and buried under vines and rubble.
	- The wheel must be turned through coordinated strength. DC 18 Athletics (STR) check, each additional member reduces DC by 3 -OR- party must pass DC 15 average.
	- As it turns, an aperture opens and radiant light breaks through, hitting misaligned lenses.
	- **Failure**: The handles of the wheel crack.
2. **Calibrating Ancient Lenses**  
	> Five numbered lenses must shine their light on the murals in the correct order.
#### The Murals
1. **The First Dawn** – A radiant being (Giant Elk) breathes life into the sky and land.
2. **The People Forget** – Mortals turn to their own power.
3. **The Lost Light** – Days of darkness follow.
4. **The Light Returns** – The light returns.
5. **The New Dawn** – Rebirth and redemption

Once the murals are lit, a sealed door behind it opens—the true temple heart lies beyond, untouched since [[Lathander]]’s fall.

### The Path of Echoes
> You find yourselves on a narrow, winding path that floats above a sea of shimmering light. Along the path, you hear echoes—voices of those you’ve healed, saved, or helped throughout you journey. These voices speak of gratitude, but as the cleric progresses, the echoes shift to voices of those who were harmed by the curse, expressing pain, fear, and betrayal. As the cleric moves forward, they must face illusions of these individuals. Each illusion presents a moral challenge, such as confronting an ally who distrusts them because of the curse or meeting a spectral version of themselves who tempts them to embrace the curse’s power. These challenges test the cleric's resolve, compassion, and willingness to confront the consequences of their actions.
#### Illusions:
- Deuteronomy
- Beggars of [[Neverwinter]] (Babblebell)
- Steel Wolves' companions: Clobb, Eihr, Perts, Schuul

## Trial 2: Trial of the Soul
> This realm is filled with a serene dawn landscape—a place where the sun is eternally rising, casting long shadows that stretch unnaturally far.
> You arrive at a crossroads guarded by three entities, each an image of [[Grrurika]], but representing a different aspect. They stand on pedestals labelled: The Tempter, The Judge, and The Healer.
The cleric must confront each entity in order to pass. Her allies can volunteer to take the damage on her behalf.
### The Tempter:
- A shadowy figure with eyes glowing with necrotic energy. This entity whispers promises of unmatched power and eternal life if the cleric would only submit to the curse. Heal if they choose power, or take necrotic damage if they choose their original path.
### The Judge:
- A radiant, stern figure that embodies justice and balance. This entity demands that the cleric account for their past actions and make a choice: to be judged by the weight of their sins or to judge themselves. They must relive the suffering they’ve caused others through the curse, feeling their pain as their own. Take necrotic damage for each time a party member who suffered as a result of [[Grrurika]] not healing them.  Take psychic damage for each sin.
#### Grrurika Sins
- Killing [[Lathander]]
- Defiling the body
- Siphoning [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] to death during Mimic fight
- Choosing not to heal [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] during undead ogre fight
- Kicking PoW Hobgoblin to death in the nuts
- Did not attempt to heal [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] when he blew himself up
- Did not heal [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] when she was downed by goblins outside [[Cragmaw Castle]]
#### Grrurika Deeds
- Revives [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] during goblin night ambush on the way to [[Helm's Hold]]
- Heals [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] during wolf fight at [[Helm's Hold]]
- Heals [[Dorian Blackthorn]] during Reconsecration
- "*Bless*" the Dorians and [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] during [[The Arcane Convergence]]
### The Healer:
- A gentle, sorrowful figure bathed in a soft light, representing the cleric’s original devotion to healing and life. This entity offers the cleric forgiveness but warns that true redemption requires sacrifice. Each teammate who put themselves on the line for her proves her worth. Restore health for each teammate who has taken damage on her behalf.

## Trial 3: The Spirit Chamber
- Upon passing the **Trial of the Soul**, the cleric's allies should be low on health. 
- A pathway opens to the final chamber. It's like the Matrix's white room, except there's a cloud of death lingering in the air. At the centre of the chamber, the [[Hand of Redemption]] floats above an altar, its light is flickering as if dying out.
- [[Lathander]] calls out to her to take the hand. Only the cleric is able to move.
- Each step (5ft) towards the [[Hand of Redemption]] reduces the cleric's health — just enough so they reach 5HP when they reach the hand.
	- Every 5ft, the cleric looks back at the door and sees the exit is within reach, giving them the choice to continue or leave. The halfway mark is the point of no return.
	- The cleric recalls a memory every 5ft.

### The Final Choice
As the cleric reaches the [[Hand of Redemption]], they are faced with a final decision:
- **Sacrifice**: Siphon their own life essence to rekindle the spark. This act of humility and selflessness purifies their soul, breaking the curse and enhancing their connection to the life domain. The chamber fills with a brilliant, blinding light, symbolizing their redemption.
- **Self-preservation**: Drain the spark—saves the cleric, but extinguishes [[Lathander]]’s last reach in the region. The light within the chamber dims, and a shadowy aura surrounds the cleric as they embrace their new, darker powers.
# Results
**Redemption or Damnation**
	- **If They Chose Power:** The cleric leaves the temple as a Death Cleric. Their powers are formidable, but they no longer have a focus on healing and supporting the team.
	- **If They Chose Sacrifice:** The cleric emerges from the temple bathed in a radiant glow, their curse lifted, and their healing abilities restored. Their allies may view them with renewed respect and trust, having witnessed their selflessness and commitment to the light. [[Lathander]]’s light is restored in the region, removing all traces of the blight.
