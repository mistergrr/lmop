# The Search for the Library of Whispers
The [[Tome of Eternal Recall]] can be found in an ancient, forgotten library — [[Library of Whispers]]. One of the largest repositories of knowledge, including forbidden secrets. Sealed away by [[Oghma]] to prevent excess power leading to ruin.

- **Recovering Ancient Maps or Texts**: Pieces of maps are scattered across Faerun, guarded by powerful entities, hidden in lost temples, or protected by ancient wards.
- **Consulting with Lost Scholars**: Spirits of long-dead scholars who once visited the library.
- **Solving the Riddle**: A riddle or puzzle locks the entrance.

![[Library of Whispers#Clues]]

- **Guardian of the Library**
	- Owl-shaped spirit. 10ft tall.
	- Attitude uncertain — ancient & foreign demeanour

# Who Wants to be a Wizardaire
Library membership is 1,000 gp or take part in The Quiz. Need membership to borrow books. Membership cannot be shared/transferred.

## The Quiz
Run like "Who Want to be a Millionaire." At the start, a bound **book thief** is presented. The mage failed to return library book — regarded as having stolen the books. Each round, [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] must choose a spell from her _learned spells_ list to cast into a vessel of *Spell Storing*. Does not use a spell slot and automatically hits. Once a spell is cast, it cannot be chosen again for 3 rounds.

- With every **correct question**, [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] casts her chosen spell on the mage. 
- With every **incorrect** question, a punishment is chosen by random.

**Involving the party**: The wizard can "phone a friend." They're limited to calling each friend once and only have 2 minutes to discuss the question.

## Punishments
Punishments last 1 round each
# Restoring Knowledge
Upon winning the game, the reward is the [[Tome of Eternal Recall]].
- Borrow one other book. Possibly [[Shadow Codex]]

The tome allows the wizard to regain all the spells and lore they had forgotten, and it grants them the ability to protect their minds from future forgetfulness. The remember _Chong's Cloudblast_
# Reward: The Boon of Eternal Recall
As a reward for their perseverance, the [[Tome of Eternal Recall]] grants the wizards these boons:

- **The Gift of Memory**: The wizard gains a permanent +1 increase to their Intelligence score, They also gain proficiency in Arcana and History. If they already have proficiency, they gain expertise.
- **"Wait a minute, I know this"** aka **Divine Clarity** (Recharge 1d4 days) Gain the ability to recall any detail, fact, or event with perfect accuracy. This blessing also enables them to instantly retrieve any information they’ve previously encountered, including languages and complex lore.
