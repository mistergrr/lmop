# Reaching the Temple

The Battle Master learns of a Temple in [[Endurance Hill]] where warriors are tested by a deity of strength or war — [[Torm]]. This temple exists in a mountain near [[Wyvern Tor]]. Reaching the arena itself is a test, as the path is fraught with challenges that require the Battle Master to think strategically rather than rely solely on brute force.

Once in the temple, the Battle Master must explain to [[Torm]] what he seeks. [[Torm]] says that in order to regain his lost strength, he must pass three trials to prove not just his strength, but his tactician and leadership capabilities as well.

**Inspiration Removal:** All party members begin the Trial of Harmony without inspiration. The battlemaster rolls a **Charisma check (DC 13)** for each ally to inspire them. Each successful roll grants an inspiration token that the battlemaster can distribute during the trials.

# Trial 1: The Trial of Harmony
The group enters a vast chamber filled with interconnected mechanisms—pulsing crystals, shifting gears, and ethereal orbs suspended in the air. The objective is to activate these mechanisms in perfect synchrony to unlock a divine barrier leading to the next trial.

### 1. Mechanism of Focus
A crystal sphere must be kept stable while absorbing chaotic magical energy. The crystal fluctuates unpredictably, making focus difficult.
- **Obstacle:**
	- Ethereal waves radiate from the crystal, causing mental disorientation.
-  **Skill Challenge (DC 13):** to attune and stabilise the crystal.
	- **DC13 Constitution saving throws** to maintain concentration against mental interference.
- **Complication:** If stability is lost, the crystal overloads and releases a blast of arcane force 3 (1d6) force damage.

### 2. Mechanism of Resonance
A glowing, musical crystal must be tuned to the right frequency using harmonic rods.
- **Obstacle:**
	- Discordant frequencies summon four [[Harmonic Wisp]] who are sensitive to sound.
- **Skill Challenge (DC 13):** to correctly tune the crystal.
		- Allies must defend against creatures using Martial Attacks or Spells.
- **Complication:** Incorrect tuning amplifies creature spawn rates or causes feedback damage.

### 3. The Gearwork Puzzle
This mechanical challenge features a wall covered in intricate, rapidly moving small pistons, gears, and shifting cogs. Hidden within the chaotic mechanism are four recessed buttons that must be pressed in the correct order to disable the mechanism and trigger the next mechanism. Small pistons slam in and out rhythmically, while gears spin and grind together. Sparks of arcane energy surge unpredictably from the overloaded system.
- **Obstacle**
	- Players must locate and press the buttons while avoiding mechanical hazards.
	- Coordination and timing are crucial to success.
- **Skill Challenges**
	- **DC 12 check to provide aid:**
		- Gain advantage when rolling the Sleight of Hand check.
	- **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) DC 15:**
		- Press the buttons without getting caught by the snapping pistons.

### 4. Resonance Plates Puzzle
Five metallic resonance plates are arranged in a pentagonal pattern. The plates hum with different pitches, growing louder and more chaotic as players interact. The room vibrates with harmonic energy. 
- **Obstacle**
	- The players must step on the plates in the correct sequence to harmonise the energy and proceed.
- **Skill Challenges (DC 13)**
- **Complication**
	- A misstep causes the resonance to destabilise, resulting in a burst of force energy. The player must make a **DC 12 CON save**  or take 3 (1d6) force damage.

### 5. Obstacle of Balance
Weighted stones must be perfectly balanced across a set of scales. As the challenge progresses, more stones must be added without causing the scales to tip.
- **Obstacle**
	- Shifting platforms increase the difficulty of maintaining balance.
- **Skill Challenges (DC 13)**
	- **Strength (Athletics)** checks to lift and hold heavy stones.
	- **Dexterity (Acrobatics)** checks to maintain footing on unstable ground.
- **Complication**
	- Each failure to balance a stone properly increases the difficulty for future checks and causes the scales to reset partially.

# Trial 2: Trial of the Tactician

### Objective:
Defend a sacred relic while waves of spectral enemies attempt to breach the group’s defences. The battlemaster must use tactical prowess to guide their allies through the encounter.

**Battlefield Setup:**
- The area contains multiple strategic positions: high ground for ranged attacks, choke points for melee defences, and environmental hazards like crumbling terrain or shifting ground.
- Allies can move between these points based on the battlemaster’s directives to gain advantages or mitigate risks.
**Tactical Directives:**
- The battlemaster makes WIS (Insight) or WIS (Perception) checks (DC 13) to identify enemy weaknesses or environmental opportunities.

**Enemies:**
    1. [[Restless Dead]]
    2. [[Kobold Sniper]] 
    3. [[Arboreal Bruiser]]
    4. [[Rift Strider]]

#### Wave 1: Initial Assault
- 2x [[Kobold Sniper]]
- 3x [[Restless Dead]]
#### Wave 2: Reinforcements Arrive
- 2x [[Kobold Sniper]]
- 3x [[Restless Dead]]
- 1x [[Rift Strider]]
#### Wave 3: The Final Push
- 1x [[Arboreal Bruiser]]
- 1x [[Rift Strider]]
- 2x [[Kobold Sniper]]
- 3x [[Restless Dead]]

**Victory and Failure Conditions:**
    - **Victory:** Survive all waves with minimal damage to the relic.
    - **Failure:** If the relic sustains too much damage, it releases chaotic energy that overwhelms the battlemaster, symbolising a failure to learn strategic leadership.
**Skill Challenge:**
- **Wisdom (Insight) DC 13**:  
    The Battlemaster makes a check to identify the weaknesses in the enemies’ approach and create a strategy to exploit them.
    
- **Charisma (Leadership) DC 11**:  
    The Battlemaster inspires their allies, giving them an edge in the battle through well-timed commands. Success grants the **party advantage** on their next set of actions or rolls.

**Relic Health:** If the relic’s health is reduced to 0, the group fails the trial and the battlemaster is unable to receive **The Blessing of Endurance**. It is essential to keep the relic protected at all costs.

# Trial 3: The Trial of Endurance — Shared Weight
The battlemaster must face a [[Champion of Torm]] in a one-on-one fight. Their allies have to hold up a heavy weight to boost the battlemaster's damage.

1. **Weight Mechanics:**
    - Each ally must make a **Strength (Athletics)** or **Constitution check** (**DC 14**) to hold the weight for a round.
    - The number of successful rolls directly influences the battlemaster’s damage boost (+3 for every successful round).
    - Each failed check reduces the battlemaster’s damage by 1 for the next attack.
    - After three failures, the weight drops completely, requiring a team effort (all allies rolling together) to lift it again.
2. **Scaling Difficulty:**
    - Add environmental effects like gusting winds or shifting terrain to increase tension without overwhelming players.
    - Offer short rest-like moments where allies can adjust grip or shift positions, providing opportunities to regroup.
3. **Narrative Boost:**
    - The weight represents both the literal burden of the curse and the unseen emotional toll the battlemaster has endured.
    - Allies' determination to share this weight symbolises loyalty and teamwork, reinforcing the theme of shared strength.
4. **Redemption Moment:**
    - When the battlemaster completes the trial, a radiant glow envelops the group as [[Torm]] recognises both individual and collective efforts. The battlemaster’s curse lifts, symbolising that strength is not just physical but born from the bonds of trust and support.

# The Reward

Upon completing the trial, the deity of strength acknowledges the Battle Master’s endurance and resolve. The curse is lifted, and as a reward, the Battle Master may receive a boon:

- **The Blessing of Endurance**: A permanent increase to their Constitution +1. (Not available if Trial 2 is a failure).
- **Bring Honour to Your Family**: [[Thunderclap]] gains +1
- Level up?
- **Go Team, Go**: Party receives +1 to all melee and attack rolls for 1 day.

This trial not only breaks the curse but also imparts a valuable lesson to the Battle Master, reinforcing that true strength is as much about wisdom, strategy, and endurance as it is about physical power.
