# Mission
Temple of [[Bahamut]] in [[Longsaddle]] currently taken over by [[Cultist]] of [[Ashmadai]], who are using the temple and its monks to summon [[Devils]] with intent to summon [[Asmodeus]] himself.

Cultists conduct rituals to **summon** fiends. Involves extensive bloodletting. Every time the Ashmadai summons a fiend, it must also bind a mortal creature to the essence of a devil. Ashmadai thugs regularly kidnap people in order to make them vessels.

Clear them out so that the place may be reconsecrated. Players don't need to reconsecrate — only clear.

**Bonus**: Find and return [[Black Obsidian Mirror]] — Acts as crystal ball that can scry other planes.
