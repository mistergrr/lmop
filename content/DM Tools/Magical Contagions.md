# Cackle Fever
Cheaply made potions and elixirs are sometimes tainted by cackle fever, which affects humanoids only (gnomes are strangely immune). A creature suffers the following effects 1d4 days after infection:

- **Fever**. Gain 1 exhaustion level (lasts until contagion ends).
- **Uncontrollable laughter**. While suffering exhaustion, DC 13 CON save each time is takes damage other than psychic. Fail: 5 (1d10) psychic damage and incapacitated as they laugh uncontrollably. Save ends on turn end. Lasts 1 minute.
## Fighting the contagion
End of each long rest, DC 13 CON save. Success: immune 1 year.
## Spreading the contagion
Any humanoid that starts its turn within 10ft makes a DC 10 CON save. Success: immune from that particular creature 24 hours.

# Sewer Plague
Fouled potions and alchemical waste can give rise to sewer plague, which incubates in sewers and refuse heaps and is sometimes transmitted by creatures that dwell in such areas, such as otyughs and rats. Any humanoid wounded by a carrier or contact with contaminated filth make a DC 11 CON save. A creature suffers the following effects 1d4 days after infection:

- **Fatigue**. Gain 1 exhaustion level.
- **Weakness**. While exhausted, regains only half HP from expending hit dice.
- **Restlessness**. While exhausted, finishing a long rest neither restores HP nor reduces the exhaustion level.
## Fighting the contagion
Daily at dawn, DC 11 CON save. Fail: gain 1 exhaustion level. Success: exhaustion decreases by 1. On exhaustion 0, the contagion ends.

# Sight Rot
Any beast or humanoid that drinks water tainted with sight rot makes a DC 15 CON save or have the blinded condition until the contagion ends.
## Fighting the contagion
Magic such as *heal* or *lesser restoration* ends it immediately. A character proficient with a *herbalism kit* can create one dose of non-magical ointment, which takes 1 hour. When applied to the eyes of the creature suffering, the contagion is suppressed for 24 hours. If the contagion is suppressed for 72 hours, (3 doses and applications) the contagion ends.
## Spreading the contagion
Any humanoid that makes skin contact makes a DC 15 CON save. Success: immune from that particular creature 24 hours.
