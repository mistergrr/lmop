## Placing Treasure
Take total site loot determined and divide into halves. Put half into one or two major troves in appropriate locations. Divide the other half among the other treasure-positive rooms, putting trifling finds in some and bigger hauls in others. Play with proportions per judgement. Make sure not all treasure requires a fight.

## State of Ruin
1. **Perilous**. Are is dangerously worn and prone to collapse. Any impacts or damage to the structure, including from spells and other AoE, have 50% chance of causing a collapse.
2. **Crumbling**. Areas are choked with rubble and have 50% chance of being difficult terrain. Half cover and hiding places plentiful.
3. **Neglected**. One dungeon hazard—such as brown mould, green slime, or yellow mould— is abundant.
4. **Abandoned**. Mostly deserted. DEX (Stealth) checks have disadv. because any sounds stand out as unusual.
5. **Secure**. Ability checks to break doors, open locks, or similar have disadv.
6. **Thriving**. Heavily populated. Any loud noises draw the attention of nearby creatures
