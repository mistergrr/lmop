
1. Each journey – determine the destination, direction, distance, speed, and terrain.
2. Each journey – have players select roles – pathfinder, scout, or quartermaster.
3. Each journey or change in terrain – determine the overall danger level.
4. Each day – determine weather.
5. Each hex – determine the risk of getting lost.
6. Each hex – choose or roll for a monument if desired.
7. Each dangerous period – roll for or select a random encounter, signs of previous activity, or signs of activity yet to come.
8. Each day – expend resources.
# Roles
- **Pathfinder**: *INT (History) or WIS (Nature, Survival)*. 
- **Scout**: *WIS (Perception, Investigation)*. Finds threats or recent activity/signs of danger.
- **Quartermaster**: *WIS (Nature, Survival)*. Provisions & foraging. Makes sure everyone's socks are clean.
# Weather
Determine once or twice per day. 1d6 — 1 is poor visibility, 6 is clear.
# Risk of getting lost.
Determine each hex. Traveling along a road or well-known path is an automatic success but can become dangerous if hostile creatures control the road.
# Danger & Encounters
Each hex, roll 1d6. More danger, higher odds (1-3 on 1d6 in dangerous territory).
## 1d6 Rolls
Higher rolls more favourable.
- Distance
- Awareness
- Attitude
# Expend Resources
Track daily. How many rations to reduce exhaustion?
