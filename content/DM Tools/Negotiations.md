- If the party is FAMOUS, with a fantastic reputation, start with +1 Patience
- If ONE party member speaks the NPC's native language, start with +1 Patience
- If THREE party members speaks the NPC's native language, start with +2 Patience
# Negotiation Rolls
- <11 = Failure
- 12+ = Partial failure or success
- 17+ Success
## Getting a read on them
Make an insight check
- **Failure**: NPC gets annoyed. -1 Patience
- **DC12 Partial Failure**: Hard to read.
- **DC17 Success**: Party learns one motivation or pitfall (player's choice).

## Appealing to a motivation
Each motivation can be spoken to only once.
- **Failure**: -1 Patience
- **DC12 Partial Failure**: +1 Interest, -1 Patience
- **DC17 Success**: +1 Interest
## Appealing to a pitfall
Auto-fail. -1 Interest & Patience.
## Appealing to neither
- **Failure**: -1 Interest & Patience
- **DC12 Partial Failure**: -1 Patience
- **DC17 Success**: +1 Interest, -1 Patience
## Deception checks
If a lie fails to increase NPC interest, you're caught out in the lie. -1 Interest.

# Motivations/Pitfalls
## Benevolence
**Motivation** — Believes in sharing what they have with others (limited capacity).
**Pitfall** — Cynical views, believing no creature has a right to anything just by being alive.
## Discovery
**Motivation** — Wants to learn new lore, explore, experiment, or uncover lost artifacts.
**Pitfall** — No interest in finding new places, peoples, or ideas. Unknown scares them or they'd rather remain ignorant.
## Freedom
**Motivation** — No authority above them and desires no authority over others.
**Pitfall** — Believes a world without authority is one in turmoil and chaos.
## Greed
**Motivation** — Desires wealth and resources above all else.
**Pitfall** — No interest in accumulating wealth or other resources. Offended if anyone tries to buy them.
## Higher Authority
**Motivation** — Staunchly loyal to a person or force they perceive as more important than themself.
**Pitfall** — Scoffs at the idea of serving another.
## Justice
**Motivation** — Wants to see the righteous rewarded and the wicked punished (according to their views).
**Pitfall** — Doesn't believe the world is a just place, and has no interest making it one.
## Legacy
**Motivation** — Fame while alive and acclaim after their death. Might even seek immortality.
**Pitfall** — Cares nothing about leaving a personal mark on the world. Vain waste of time.
## Peace
**Motivation** — Calm. Want to be left alone.
**Pitfall** — Hates boredom. Wants excitement, drama, and danger. Nothing worse than status quo.
## Power
**Motivation** — Covets the authority of others. Want to increase influence.
**Pitfall** — No interest in authority for themself.
## Protection
**Motivation** — Has land, people, information, items, or an organization they want protected above all else.
**Pitfall** — Happy to leave others to fend for themselves. Not their responsibility.
## Revelry
**Motivation** — Girls just want to have fun.
**Pitfall** — Sees social encounters and hedonism as a waste of time. Takes pleasure only in work or in building their own skills and character.
## Vengeance
**Motivation** — Revenge.
**Pitfall** — Revenge solves nothing.
