# Spell Scroll Creation Process
## Ingredients
Creating a scroll requires the following ingredients:
1. **Parchment**. Regular or magical (the latter being more stable but more expensive)
2. **Ink**. Standard (for spells up to level 3) or alchemical (4+)
3. Requisite **Items**. Unique to the spell (e.g. phoenix feather for fire spells)

## Creation Check
To create a scroll, the player must make an Intelligence (Arcana) check. The following modifiers may apply:
### **Positive Mods**
- +PB if proficient in crafting or alchemy
- +2 if using magical calligraphy tools
- +2 if double time spent
### **Negative Mods**
- -2 if exhausted or wounded
- -2 if materials are poor quality

## Check Results
Result | Outcome
-- | --
< DC - 5 | Failure — Scroll destroyed and materials are lost
DC - 4 to -1 | Partial Success — Scroll is unstable. Roll side effect (or DM decide)
DC to DC + 4 | Success — Scroll works as intended
DC + 5 or higher | Masterpiece — Scroll has additional feature (DM decide)

## Unstable Results

1d6 | Effect
-- | --
1 | Spell lasts only 1 minute
2 | Requires a DC 12 Arcana check to cast
3 | Functions, but effects are random (e.g. harm instead of heal)
4 | Spell has double the effect, but user gains a level of exhaustion
5 | Spell is cursed — after casting, DM chooses effect (e.g. silence)
6 | Roll twice

## Training
- After 5 successful scrolls, +1 to checks to create.
- After 10, can add +3 when doubling time spent.
- After 20, can create 2 scrolls simultaneously.
