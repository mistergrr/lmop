### Tentacles
The creature gains one tentacle. It gains one Free Attack Action per tentacle. Each tentacle’s attack does STR mod. damage. If it causes Damage, it can also give its opponent an Entangled Condition, which will initiate a Grapple between the target and that tentacle. The tentacle cannot attack while Grappling.

### Animosity
When subject to Animosity , you must immediately attack the disagreeable creatures, either socially (insults, jibes, and similar) or physically (most commonly with fists), as appropriate. You also gain a bonus of +1 on any attempts to socially or mentally attack the group. Animosity is over-ridden by Fear and Terror.
