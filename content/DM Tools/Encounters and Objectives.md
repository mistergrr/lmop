# Encounter Calculator
This party of 5 level 5 adventurers has **4** encounter points.  
  
- An **easy** matchup has a total CR of **4** and costs **½** an encounter point.
- A **medium** matchup has a total CR of **8** and costs **1** encounter point.
- A **hard** matchup has a total CR of **12** and costs **2** encounter points.
- A **deadly** matchup has a total CR of **16** and costs **4** encounter points.
- An **impossible** matchup has a total CR of **25**.
  
IMPORTANT! The **maximum CR** of any single monster should be **7**.  
  
Remember that ELITE monsters count as TWO monsters of that type.
