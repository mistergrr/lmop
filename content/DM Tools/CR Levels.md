# Tier 1 (CR 0-4)
- **CR 1/8**: Avg. town guard/bandit, [[goblin]] grunt
- **CR 1/4**: Needs 2 guards; skeleton/zombie, wolf
- **CR 1/2**: Dont 1v1; crocodile, [[hobgoblin]], [[gnoll]], [[orc]]
- **CR 1**: Party of guards; apex land predator, pirate, spectre/ghoul, brass/copper wyrmling
- **CR 2**: 1-hit kill guards; [[ogre]], [[griffon]], allosaurus
- **CR 3**: Can 1v1 most monsters; green hag, guard captain, [[owlbear]], [[yeti]], [[Werewolf]], wight, orca, minotaur
- **CR 4**: Local BBEG; Knight (swapped with capt.), [[banshee]], gnoll fang of yeenoghu, bull elephant, red dragon wyrmling
