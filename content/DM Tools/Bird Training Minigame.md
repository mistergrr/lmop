**Objective:**  
Train a wild bird to reliably carry letters. Success varies from fleeting contact to a bonded messenger.

---

## **Step 0 — Bird Determination**

**Roll 1d6 (no modifiers):**

|d6|Bird|Base DC|
|---|---|---|
|1|Sparrow|10|
|2|Pigeon|11|
|3|Seagull|12|
|4|Crow|13|
|5|Raven|14|
|6|Barn Owl|15|

> This sets the Base DC for all stages.

---

## **Stage 1 — First Contact (Animal Handling)**

**Skill:** Wisdom (Animal Handling)  
**Goal:** Approach calmly, establish tolerance.

|Approach|DC Modifier|Success|Failure|
|---|---|---|---|
|Cautious|+0|+1|0|
|Deliberate|+2|+2|–1|
|Bold|+4|+3|–2|

> **DC = Base DC + Approach Modifier**

Describe your approach in-game: posture, offering, soft movements.

---

## **Stage 2 — Reading the Bird (Nature)**

**Skill:** Intelligence (Nature)  
**Goal:** Understand species behaviour, signals, and timing.

|Knowledge Approach|DC Modifier|Success|Failure|
|---|---|---|---|
|Common Knowledge|+0|+1|0|
|Studied Behaviour|+2|+2|–1|
|Expert Insight|+4|+3|–2|

> **DC = Base DC + Knowledge Modifier**

Narrative flavour: watch posture, eye movement, feeding habits.

---

## **Stage 3 — Feeding & Trust (Animal Handling)**

**Skill:** Wisdom (Animal Handling)  
**Goal:** Offer food, allow approach, test trust.

|Feeding Method|DC Modifier|Success|Failure|
|---|---|---|---|
|Tossed Offering|+0|+1|0|

---

## **Stage 4 — Return Training (Animal Handling)**

**Skill:** Wisdom (Animal Handling)  
**Goal:** Condition the bird to return reliably to a location.

|Training Method|DC Modifier|Success|Failure|
|---|---|---|---|
|Passive Conditioning|+0|+1|0|
|Reinforced Training|+2|+2|–1|
|Imprinted Behaviour|+4|+3|–2|

> **DC = Base DC + Training Modifier**

Narrative flavour: repeated release and recall, consistent feeding.

---

## **Total Modifier**

- Each stage grants **+1 to +3** on success or **0 to –2** on failure.
- **Range:** –8 (worst case) → +12 (perfect run).
- Running total is applied to the **Final Outcome Roll**.

---

## **Final Outcome Roll (Revised Distances)**

**Roll:** `1d20 + total modifier`

|Final Total|Outcome|Messenger Capability|
|---|---|---|
|9 or lower|Fleeting Contact|Bird sometimes follows but does not carry letters reliably|
|10–14|Limited Training|Carries **a single letter, once**, up to **8 miles / ~13 km**|
|15–19|Messenger|Reliable delivery, up to **24 miles / ~38 km**|
|20-29|Bonded Messenger|Reliable + enhanced, up to **48 miles / ~77 km**, slightly faster or heavier payload possible|
|30+| Bonded Messenger+|Brings a trinket once a week.
