# 3-mile hexes
#hexcrawl 

| Type                                 | Time     | Per day  |
| ------------------------------------ | -------- | -------- |
| Roads                                | .75 hour | 10 hexes |
| Plains/Grasslands/Hills/light forest | 1 hour   | 8 hexes  |
| Bog/Deep Forest                      | 2 hours  | 4 hexes  |
| Mountains                            | 4 hours  | 2 hexes  |

Each time the party enters a new hex:
> “Who’s leading the way today? Roll me a **Survival check** to stay on course through the forest.”
- **DC 12**: clear weather
- **DC 14–15**: bad visibility (rain, blight, fog)
- On failure: the party moves into a random adjacent hex

# Each Day
1. Choose direction of travel
2. Determine if the Party is lost
3. Roll 3 times on Encounters and Events
4. Track supplies
## Encounters and Events
Roll 1d8 3x per day (morning, noon, night)

1-2 Monster - these can either be lairs (1-2) or wandering (3-4) or footprints (5-6)
3 Trap /Hazard- I use this for actual traps (set by monsters), difficult terrain such as a river crossing, cliff face, etc., or clues such as trails, totems, footprints, etc.
4 Special - Keyed location for that Hex - or use “Totally Random”
5 Signs of destruction Table III
6 Weather Event - Table
7 Location Table I
8 Pilgrims Table II
### Weather
2 Freak Event (Damage if not under cover) ex. Hail Stones
3-5 Unusual (Ruins Gear) ex. Acid Rain
6-8 Inconvenient (Slow Movement) ex. Winds/Rain
9-11 Atmospheric (Effects abilities) ex. Poison Fog (Drains CON)
12 Extreme (Damage or Destroy Gear) ex. Tornado
### Trap / Hazard
2 Trap (roll Monster type to determine)
3-5 Off Course (Must go around) ex. Raging river
6-8 Difficult Terrain (Slows Movement) ex. Heavy growth/Marsh
9-11 Impassable “dead end” (Must back-track) ex. steep cliffs
12 Caught in natural disaster (damage/lost) ex. Flash flood

**Example Cult**
The Rune Blood cult - The templars of scar and ink
This group of 4 men (MU1)and 1 woman (MU3) are naked save for a loin cloth. Their bodies are covered in tattoos and each has 1d3 magic runes as tattoos (one per spell know)
they cast spells by using a sharp flint to cut the shape of the tattoo (thus drawing blood) rune - they are magic users and can cast freely once each, after the first spell they must make an INT check and save to avoid catastrophe (as spell craft rules)- all the men have 4 HP each (lose 1 per casting) the woman has 12 HP and saves as a MU3. Using the magic system “Wonder & Wickedness”
