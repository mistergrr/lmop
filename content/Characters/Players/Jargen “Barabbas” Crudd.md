# Background
- Born in the **slums** of [[Mintarn]]
- **Clanless** runt left to fend for himself.
- Raised in the [[Saltstone Orphanage]]
- **Pescetarian** — he only eats fish and produce from the ground (bad experience with mystery meat growing up).
- Formed a **criminal crew**, [[Crimson Hands]]. Smuggling, burglary, and the occasional strongarm work.
- Were chasing a tip of a shipment passing through [[Triboar Trail]]. [[Redbrands]] were there and **slaughtered** the [[Crimson Hands]].
- Jargen woke up in a shallow grave, surrounded by his dead comrades. A **bloodstone** replaced his right eye.
- A story from his early childhood was brought to mind: the Barabbas Parable (one from his upbringing in Saltstone orphanage); where a man named Barabbas, a convict on death row, is pardoned from the bloodlust of the townsfolk in exchange for someone else to take his execution. This exchange of destiny was a bedtime story told to children in efforts to bring correction for their misconduct (be ye the townsfolk who hold life and death in your hand than be the felon staring at the sunset on the gallows). Jargen sees this story as a twist of fate for himself — that he was once dead but now finds himself alive with a second chance at living, hence why he refers to himself simply as “Barabbas”.
	- **Sacrificial exchange**. Someone else was sacrificed to bring him back — [[Iarno Albrek|Iarno]] practicing.
# Goals
- Investigate the bigger picture: Were the [[Crimson Hands]] targeted? Was he the target? Or was he just caught in the crossfire? Why were the [[Redbrands]] involved? Some of those answers might lie in [[Tresendar Manor History]], or with other citizens in [[Phandalin|Phandalin]] that have ties to the criminal underworld throughout the Sword Coast. 
- Assist the Heroes in their adventures: Barabbas feels indebted to the heroes for enacting vengeance on his attackers, and wishes to journey with the heroes to assist them by any means necessary. Now with a second chance at life, Barabbas feels like there’s a lot of wrongs he needs to right, and this band of heroes might be his way of doing so.

# Flaws
- Doesn’t trust fate, prophecy or the divine. If he was brought back for a reason, he surely would’ve been told by now. From the disappointment of a lack of revelation, and from his upbringing in the slums where his prayers were never answered, Barabbas keeps himself guarded when talking about all things spiritual and divine.
- Will always start a fight over religion. He gets tense when people make testimonies of “miracles” and “divine favour” they’ve experienced, and will often counter with “if your deity is so high & mighty, why would they let others struggle”.

# “Get to Know You Homework”
- **First Adventure (gone wrong)**: My First outing as an adventurer (mercenary): Barabbas’s crew did the simple job of a hit & run for a high-value noble that was looking to kill his dangerous business partner. The business partner was also in league with some officials connected to the local authorities, which led to the Crimson Hands burning down the local law enforcement headquarters and shifting the blame to a rival gang. That gang was later tried and hung for destruction of property. 
- **Top 3 Bards & Bands**: 1. Switch-Half-Foot, 2. My Alchemical Allure, 3. Tavern Basement
- **Role of Family**. Barabbas’s family have long gone. His mother abandoned him in the gutters of a [[Mintarn]] alleyway, his father he never knew. But his adopted family, formed him and shaped him to even now, 40+ years later, where he’s able to remember those life lessons and put into practise the lessons from his rebellious and rage-filled youth. Barabbas still gives some of his earned rewards to the orphanage to this day under his old name, to which his adopted mother Vensha only hopes that the said gold is from good, well-earned labour. 
- **Favourite form of communication**: Messenger rat. 
- **Views on Orcs**: Don’t headbutt an orc, they’ll headbutt you back twice as hard. They’re good folk if you treat them well, otherwise stay clear if they’re in a rage.

Mother Vensha: Older human woman in her late 70’s.

Role: Matron of Saltstone Orphanage

Personality: Stern but fiercely protective. Not the type to “coddle” but will stick up for her kids no matter what.

Backstory: Grew up in Mintarn, and inherited Saltstone Orphanage after both parents passed away in a shipwreck. Formerly widowed; lost her first husband to invading pirates, found running Saltstone as a way to cope with her grief. 

Connection to Barabbas: She always considered Barabbas to be the silent, sharp-witted and observant type, will still call him by his birth name, and hopes that these newly habited financial donations are coming from a legal income. 

  

Dovik Greymurk: Older dwarven male, late 50’s, blind in both eyes.

Role: Cook, Handyman (reluctant storyteller). 

Personality: Gruff, sarcastic, dry-witted, soft-hearted (which he pretends to not have). 

Backstory: Former sellsword who lost his sight in a bar-fight, Dovik took refuge at Saltstone after pirates swept through Mintarn; comforted the widow Vensha. Dovik teaches the children to work with their hands - carpentry, mending fishnets, plastering walls, and passes on street-smarts to those he can recognise will take hold of those special skills. 

Connection to Barabbas: He knew Jargen had the stoic nature of a king or a killer. He trained Jargen on his crossbow skills, he’d say he’s proud of Jargen but his ego would never allow it. He still keeps Jargen’s old tools down in the basement.**
