## Hobgoblin Captain
After the party killed [[King Grol]], Targor filled the gap as leader of the [[Cragmaws]]. It's a position he was eying for years, but was never able to dethrone [[King Grol]]. Now he has his position, he offers the party thanks and gives them a chance to leave without a fight.

Has two wolves with him.
# Stats
**AC**: 17
**HP**: 58
**Initiative**: 14

**Aura of Authority**: All allies in a 10ft emanation have adv. on attacks and saves.

# Actions
- **Multiattack**. Two melee attacks
- **Greatsword**. +4; 9 (2d6 + 2) Slashing  plus 3 (1d6) Poison.
- **Longbow**. +4;  150/600 ft; 6 (1d8 + 2) Piercing  plus 5 (2d4) Poison.

# Bonus Action
1. *GET IN HERE!*. Summons 3 goblin minions on round 1. 1 per round thereafter.

# Reactions
1. Get up, worm. DC 12 CHA check when a goblin goes down. 1HP on success.

# Villain Action (1/round on a player's turn) 
1. *WHAT ARE YOU WAITING FOR*?: Every goblin gets either a free movement or attack (does not provoke)
2. *FOCUS FIRE*: All goblins move to a selected enemy (does not provoke)
3. *KILL*: All goblins get  1 or 2 free scimitar attacks

# Battlefield Action
## Cleave:
### Tell
The boss raises its weapon, readying a devastating attack against anyone in front of it. Whenever a creature deals damage to the boss, the boss faces in the direction of the creature that attacked it, changing the area affected by their Cleave.
### Resolution
The boss Cleaves a 15-foot Cone area in the direction it's facing. Every creature in that area must make a DEX saving throw, taking 12 damage on a failure and half on a success.
