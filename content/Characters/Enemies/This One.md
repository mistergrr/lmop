A Nothic living in the cave beneath [[Tresendar Manor History]]. It uses the crevasse filled with necromantic aura that preserves meat to stash its treasure. The treasure includes the longsword, [[Talon]], a [[Scroll of Augury]], two healing potions, and some coins.

He used to work for [[Iarno Albrek]] in exchange for treasure and the gift of meat, but the party have taken that role.

It gives the party information in exchange for meat.
