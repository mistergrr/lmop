# Backstory:
Born into **House Zauvirr**, traded as a diplomatic consort, eventually discarded. Banished from [[Omphalos]] when his spellcraft threatened tradition (this is a lie—all drow are natural spellcasters). Nezznar is part of a sect of drow in [[Omphalos]] who secretly worship [[Vhaeraun]], rejecting [[Lolth]]’s matriarchy. 
# Main desire/fear. How it affects their behaviour
- Craves **freedom** from subjugation—freedom from [[Lolth]], the hierarchy of matriarchal rule, being just another dark elf among thousands. This craving is shackled to his **fear of insignificance**. Seeks power to validate his existence in a society that sees him as expendable.
- Sees [[Omphalos]] as a city that could rise outside [[Lolth]]’s web — Far enough away from [[Menzoberranzan]] and its politics.
- Believes the Forge’s magic is the ultimate defiance of [[Lolth]]’s design. A machine that creates relics **without gods**.
- Goal: **a free Omphalos**, a city of shadow and steel that answers to no god. Method: Create a magic relic to raise a protective mantle around the city, cutting off [[Lolth]]’s divination from reaching it.
# Secrets
- He built a secret cell of followers or [[Vhaeraun]] among artisans, slaves, and lower-born males in [[Omphalos]]. When he was discovered, his mother made an example of him: his cell was burned, his followers crucified in webs, and he was stripped of House and name.
- Nezznar drew [[Lolth|Lolth’s]] eye when he was banished from [[Omphalos]]. He’s haunted by the image of becoming a drider.
- His mask partially hides him from [[Lolth]]'s gaze, but he cannot use much magic until he imbues it with more power from the [[Forge of Spells]].
- Was assembling [[Many-Arrows Orcs]], [[Cragmaws]] and [[Redbrands]] along with his [[The Black Spiders]] to take [[Wave Echo Cave]], but the loss of most of those factions due to the party have left him short on forces.
# Contrasting/conflicting/unexpected aspect?
- To protect his vision, Nezznar must control it. He fears that without a strong hand, [[Omphalos]] would collapse into chaos — the very chaos he fled. So he becomes _exactly what he despises_: authoritarian, manipulative, obsessed with control.
- He believes his tyranny is temporary — “until freedom learns to stand.” But the truth is, it's all he knows.

# Driderhood
> The air grows heavy with a sour, metallic taste, as if the cavern itself resents what is about to happen. Nezznar staggers, clutching his chest, his dark eyes burning with fury and desperation. He screams into the shadows, _"Vhaeraun! Father of Shadows—give me strength! I will not kneel to her!"_
> For a heartbeat, there is silence. Then the sound of skittering, thousands of legs scraping stone, fills the chamber. Threads of glistening web descend from nowhere, weaving themselves around Nezznar, binding his limbs.
> A woman’s laughter—cold, layered with venom—echoes in every mind present. _"You are mine, little spider. No shadow god can take you from me."_
> Nezznar thrashes, tearing against the cocooning strands. His mask falls to the floor, his face a mask of defiance.  _"I reject you, Demon Queen! I follow the Masked Lord!"_
> The webbing tightens, searing into his skin like molten iron. His cries turn from anger to agony as his body warps—legs bending backward, arms fusing with growing chitin. The transformation is horrifyingly slow, deliberate, each snap of bone and rip of flesh punctuated by [[Lolth]]’s mocking whispers.
> When the cocoon finally splits, a half-drow, half-spider monstrosity crawls forth. Nezznar’s once-fine features are now grotesque, his lower body a swollen, chittering arachnid form. His eyes—eight of them now—burn with rage and shame.
> Lolth’s voice slithers one last time into the chamber, _"You sought freedom in shadows, Nezznar. But shadows are mine. Crawl now, beast, as proof that none escape my web."_
> Nezznar lets out a guttural roar—part fury, part despair—before skittering into the darkness, his defiance drowned in the echo of his curse.
