**Age**: 1120
**Voice**: Whispery
**Description**: 
Was an elf before becoming a banshee. Might have been beautiful once, but a hateful expression now twists her features. Loots the Dessarin, trying to rebuild her riches.

Viewed the people of [[Conyberry]] as allies. Often used her spells to bring them beasts for food in the worst winter weather. Also slaughtered orcs and brigands who ventured too near. Folk in Conyberry used to regard Agatha almost affectionately as their guardian and friend. Unfortunately couldn't hold of the orc horde that decimated the area.
