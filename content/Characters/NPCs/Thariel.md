# Backstory:
Thariel was a powerful mage who worked for Saint Nikolais. They were a part of "His Royal Extension of Good Tidings" until they met their end in the spell battle at the climax of the orc attack. Centuries of anger have poisoned their soul, transforming them into a hate-filled apparition.

Thariel leads the undead that haunt [[Wave Echo Cave]]. The wraith spends their time here because a special treasure they were given in life is in the scorched chest. No longer corporeal, they cannot touch or possess the wealth they enjoyed in life.
# Combat
“Dance of the Peacebringer” (+2AC against ranged attacks, light aura extending 10ft, allies in aura gain adv. on saving throws against intimidation & fear based effects. Additionally, once per long rest, she can use a “Radiant Flow” while attacking. When she strikes a foe, they must succeed a DEX saving throw (the DC is her spell save DC) or take 3d8 radiant dmg, as Saint Nikolais’ power manifests in her blade.
# Main desire/fear. How it affects their behaviour
**Common Desires:**
Collecting Magical treasures such as (particularly scrolls), spellbooks, and arcane knowledge. 

**Common Fears**
Losing the crystal given by Saint Niklaus. Will attack anyone who touches it.
