# Goals
- **Survive at Any Cost** – He’ll betray, lie, or bribe anyone to stay alive.
- **Avoid Open Conflict** – He doesn’t want to pick a side—he just wants to not be crushed.
- **Find a Way Out** – If Glasstaff’s control weakens, he might try to flee, cut a deal with someone stronger, or shift blame onto others.
- **Stay in Power (Barely)** – He doesn’t crave power, but he knows losing his position could mean losing his safety.

# Harbin's Secrets
- Harbin has been skimming off the top of the town's funds for months.
- He was planted as townmaster by [[Iarno Albrek|Glasstaff]]. He's terrified that [[Iarno Albrek|Glasstaff]] and the [[Redbrands]] will kill him because the party dismantled their whole operation in town.
- Harbin knows [[Iarno Albrek|Glasstaff's]] true identity, but is afraid of the consequences for revealing it.
- [[Iarno Albrek]] originally approached Harbin as an aide from [[The Lord's Alliance]]. [[Iarno Albrek|Iarno]] provided funds to the town, including leasing [[The Wee Inn]] to stimulate the local economy.
- [[Iarno Albrek|Iarno]] played the long game, supporting Harbin in his campaign for townmaster. **Daran Edermath** seemed like the favourite to be elected next, so [[Iarno Albrek|Iarno]] offered to rig the election in return for his favour. Harbin declined, but [[Iarno Albrek|Iarno]] blackmailed him, saying he knew about the embezzlement and would report Harbin to [[The Lord's Alliance]].
