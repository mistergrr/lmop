For generations, the Harpells have brought [[Longsaddle]] an importance in the North far greater than its size and purpose would normally warrant.

Have always been mages. This began with **Authrar Harpell**, who was famous an age ago for single-handedly destroying an onrushing orc horde.

**Malchor Harpell**, along with his **Company of Crazed Venturers**, once fought off a demigod. Fighting alone, he destroyed two Red Wizards of Thay and their undead beholders. These days, keeps to the peace of his own [[Tower of Twilight]] and is rarely seen.

Current [[Longsaddle]] village elder is **Ardanac Harpell**, son of Adanac. Only Harpell bothered with the headaches of local politics. By no means the most powerful or eldest in the Ivy Mansion. By tradition, the oldest Harpell women control the house.

Kind-hearted clan, but their magic is capricious. [[Uthgardt]] barbarians never dare raid Longsaddle, though defiant bands sometimes steal cattle and horses from the outlying ranches.

**Haversham Harpell** was overwhelmed and subdued by [[Ashmadai]] cultists when he investigated a disturbance at the [[Temple of Bahamut]]
