# Defining Traits & Skills
- **Exploit Opening (3/day).** Gain adv. on attacks. Life from Death (RA). When a creature within 30ft fails a death save or dies, heal another conscious creature within 30ft 14 (4d6) HP.
- **Oblation of Blood! (BA)** If undamaged, self-attack 2 (1d4) dmg. Adjacent enemies DC 15 WIS save or disadv. next attack.
- **Blood-Soaked Resolve**. While bloodied, adv. on saves.
