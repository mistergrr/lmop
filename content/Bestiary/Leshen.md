# Description
> We never hunt in these woods. Never. Even if it means the whole village starves. – Mulliver, ealdorman of Hoshberg in lower Aedirn

At the **heart of the forest** lies a secret. In a place born of darkness and primeval nature, resides a mighty and terrifying guardian. **Immune** to human steel, it is believed the leshen is nature's way of protecting the forest and the animals that live within it from the threat humans started to pose upon their ravaging expansion deeper into the lands.

In some areas, these creatures form the centre of local **cults**. Peasants make **offerings** to them, usually hares and birds, and sometimes human blood. This has given rise to many **legends** about helpful leshens chasing game towards hunters, helping lost travellers find their way in the forest or saving them from bandits. All false. Leshens see men only as sources of energy on which to feed. They will **drain the life force** of those who live nearby until only limpid husks remain.

They can **heal** other woodland animals and summon nekkers or crows to protect the forest. Its attacks are **slow, but deadly** — be cautious not to get **shackled** by its underground roots.

Leshens old enough to earn the appellation "_ancient_" wield advanced skills and tactics that make them particularly dangerous. Some create magic **totems** that increase their strength – in such cases these totems should be destroyed before or while fighting them. Furthermore, some leshens can create for themselves a kind of **immortality** by wounding a lost traveler with its claws and casting a black magic **curse** on them. For as long as the individual marked in this way stays near its territory, the leshen will be **reborn** every time it dies. Such leshens can only be defeated by driving them away or killing the person they have marked.

# Lore
A character knows the following information with a successful **Nature** check.
- **DC 10**: Will drain the life force of those who live nearby until only limpid husks remain.
- **DC 15**: Immune to steel.
- **DC 20**: Vulnerable to fire.

# Tactics
Leshens use not only their long claws but also their many allies: wolves or raven swarms. Leshens are also able to bend plant life to their will and make it aid them in battle. They dig their rootlike arms into the ground, commanding roots to strike — [[Battlefield Actions#Eruption]]

They also have the ability to _misty step_ unless burning.

Can heal their animal companions.
