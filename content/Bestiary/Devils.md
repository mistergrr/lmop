# Devils Defining Traits & Skills
## All Devils
- **Immune**: Fire
- **Magic Resistance**. Adv. saves vs. spells & magic effects.
- **Infernal Ichor**. On 0HP, explode; 5ft; PBd6 fire.
- **Diabolical Restoration**. If the devil dies outside the Nine Hells, its body disappears in sulphurous smoke, and it gains a new body instantly, reviving with all its Hit Points somewhere in the Nine Hells.
## Greater Devils & Archdevil Traits & Skills
- **Empowered by Death**. Creature within 30ft dies; regain CR × 2 HP.
- Relish Your Failure (Trait). Creature within 50ft fails a save; Gain 1/2 CR THP. If already have THP, instead regain HP, up to their HP max.

# Imp
Spies & emissaries for more powerful devils. Mortals often make bargains, thinking the weak devils are easy to control. Ultimately prove their loyalties lie with the Lords of the Nine. Devious and deadly mischief-makers. Take pleasure in tricking mortals into harming one another.
## Tactics
Reluctant combatants. When forced to fight, turns invisible and waits for an opponent to become distracted before attacking with its tail sting. Doesn’t hesitate to flee.
## Lore
**Religion** check.
**DC 10**: Partner with mortals who seek magical power. By helping their “masters” attain new spells or locate magic items, they foster a madness for power.
**DC 15**: Have the abilities of shape shifting and going invisible.
**DC 20**: Possess impressive knowledge about magical subjects. Gain most of their information from other devils, past experience, or spying efforts.
## Salvage
Wings and bones can be used to create a [[Potion of Fire Resistance]] with a DC 15 INT (Arcana) check from a character proficient in Alchemist's Supplies. Takes 2 hrs and 10 GP of materials. 10% chance that the person taking the potion will have Vulnerability to all other damage.

# Spined Devil (Spinagon)
Scouts & sentries. Gather in raucous, marauding bands. Some serve as spies and messengers.
## Tactics
Disdain melee and prefer to pelt victims with burning, poisoned spines. After victim is bloodied, descend to tear them apart with their wicked claws.
## Lore
**Religion** check.
**DC 10**: (AKA spinagons) Commonly summoned because they are relatively weak and easy to control with promises of mayhem and plunder.
**DC 15**: Enjoy tormenting anything that crosses their path. Aerial scouts and skirmishers, eagerly harrying any foes they encounter with volleys of burning, poisonous spines.
**DC 20**: Disdain melee and prefer to pelt victims with burning, poisoned spines. After victim is bloodied, descend to tear them apart with their wicked claws.

# Merregon
Souls of fallen soldiers, mercenaries, and bodyguards who served evil without reservation often find everlasting servitude in the Nine Hells as merregons. These faceless foot soldiers are the Hells' legionnaires, tasked with protecting their infernal plane and its rulers against intruders.

Merregons have no individuality and hence no need for faces. Every merregon legionnaire has a metal mask bolted to its head. Markings on the mask indicate the only elements of the wearer's identity that matter: the commander it serves and the layer of the Nine Hells it protects.

Because of their unshakable loyalty, merregons form the backbone of many devils' protective retinues. They shrink from no task, no matter how dangerous. Unless ordered to fall back, they never retreat from a fight.

## Tactics
Straightforward melee combatant.

**DC 15**: Have no individuality and hence no need for faces. Wear metal mask with marking to indicate master and layer of hell.


# Bearded Devil (Barbazu)
Fierce warriors used as guards and assault troops. Violent, wrathful, relish battle. Murderous, lust for annihilation. Serve as the foot soldiers of Hell’s armies, often leading hordes of lesser devils such as imps and lemures into battle.
## Tactics
Straightforward melee combatant trained to protect adjacent allies using its devilish teamwork.
## Lore
**Religion** check.
**DC 10**: (AKA barbazus). Among the weakest devils. Savage and dangerous fighters.
**DC 15**: Followers, not leaders. Usually serve as bodyguards or assault troops. Rarely seek to tempt or lure mortals, preferring straightforward slaughter and terror instead.
**DC 20**: Wield serrated glaives to inflict jagged gashes, resulting in tremendous blood loss. When enemies come too close, they strike with the spines of their wriggling beards to deliver a wretched contagion called Avernal fever, savouring the sight of their victim’s strength being slowly devoured from within.

# Nergaliid
Subsists on the life force of unsuspecting humanoids. Creatures that die to the nergaliid's feeding become a [[Undead#Rotfiends & Devourers|Rotfiend]].

Favours feeding on life force of sleeping humanoids, feasting on the same prey nightly—so long as its victim remains unaware. Ensures lasting source of nourishment. Victim beset by terrible nightmares as long as the feeding continues. When discovered, it tries to strike a deal. It might offer material wealth in exchange for freedom and silence. Only attack as last resort.
## Tactics
Only attack as last resort.
## Lore
**Religion** check.
**DC 10**: Subsists on the life force of unsuspecting humanoids.
**DC 15**: Favours feeding on sleeping humanoids — ensures lasting source of nourishment.
**DC 20**: Victims that die to the feeding become a [[Undead#Rotfiends & Devourers|Rotfiend]].

# Barbed Devil (Hamatula)
Infernal collectors. Fanatically protect troves of treasure and scour the planes for additions to their hoards. Bedeck their barbed hides with their most prized possessions and trophies.

Often serve as guards and accountants for ice devil generals, pit fiend warlords, archdevils, and similarly powerful villains. In return, gain protection for their own collections. Many also maintain networks of imps that search the planes for treasures or usefully greedy mortals.

Rarely collect anything as prosaic as coins and gems. Pride themselves on having the multiverse's greatest collection of one kind of thing. Refuse to steal what they covet; instead they strike bargains to claim both treasure and mortal souls.
## Tactics
Run and gun.
## Lore
**Religion** check.
**DC 10**: (AKA hamatulas). Infernal collectors. Fanatically protect troves of treasure and scour the planes for additions to their hoards.
**DC 15**: Many maintain networks of imps that search the planes for treasures.
**DC 20**: Refuse to steal what they covet; instead strike bargains to claim treasure and souls.

# Chain Devil (Kyton)
Sadistic Tormentors. Jailors and torturers of the Nine Hells. Seek to capture their victims alive so their suffering can be drawn out for days. Consider themselves morbid artisans.

 Encourage ruthless individuals to pursue forbidden magic, leading them down paths to the Nine Hells.

Psychological threats and physical harm. Unnerving gaze makes victims perceive their worst fear rather than the monster.
## Tactics
Uses its unnerving gaze to make its victims perceive their worst fear. Restrain particularly troublesome enemies. It uses Chains of Vengeance when first bloodied.

|2|A disapproving deity.|
|3|A harsh instructor or superior.|
|4|The viewer at their lowest point in life.|
## Lore
**Religion** check.
**DC 10**: (AKA kytons) Jailors and torturers of the Nine Hells. Sadistic Tormentors.
**DC 15**: Encourage ruthless individuals to pursue forbidden magic, leading them down paths to the Nine Hells.
**DC 20**: Uses unnerving gaze to make victims perceive their worst fear.

# Bone Devil (Osyluth)
Ruthless taskmasters. Fill a mid-ranking class of overseers and monitors who report on lesser devils. Sometimes found in the service of powerful mortal tyrants, watching the ranks for the smallest signs of disloyalty or incompetence.
## Tactics
Targets enemies with its fiendish focus on battle start to make them more susceptible to attacks while overwhelming with its aura of fear. Tries to use double attack. As allies become bloodied, uses aura of obedience to make them fight harder.
## Lore
**Religion** check.
**DC 10**: (AKA osyluths). Severe taskmasters. Ensure lesser devils obey their superiors. 
**DC 15**: Universally reviled by lesser devils because of harshness. 
**DC 20**: Fiendish Focus helps allies find weak spots to attack.
