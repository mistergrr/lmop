# Defining Traits & Skills
- **Fey Ancestry**. Adv. vs. Charmed; Immune sleep.
- **Regimented**. Extra dmg if within 5ft of an ally (scales with level).
- **Feywild Sacrifice (3/day) (RA) — leaders only**. On failed save, succeed instead. Allies within 60ft take 5 necrotic.
- **Innate Spellcasting**: toll the dead (lvl 0), compelled duel (lvl 1), inflict wounds (lvl 1), arcane vigour (lvl 2), thunder step (lvl 3), fire shield (lvl 4), steel wind strike (lvl 5)
