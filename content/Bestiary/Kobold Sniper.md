**AC:** 13 (Leather Armor)  
**HP:** 8 (2d6 + 1)  
**Speed:** 30 ft  
**Resistances:** None  
**STR:** 8 (-1), **DEX:** 16 (+3), **CON:** 12 (+1), **INT:** 10 (+0), **WIS:** 12 (+1), **CHA:** 8 (-1)

**Abilities:**
- **Crossbow Attack:** Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d10 + 3) piercing damage.
- **Pack Tactics:** The Kobold Sniper has advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the sniper’s allies is within 5 feet of the target.
- **Clever Escape:** When the Kobold Sniper takes damage, it can use its reaction to take the Disengage action, allowing it to move up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Terrain Bound:** The Kobold Sniper favours elevated terrain for an advantage in battle. If it starts its turn on higher ground (such as a 10-foot ledge), it gains +2 to its attack rolls until it moves from that position.
