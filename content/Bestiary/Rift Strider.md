**Role:** Agile Summoners (Teleporting Enemy – One Max)

- **Description:** Slender, flickering phantoms etched with glowing runes. They destabilise the battlefield with their ability to summon reinforcements.
- **Abilities:**
    - **Dimensional Leap:** Teleports once per round as a reaction or bonus action.
    - **Phantom Strike:** Moderate melee damage with a chance to restrain.
    - **Summon Restless Dead:** Calls forth up to two Restless Dead as an action (usable once every 3 rounds).
    - **Resilience:** Resistant to non-magical damage, vulnerable to lightning.
- **Tactical Purpose:** Forces players to prioritise its elimination to prevent being overwhelmed by summoned enemies.
