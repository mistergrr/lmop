# Personality
Wily, seductive, manipulative, controlling, scheming, and subtle. Can swallow you whole but doesn’t mean it will—would rather wrap you around its finger.

Seeks dominion over forest and treasure. Broad definition of treasure includes minions and pawns it can use to gain more treasure. Control is its driving desire—over environment and every living thing therein.

Master of misdirection. Bends others to its will by letting them think they’re getting what they want, right up until it’s too late. Skilled at assessing desires of opponents and playing off of them. Anyone foolish enough to subdue a green dragon learns sooner or later that it is only pretending to serve, while actually manipulating its “master.”

# Environment
Live in forests and jungles in any climate. Sometimes compete with [[Black Dragon|black dragons]] in marshy forests (or mangrove swamps) and with [[White Dragon|white dragons]] in subarctic taigas. But it’s not hard to tell whether a forest is controlled by a green dragon or some other sort.

Perpetual fog hangs in the air — hint of green and a whiff of acrid chlorine. Trees crowd close except where winding paths form a maze toward the center. Thick moss turns trunks emerald, light barely reaches the floor, and fog muffles sound. Branches snag, roots catch ankles, and half‑seen shapes slip through fog—luring or warning at the dragon’s whim. Fog makes navigation nearly impossible, sometimes keeping intruders out, sometimes hemming them in.

Creatures in forest serve dragon. Squirrels freeze as watchers, crows trade warnings, snakes and lizards track movement, turtles lurk in streams, and larger animals stay skittish, aware they could become prey.

# Servants
Green dragons delight in corrupting elves, sometimes twisting a minion’s mind so deeply that forest fog reflects their nightmares. Other fey are food, but bugbears, goblins, ettercaps, fomorians, kobolds, peryton, and yuan‑ti make useful servants.

# Hoard
Favored treasures include dominated people—especially notable ones—alongside emeralds, carved wood, musical instruments, and humanoid sculptures.

# Lair as Dungeon
At forest’s heart stands a massive tree with roots concealing a cave, sometimes grown over ancient elven ruins. Inside, tunnels branch like roots, with small dens for minions and roots hanging from ceilings that dragon can animate.

Fog fills tunnels, disorienting intruders and letting dragon thicken it to obscure sight, slow movement, weaken minds, sicken, or kill. At core lies dragon’s nest, often with a stream running through it and many passages allowing escape—or letting dragon circle behind lost invaders.
