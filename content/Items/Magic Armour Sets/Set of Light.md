# Description
Set of Light is armour of God: Belt of Truth (Wonder Woman lasso), Breastplate of Righteousness (Balance), Shoes of Peace (Boots of Elvenkind), Shield of Faith (Cavalier), Helmet of Salvation (Brilliance), Sword of the Spirit
# Collection Benefits
**2 pieces**: +2 to WIS saves.
**5 pieces**: Adjacent allies gain +2AC
