# Description.
+4 AC. *Requires attunement*.

Tower shield lacquered in white with an image of a rising sun at the centre. 

- **Forceful Bash**. During an attack action, make one of the attacks using the shield as a melee weapon. Deals (2d6 + 2 + STR mod.) force damage; push creatures 10ft; Creatures up to your size knocked prone.
- **Protective Field. (1/day)** Reaction; 1 minute, concentration; You or an adjacent ally is targeted by an attack or makes a save against an AoE; Create an immobile 5ft Emanation on you (creatures or objects not fully contained are pushed outside). The triggering attack or AoE has no effect to those within. Nothing can pass in or out. Any inside inside can't be damaged by external forces, and vice versa.
