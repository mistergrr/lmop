*Requires attunement*
# Description
While you wear these boots, your speed becomes 30ft unless your speed is higher, and your speed isn't reduced by you carrying too much weight or wearing heavy armour.

Once on each of your turns, you can jump up to 30ft by spending only 10ft of movement.
