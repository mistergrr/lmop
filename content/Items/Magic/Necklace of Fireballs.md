# Description.
1d6 + 3 beads. Magic action to detach a bead and throw it up to 60 feet. On contact, the bead detonates as a level 3 Fireball (save DC 15).

You can hurl multiple beads, or even the whole necklace, at one time. Increase damage by 1d6 for each bead after the first (maximum 12d6).
