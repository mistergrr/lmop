# Description.
When used as a spellcasting focus, grants its wielder advantage on checks made to maintain concentration. Can concentrate on two spells at the same time.

*Value*: 3,000 GP
# History
Wizard Flangdar was wearing it at [[Goblin Gully]] when a goblin raid wiped out the inhabitants.
