Made for a cleric of [[Lathander]], the god of dawn. The head of the mace is shaped like a sunburst and made of solid brass.

Glows as bright as a torch when commanded. While glowing, deal extra 1d6 radiant to undead; +1 mace; 1d6 Bludgeoning; *Mastery*: Sap
