**Description**
The potion is separated into brown, silver, and grey layers resembling bands of stone. Shaking the bottle fails to mix the colours.

**Effects**
When you drink this potion, you gain a Climb Speed equal to your Speed for 1 hour. During this time, you have adv. on STR (Athletics) checks to climb.
