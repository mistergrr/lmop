*Requires attunement*.

Set with 1d10 diamonds, 2d10 rubies, 3d10 fire opals, and 4d10 opals. Any gem pried from the helm crumbles to dust. When all the gems are removed or destroyed, the helm loses its magic.

You gain the following benefits:
- **Diamond Light**. Must have at least one diamond; 30ft emanation; When at least one Undead is within the area, the emanation is filled with Dim Light. Any Undead that starts its turn in the area takes 1d6 radiant damage.
- **Fire Opal Flames**. Must have at least one fire opal; Magic action; One weapon you are holding bursts into flames. Emit bright light + dim light, 10ft + 10ft. Flames are harmless to you. On hit, deal extra 1d6 fire damage. Bonus Action or drop/stow to extinguish.
- **Ruby Resistance**. Must have at least one ruby; Fire resistance.
- **Spells**. Cast one of the following spells (save DC 18), using one of the helm's gems: Daylight (opal), Fireball (fire opal), Prismatic Spray (diamond), or Wall of Fire (ruby). Destroy gem when cast.

**Taking Fire Damage**. Roll 1d20 if you take fire damage after failing a save vs. spell. On a 1, the helm emits beams of light from its remaining gems and is then destroyed. All within a 60ft emanation must succeed on a DC 17 DEX save or be struck by a beam, taking Radiant damage equal to the number of gems in the helm.
