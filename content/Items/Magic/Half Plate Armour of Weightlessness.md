# Description.
This armour has 5 charges. While you wear it, you can use a bonus action to expend 1 or more charges to cast one of the following spells from the armour, targeting yourself: Jump (1 charge) or Levitate (2 charges).

This armour regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn.

The wearer has Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

*Value*: 1150 gp
