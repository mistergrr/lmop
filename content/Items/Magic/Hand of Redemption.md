# Description.
Must be attuned. The wielder gains advantage on saving throws against death and necrotic effects. Additionally, any creature brought back from 0 hit points by the wielder’s healing spells also gains temporary hit points equal to the healer’s Wisdom modifier.
# History
Priests of [[Lathander]] had this artifact commissioned at the [[Forge of Spells]] while they were building the [[Temple of the Dawning Light]]. This artifact is one of the few that helps [[Lathander]] enter the Material Plane as a more complete and powerful form of himself.
