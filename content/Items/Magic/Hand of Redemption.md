*Attunement required*. Gain adv. on saves vs. death and necrotic effects. Additionally, any target brought back from 0HP by healing spells also gains THP equal to healer’s WIS mod.
# History
Priests of [[Lathander]] had this artifact commissioned at the [[Forge of Spells]] while they were building the [[Temple of the Dawning Light]]. This artifact is one of the few that helps [[Lathander]] enter the Material Plane as a more complete and powerful form of himself.
