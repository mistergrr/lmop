# Description.
*Divination*
Casting Time: 1 minute
Duration: Up to 1 minute

See and hear a creature you choose on any plane of existence. Target makes modified WIS save (see the tables below). Target doesn't know what save is against, only that they feel uneasy.

Knowledge Is... | Save Mod.
-- | --
Secondhand (heard of the target) | +5
Firsthand (met the target) | +0
Extensive (know the target well) | -5

You Have Target's | Save Mod.
-- | --
Picture or other likeness | -2
Garment or other possession | -4
Body part, lock of hair, or bit of nail | -10

Successful save: the target unaffected; you can't repeat scrying them 24 hours.

Failed save: Invisible, intangible sensor within 10ft of target. You can see and hear through the sensor as if you were there. Sensor moves with target, remaining within 10ft for duration. If something can see the sensor, it appears as a luminous orb about the size of your fist.

Instead of targeting a creature, you can target a location (sensor doesn't move).
