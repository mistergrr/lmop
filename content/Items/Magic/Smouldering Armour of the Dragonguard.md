# Description.
This scale mail armour has a platinum dragon motif worked into its design. Wisps of harmless, odorless smoke rise from this armour while it is worn. This suit of armour is reinforced with adamantine, one of the hardest substances in existence. While you're wearing it, any Critical Hit against you becomes a normal hit.

The wearer has Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.
*Value* 2100 gp
