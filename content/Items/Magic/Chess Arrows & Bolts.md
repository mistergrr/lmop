# Description.
1. **Pawn**. Target and one other chosen creature within 5ft of it take 2d6 piercing damage.
2. **Knight**. On target hit, you gain the benefits of the _jump_ spell for 1 minute.
3. **Bishop**. On target hit, a 5ft-wide line of celestial light extends out from you to the target. Target and each creature in the line (excl. you) make a DC 15 CON save. **Fail**: 4d8 radiant damage. **Success**: Half damage.
4. **Rook**. Target makes a DC 15 CHA save. **Fail**: You and the target switch places. Can shoot willing creature for no damage and auto-success.
5. **Queen**. On target hit, a creature of your choice within 30ft of target gains the following 1 minute:
	- Movement speed increases by 10 feet.
	- Do not provoke opportunity attacks.
	- Advantage on DEX saves.
6. **King**. On target hit, one ally of your choice within 5ft of target can use their reaction to make one melee weapon attack against the target with advantage.
