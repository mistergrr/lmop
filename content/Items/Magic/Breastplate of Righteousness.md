AC 14 + DEX. *Requires attunement*.

Burnished copper breastplate. Looks as if it were made of interlocking gears. Merchant's scales emblazoned across the chest.

4 charges. Uses while wearing:
1. **Equalize (1 charge)**. 60ft; Reaction; When you or anyone in sight rolls with adv./disadv.; negate the adv./disadv.
2. **Expunge Imbalance (2 charges)**. Bonus Action; Cast Lesser Restoration.

Regain 1d4 expended charges daily at dawn.
