This magic item replaces a lost limb—a hand, an arm, a foot, a leg, or a similar body part. While the prosthetic is attached, it functions identically to the part it replaces. You can detach or reattach it as a Magic action, and it can't be removed against your will while you are alive.
# All-Purpose Limb Upgrade
_Requires attunement_

Upgrade cost: 300 gp (271 gp + artificier charge)

This hand of this prosthetic can transform into a variety of tools; as an action, you can touch the item and transform it into any type of artisan's tool of your choice (see the "Equipment" chapter in the Player's Handbook for a list of artisan's tools). Whatever form the tool takes, you are proficient with it. Besides the regular **utilize** action of the tool, once per day, you may use the **craft** mechanic to craft an item if you have the necessary materials to do so.
# Magic Enchantment
Upgrade cost: 500 gp

As an action, you can expel the tool's inner lubricants and cast *Grease*. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
