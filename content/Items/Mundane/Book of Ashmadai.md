This red leather-bound book has the symbol above debossed onto the cover. No other marks adorn it, save for the wear of heavy use.

A closer reading reveals this to be a cult manual for the “Ashmadai.” The cult worships Asmodeus, the ruler of the Nine Hells. They venerate chaos in all forms, focusing their rituals around the practice of summoning fiends.

Rituals to summon these fiends are outlined in this book. The rituals involve extensive bloodletting. Because they're Asmodeus's devotees, the cultists have a unique pact with the Nine Hells. Every time the Ashmadai summons a fiend, they must also bind a mortal creature it. Thus, Ashmadai thugs regularly kidnap victims from across the region and deliver them to higher-ranking members to draw as needed.

Summoning a fiend involves calling out to a cosmic power. That entity will send a loyal follower to the summoner. If a specific creature's name is known, that name can be spoken when the spell is cast to request that creature, otherwise the creature type may be requested (e.g. Baatezu).

There’s no guarantee the summoned will be friendly to its summoner — invoking its true name improves chances, but still no guarantee. In order to bind the creature, it is first summoned into the center of the inverted version of the _Magic Circle_ spell to trap it while the hour-long ritual to bind it  is cast.

If there isn’t time to cast the binding ritual, the caster can form a circle around themselves on the ground with the blood used in the summoning. The summoned fiend can’t cross the circle, harm it, or target anyone within it. This should allow the summoner sufficient time to forge a pact or bind the fiend.

Defeated fiends return to the plane they were summoned from.

It might be possible to rescue a possessed individual, but the longer a fiend dwells inside a mortal body, the more the body decays. Within days, it is fit only for an unclean spirit.
