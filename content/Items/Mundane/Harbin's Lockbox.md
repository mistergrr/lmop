**Material:** Blackened iron with silver inlays, cold to the touch.
**Size:** Small enough to fit in a backpack (about 30 cm wide).
# Locking Mechanism:
- A **complex mechanical lock** (DC 18 Thieves’ Tools to pick).
- **Arcane Seal** DC 10 (Arcana) to identify. Dispelled with _Dispel Magic_ or broken with DC 15 (Arcana) check.
- **Trapped with a Glyph of Warding** DC 20 (Arcana) or _Dispel Magic_ to dispel — if forced open, it releases:
	- A **Sleep spell (5d8 HP)** (Harbin might think this is enough).
	- A **summoning effect**, releasing a tiny _imp_ or _shadow_ to attack intruders.
# Contents
- 2x pouch of gold (160gp)
- Fake travel papers under a new identity
- A forged letter of recommendation for a government position in a distant city
- A purple handkerchief embroidered with [[Harbin Wester]]'s name. Relic that casts  _Protection from Good and Evil_ (200gp)
