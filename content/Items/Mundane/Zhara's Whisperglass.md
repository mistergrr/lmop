# Description.
Once per long rest, as an action, the bearer may activate the Whisperglass and hear a softly whispered word of encouragement or warning in Zhara’s voice. The word is determined by the DM and may reflect Jargen’s current struggle or state of mind (eg “Mercy,” “Focus,” “Don’t shoot,” “Not him,” “Come back,” etc.) This can grant advantage on a Wisdom saving throw or Insight check, once per activation.
# History
Given by [[Vel’Zhara T’sirin]] and infused by her arcane powers.
