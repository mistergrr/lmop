# Description.
When you take the Attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a Jar of Piss. Target one creature or object you can see within 20ft of yourself. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 plus your DEX mod. and PB or take 1d4 poison damage and be poisoned for 1d4 rounds.
