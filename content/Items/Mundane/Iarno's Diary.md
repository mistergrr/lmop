Curse the gods and devils alike!

My son is dead.

Woe is the father who has to bury his only son. My poor Deuter had so much more to give, experience, and see. How could one who has only ever dedicated his life to helping and healing others be taken away so mercilessly?

Though his soul rests with [[Bahamut]] now, I can't escape the pain and grief of his absence. The last of my family has been taken from me; stolen while saving the city he loved.

I've heard whispers of one called [[The Black Spider]] who is searching for the [[Forge of Spells]]. Perhaps I can make a deal with them to further both our goals.

To hell with Neverwinter.
To hell with the Lord's Alliance.

The gods can wait. I will have my son back.
