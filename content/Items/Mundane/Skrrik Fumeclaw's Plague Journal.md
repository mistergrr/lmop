# [[Skrrik Fumeclaw]] — Experiment notes
## Entry-Scratch 12
Bah-faugh! Third-batch fails again. Flesh-warp spreads too much—subject’s bones drip-melt into rat-paste in less than 6 heartbeats. No transformation! Only scream-squeal, then POP. Useless. Rsolk-sire says “Too unstable.” I say “Too juicy.” Will refine next with less plague-residue, yes-yes.
## Entry-Scratch 17
Added thimble-drop of blue-fire essence to lycanth-blood. Good-good reaction. Subject screech-snarled into hybrid-form—halfman-halfrat, strong claws, foam-mouth, no eyes. Tried to speak like man-thing. Rsolk hissed. “Still too clever.” Wants beast with orders, not thoughts. Next batch, more suppressor-marrow. More mind-dulling.
## Entry-Scratch 22
Progress! Subject tried reverting to man-shape — FAILED. Limbs snap-snap, fur returns, jaw stays wide. Memory flickers, then gone. Still knows how to stalk, how to kill — but not why. Follows commands, simple orders. Intelligence blunted, but instincts sharp-sharp. Just enough to obey. Beautiful result. Closer now.
## Entry-Scratch 28
Success! Serum holds. Hybrid-form locked. No more man-thoughts, no more shifting back. Subject takes commands from scent-totem and word-tone. Still clever enough — sets traps, stalks prey, works in packs. But the will? Gone. Drowned in bluefire plague. Easy to guide, easy to point at foes.

We make soldiers now.
