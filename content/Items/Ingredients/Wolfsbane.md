AKA *monk's hood* or *aconite*. Deadly plant found in **cool, mountainous terrain**. Used to craft highly toxic poison. Recognizable for its flowers, usually **dark blue or purple**, and shaped like the hood of monk's robes.

On touch, DC 8 CON save or take 7 (2d6) poison damage. Auto-success if proficient with poisoner's kit or wearing gloves.

Craft wolfsbane poison from raw wolfsbane using **poisoner's kit**. 
- **Not proficient**: 1d4 doses per plant.
- **Proficient**: 2d4 doses. 
- **Expertise**: 3d4 doses. 
When crafting, choose **Injury or Ingested** poison. *Note*: sharply bitter taste is hard to disguise.

Target of poison takes 7 (2d6) poison damage, plus DC 14 CON save or *poisoned*. If failed by 5+ , also paralysed while poisoned. Repeat save every hour, taking an additional 7 (2d6) poison damage on a fail. On three successes, poison ends. Lycanthropes and other shapechangers have disadv. on these saves.

*Value*: 1 gp as plant; 50 gp as poison.
