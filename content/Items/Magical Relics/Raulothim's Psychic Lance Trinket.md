# Description.
Casting Time: Action
Range: 120 feet

Unleash a lance of psychic power from your forehead at a creature you can see. Alternatively, utter a name of a target within range (even if you can't see them). If the named target isn't within range, the lance dissipates without effect.

The target must make an INT save. Failure: 7d6 psychic damage and incapacitated until the start of your next turn. Success: half damage and not incapacitated.

Relic crumbles to dust after use.
