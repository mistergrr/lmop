# Day 11 (Cont'd)
**Combat**
The stranger peers at the party until his eyes lock on to [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]]. He then starts waving his arms about. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] immediately opens with *Sacred Flame*. The stranger is momentarily engulfed in flames before he shrugs it off. [[Grrurika]] follows up with *Guiding Bolt*, doing 18 damage. The stranger calls out, "Come, my pretties," and rustling is heard from the trees nearby. The stranger points his staff at [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] and sticky web fills a 20ft. cube around him, restraining him and [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]]. The stranger follows up with magical darkness, also centred on [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]].

[[Dorian of Galdor]] runs to the side so he can see past the darkness and casts *Scorching Ray*, all three rays hitting for 20 total. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] frees himself of the web and advances towards the stranger. [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] helps break [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] out of her web. [[Dorian Blackthorn]] misses his fire bolt. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] takes steady aim and get a critical hit for 28 damage.

Giant spiders suddenly emerge from the flanks, One webs [[Dorian of Galdor]] and the other webs [[Grrurika]]. A wolf spider runs out at [[Grrurika]] and gets a critical attack in on her. She goes down. Through sheer force of will, she staunches the bleeding and gets up. Another wolf spider takes a bite out of [[Dorian of Galdor]], poisoning him.

The stranger, almost killed in the first round, coughs up blood and is shrouded in silvery mist. When the mist clears, he's gone.

[[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] moves back through the web to get closer to the spiders and hucks a javelin. His curse limits his damage, but the spider is slowed. He then shouts a rallying cry, bolstering [[Dorian of Galdor]] with 7 temp HP. [[Dorian Blackthorn]] flings *Magic Missiles* at one of the giant spiders, killing it. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] kills a wolf spider with her crossbow, and uses *Wails of the Dead* to damage the nearby giant spider. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] kills the last wolf spider with *Sacred Flame*, and [[Dorian Blackthorn]] finishes off the last giant spider with *Fire Bolt.*
**Combat End**

The party makes camp here. #rest 

# Day 12
[[Dorian of Galdor]] and [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] wakes up and find their intelligence has decreased by 2 points. They realise that they've been cursed for stealing items from [[Oghma]]'s altar, but they think it's worth it for the loot and the party continues to [[Phandalin History]].

They arrive in town around midday. [[Ziggywix Tinkerton]] runs up to [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] and says he's been snooping around and thinks that [[Halia Thornton]] is a good contact to make to find shady sort of work. They notice a "foreclosed" sign outside [[The Wee Inn]]. 

The party brings [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] to the townmaster's office, where he reunites with [[Sildar Hallwinter]]. They return [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]]'s gear and he generously compensates them with 100gp. [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] offers them 50gp each for rescuing him, but he will need a day to get the funds. He also offers them a share of the mine's profits if they can help him gain control of the [[Forge of Spells]].

The party asks [[Harbin Wester|Harbin]] what's happening with [[The Wee Inn]], and he offers it to them (as a way of boosting the town's economy now the [[Redbrands]] are gone, and also to entice them to stay and protect him and the town.) They also negotiate the deed to [[Tresendar Manor History]]. [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] says his brother is an architect and can help them rebuild a manor/fort there.

[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] visits [[Halia Thornton]] and receives Quest: [[Investigate Harbin]]. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] doesn't want to involve the other in shady dealings, so she only takes [[Grrurika]] and [[Dorian Blackthorn]] for help. [[Grrurika]] offers to occupy [[Harbin Wester|Harbin]] and the others' time by taking them to the tav. [[Dorian Blackthorn]] volunteers to distract [[Harbin Wester|Harbin]]'s personal guard at his house.

The guard is chatting up a local gal, so [[Dorian Blackthorn|Dorian]] goes up to him and soldier-to-soldier starts talking him up in front of the girl. The guard swells with pride and starts showing off his battle scars.

Meanwhile, [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] slips round the back of the house and shimmies up a gutter. She picks the window lock on an upper-floor windows and sneaks in. She finds herself in a bland bedroom with nothing of interest. Outside the room, in the hall, she hears small footsteps approaching. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] manages to duck behind a couch in time and hears the panting of a small dog. She throws a ration into the bedroom as bait and shuts the door behind the dog after it runs in.

The last room upstairs is a study. In the locked desk, she finds an iron key. The key opens a safe in a nearby cupboard. The safe contains silver bars and coins, but she doesn't take anything besides [[Harbin's Letter]]. She locks the house up exactly as it was and leaves out a window and down the gutter.

[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] grabs [[Dorian Blackthorn]] and they head to the tav to regroup with everyone. In the tav, [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] is buying everyone drinks, including a very drunk [[Harbin Wester]] and a tipsy [[Grrurika]]. [[Harbin Wester|Harbin]] goes home and [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] rounds everyone up to meet her in [[The Wee Inn]] so she can share what she found.

She reads the letter out loud to inform them what has been happening in town. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] is very suspicious of [[Halia Thornton]] and questions whether they can trust her and is worried about what she'll do with the information. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] says [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] doesn't have to trust her, but she will. She stops by [[Ziggywix Tinkerton|Ziggywix]] to check with him. He think [[Halia Thornton|Halia]] will make a good contact for work.

# Day 13
[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] delivers the letter to [[Halia Thornton|Halia]], who inducts [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] into [[The Zhentarim]].

[[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] is also inducted into [[The Lord's Alliance]] when he drops by to see [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]] and to collect the money from [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]]

In the morning, [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] and [[Dorian of Galdor]] find their intelligence has decreased by a further two points, so they decide to go back to [[Cragmaw Castle]] to return the stolen goods.

Everyone buys horses.
