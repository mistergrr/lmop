# Day 13
The party leave [[Phandalin|Phandalin]]. [[Ziggywix Tinkerton|Ziggywix]] is asked only to look after [[The Wee Inn]]. They take a day of travel to get near [[Cragmaw Castle]]. They make camp a safe distance away.

In the middle of the night, [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] hears noise of something big moving their way fast. There's also a strange lightness to the sky in that direction. He tries waking everyone up when an owlbear burst through the trees. He uses his [[RR Collar]] to his advantage and convinces the owlbear to pause. This is when he notices it wasn't looking at them, but past them.

The party wake up one by one and run out of the way of the owlbear, who looks like it's about to pounce. A small herd of elk burst through as the smell of smoke reaches their noses. The party realize the animals are fleeing a forest fire. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] looks to the sky to see if there's a dragon about, but there is none.

The party don't have time to pack their camp before the wildfire is upon them. The mount their animals and flee, taking a wide berth to circle back to [[Cragmaw Castle]]. [[Grrurika]] and [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] are exhausted from the lack of sleep.

# Day 14
## Return to Cragmaw Castle
The party try to approach [[Cragmaw Castle]] stealthily, but fail to avoid the gaze of a wandering party of five goblins. The goblins attack. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] uses *scorching ray* to kill two goblins. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] hucks a javelin at a goblin, but misses. The goblin turns to flee and sounds out, raising the alarm. One of the other goblins fires a bow at [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]], hitting her. She replies in kind by killing all three remaining goblin minions with *magic missiles*.

[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] feels let down by the party's sneaking ability and breaks off from the group. She heads to the broken tower entrance, only to find it guarded by two hobgoblins. Looking for an alternative route in, she scales the building that contains the shrine and tries to pop a rooftile off.

Meanwhile, the rest of the party attempt an assault on [[Cragmaw Castle]]. Arrows fly out of slits in the guard towers, bouncing harmlessly of [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor's]] shield. An arrow sticks [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] in the thigh. She sends an *acid splash* to a random point past the castle gates, possible damaging some enemies. Everyone tries to take cover behind [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] and [[Grrurika]].

[[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] tries to send a spell into an arrow slit, but it warps with wild magic. #wild-magic Pustules erupt from his face and explode on all present party members besides [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]]. He does 52 total team damage, siphoning health from everyone to himself. The damage reduces [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] to 0HP. [[Grrurika]] doesn't take the risk of healing her #grrurika-sin, so the party drag [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] away and they make a hasty retreat, where a potion of healing is used to heal [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]].

Back at the castle, [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] manages to pop a rooftile off, giving her enough of an opening to rappel down. She stops right above the altar and returns the [[Obsidian Knife]]. She ascends her rope. On the roof, a cool breeze blows across her face as she feels the brain fog dissipate and the curse lift. She returns to where she left the party, only to find them gone. She eventually finds them a couple miles away and they catch her up on what happened.

The lie for a long rest at around midday. [[Grrurika]] cures her exhaustion, but [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] fails to get good sleep: exhaustion level 2. #rest 

## Cragmaw Castle, Take Three
The sun has set when the party wake up. They head back to [[Cragmaw Castle]]. They work their way round to where the broken wall entrance is and see two hobgoblins posted as sentries. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] decides to do his own Mission Impossible alone. He somehow sneaks around the guards' view and scales the building. He uses [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina's]] roof entrance and rappels down. He replaces the chalice he stole and sacrifices a [[scroll of Animate Dead]] as an offering to [[Oghma]].

His curse is also lifted and his intelligence returns. He returns to the group.

They party decide to assault the keep. [[Grrurika]] booms her voice and makes the ground tremor to shake the hobgoblin sentries. "Leave now," she says. It works to scare the sentries, but they back into the castle instead of running outwards.

The party file into the ruined tower and cautiously follow the guards. One of them alerts their leader. A large hobgoblin steps out to parley. It's [[Targor Bloodsword]], the new leader of the [[Cragmaws]]. He asks if they're the ones who killed [[King Grol]]. When they confirm, he says,

>"I'm at an impasse; I don't know whether to thank you for aiding my promotion, or kill you for trespassing. So I'll make you an offer. It's only on the table for a minute, then it's gone forever.
>
>"Leave now, and we'll turn a blind eye. If you stay and fight, you won't survive. You have one minute to decide before we kill you."

They party have a hot debate. They would like to clear the threat from the region (and kill a bunch of goblins), but surviving the woods has taken a toll and they don't really have business left here. They decide to leave peacefully.

## Into the Woods
Into the woods they go, with [[Dorian Blackthorn]] navigating the way through the woods. They head northwards until they hit a patch of deadwoods. There's an ominous fog and magical aura about the place. It's swirling with necromantic energy. They skirt the edge until night falls.

During the night, [[Dorian of Galdor]] and [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] hear howling/ They wake the party. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] shines her lantern into the dark and they see six eyes reflecting the lantern's light. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] uses her [[Scroll of Fireball]] without hesitation, hurling it toward the eyes. A wall of fire erupts, extinguishing the eyes of whatever creature or creatures were there, but igniting a new forest fire.

The party are forced to move camp again. Everyone except [[Dorian Blackthorn]] and [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] are exhausted from pushing themselves further.

# Day 15
## Meeting Owlbear Grylls
[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] is the only one who didn't manage to get any rest and is now at exhaustion level 2. It slows down the whole party's pace, but the day passes without much significance until evening arrives. A voice calls out from the thickets, "Halt. are you friend or foe?" A ranger emerges, bow drawn, "What business do you have in these parts?"

The party explain they're looking for the [[Temple of the Dawning Light]] to right a wrong. The ranger lowers his bow. "Then you are a friend. The forest, while always dangerous, has had a sudden blight emerge, roughly eight days ago"

[[Grrurika]] blanches when she works out it's around the time she killed the aspect of [[Lathander]]. The ranger introduces himself as [[Owlbear Grylls]] and recommends they leave the forest and head back to civilisation because of a newly formed Gulthias blight.

[[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] and [[Dorian of Galdor]] recall the lore about the vampire, Gulthias. [[Owlbear Grylls]] says that the new blight has already had severe consequences to the woods and its inhabitants around the area and offers to lead safely through the woods to see if they can eliminate the blight. He tells them that the source of the blight is a [[Gulthias Tree]] that was once the oldest oak in the forest. The oak was planted when followers of [[Lathander]] built the [[Temple of the Dawning Light]]. The area was blessed with growth and life and came to be called the [[Cradle of Life]]. It's the birth site of the [[Neverwinter Wood]]. The [[Gulthias Tree]] is roughly a mile away from the [[Temple of the Dawning Light]].

He helps them set up a camp (since they lost their supplies in the first forest fire). He offers to create a sleeping salve for [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] — he'll just have to forage for ingredients as they travel the next day.

They spend some of the night exchanging stories. [[Owlbear Grylls]] nearly gets [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] to partake of his jar of piss, but [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] rescues him. #rest 

# Day 16
In the morning, [[Owlbear Grylls]] has a breakfast prepared for them — pissfish from Edal's Creek. He then leads them towards their destination. Travelling with him is faster than without, as he deftly leads them through the woods without any encounters.

They reach the crotch in Edal's Creek around nightfall. Across the creek, the party can see the blighted woods. The prepare to rest up in preparation for the big day ahead. #rest
