# Day 9
[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] is pulled over by a mysterious halfling outside the **Old Dirty Dwarf**. News of her event with the mimic has spread and someone in the [[Thieves' Guild]] thinks she has what it takes to join. She's invited to visit the [[Thieves' Guild]] by following the marks through Scar Alley. She's also warned that flashing gold coin draws unwanted attention and to be wary of Spellscarred.

The party visits the blacksmith. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] and [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] trade some rarely-used weapons for silvered short swords. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] also buys 20 bolts.

While passing through Scar Alley, they stop to ask a Spellscarred peasant what's going on. The peasant is scared he's being mugged, but [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] gives him food, which calms him down. He doesn't have much useful information, besides the presence of Spellplague and that he hears choirs calling out to him.

[[Ruby Roo|Ruby]] leads the party out via the north gate. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] collects and dons his armour. The party are given a day-pass which allows them to re-enter the Hold within 24 hours without paying tax again.

On the way to the Whispering Woods, clouds form overhead. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] deduces that it's a magical effect that's turning day to night. Deeper into the woods, howls echo before the party are attacked by wolves.

**Combat**
The wolves flank the party from either side. The biggest wolf hangs back a round before leaping in. [[Ruby Roo]] runs off to safety. During the fight, one of [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G's]] spells misfires and turns him blue. #wild-magic  Four of the seven wolves are killed. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] manages to grapple the alpha and pin it to the ground. [[Dorian of Galdor]] casts "Speak with Animals" and gets the alpha to surrender. He finds out that the wolves are hungry because the monsters have chased the prey away. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] promises the alpha that the party's mission is to eliminate the monsters. He convinces the wolves to help in the fight.

The wolves hold a wake for their fallen family. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] howls with them. This counts as a short rest. [[Grrurika]] uses her "Channel Divinity" to heal [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] #grrurika-deed , who was the only one who got hurt. The others use the time to find [[Ruby Roo|Ruby]] and convince her the wolves won't attack her any more.

They continue toward [[Ruby Roo|Ruby's]] home. A thick fog descends and the overcast day turns to night. It's as if there's an eclipse, but [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] and [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] confirm that there isn't supposed to be one on this day. They deduce that there's something necromantic going on.

They see figures moving around in the fog. A voice suddenly booms:
>Darkness falls across the land  
The midnight hour is close at hand  
Creatures crawl in search of blood  
To terrorize y'all's neighbourhood 
And whosoever shall be found  
Without the soul for getting down  
Must stand and face the hounds of hell
And rot inside a corpse's shell
>
>The foulest stench is in the air  
The funk of forty thousand years
And grisly ghouls from every tomb  
Are closing in to seal your doom
And though you fight to stay alive  
Your body starts to shiver
For no mere mortal can resist  
The evil of the thriller

The figures in the fog advance upon the party and attack.

**Combat**
Waves of zombies attack the party. A few go down and stay down, but some keep getting back up. The party tries fire, but all that does is create fiery enemies. One of [[Dorian of Galdor]]'s spell misfires and causes all creatures within 30ft to become vulnerable to piercing damage for the next minute. #wild-magic  In the fog, the wolfs help thin the zombie horde. As the rounds go on, a distant thumping continues to grow closer.

Thirteen zombies are killed before two leap onto [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] and wrestle him to the ground. The thumping arrives as a zombie ogre with a morningstar. It attacks [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] and the two zombies lying on him. The zombies smash to pieces. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] manages a massive attack to the ogre thanks to its temporary vulnerability to piercing damage. The ogre manages to hit [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] with another attack. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] informs the party he can't take another of those. [[Dorian Blackthorn]] casts a spell to prevent the ogre from making any opportunity attacks to allow [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] to make a tactical retreat. #grrurika-sin 

[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] takes careful aim with her crossbow. The bolt pierces the zombie ogre in the brain stem. As it falls, she leaps onto it and stabs it multiple times, leaping back off before it hits the ground. The sixteenth and final zombie makes a move at one of the Dorians, but the alpha wolfs slams into it, carrying them both out of sight into the fog. The wolf returns, chewing on a leg bone.

Now that the last zombie has fallen, the fog and darkness clear, revealing what appears to be a zombie mage. "You ruined everything!"

The wolves pounce on the mage and he immediately surrenders.

**Post-combat**
The wolves stand menacingly over the mage. Something seems weird about the mage — he looks like a zombie, but isn't behaving brain-dead or with a hunger for brains. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] blows a gust of wind at him and a mask flies off, revealing a human who's dressed as a zombie.

The mage explains that his name is Rob Rombie and he was searching for a pirate's treasure that might have been buried in the area over 200 years ago. He would've gotten away with it if it weren't for you meddling kids.

He has documents tracking the pirate's activities — ships they robbed. It estimates a sizable sum of loot stolen over the years. He also has a journal written by someone who was capturing the history of the pirate's exploits. It says that the pirate was last seen heading to the Whispering Woods to bury his treasure, although the author could never figure out exactly where it was buried.

Rob Rombie was raising zombies to see if they could lead him to the treasure, but he lacked the ability to raise any dead he could speak to. The zombies turned on him and he donned a zombie disguise to protect himself. He was trying to improve his necromancy to get an answer when the party stopped him.

The party ties him up and the wolves leave. [[Ruby Roo]] returns and leads the party to her home. It's a mess inside — leftover pizza strewn about, arcade machines, junk everywhere. [[Ruby Roo|Ruby]] rewards them with loot. She also takes her stash of "root" and lights it in a pipe. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] also takes a hit of the pipe to help her sleep off the exhaustion.

The rest of the party try searching for the pirate's treasure, but they don't find any clues to its whereabouts — 200 years has removed any traced of burial. They also don't have any digging tools to help them out. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] eats a Ruby Rax and gains 1d4 temp HP. He also plays on the arcade machine to see if making the high scores reveals any hidden rooms, but he fails to set a decent score.

The party decides to turn in and have a long #rest 

# Day 10
[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] wakes up fully rested. The party starts making their way back to [[Helm's Hold]], looking for signs of buried treasure, but failing to see anything. At the gate, they're told they can turn their prisoner in to **Captain Dunfield**. He's located in the [[Dragon's Gauntlet]].

They head there and turn him in. [[Grrurika]]'s rank and testimony are enough evidence of Rob's necromancy. They're rewarded 200gp and informed that Rob Rombie will see the gallows soon. Rob is taken to a cell.

[[Dorian of Galdor]] asks if he can view the log of visitors to [[Helm's Hold]], explaining that he's looking for his lover. **Captain Dunfield** allows it (thanks to [[Grrurika]]'s presence). There's no entry of [[Erynel]] having visited [[Helm's Hold]] in the last 6 months.

[[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] buys rations for whoever needs more.

[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] leaves to find the [[Thieves' Guild]]. 

[[Dorian of Galdor]] goes to the cells to ask Rob if he knows of any Ley Line convergences nearby. Rob tells him it's not nearby, but he knows of a famous necromancer's wizard tower in [[Old Owl Well]]. Rob begs [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] to break him out in exchange for a split of the pirate's treasure, but since he has no guaranteed way of finding it, [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] declines.
