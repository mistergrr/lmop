- A **goblin horde** swept through the mine, killing the inhabitants. Among the lost was wizard named **Flangdarf**, who happened to be visiting to broker a trade of the old mining equipment.
- Goblinoids now use the mine as a **base**. They **attack** passing travellers and take their loot.

The main entrance to the pit is a cave now overgrown by a huge tree.

# Day 61 | An Illusive Patron
[[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] silvers a case of bolts and sells his scimitar for two hand crossbows.

[[Tom Hagen]] requests to meet with the party, He speaks on behalf of the [[Iarno Albrek|Illusive Man]] who wants to act as the party's patron. The party requests 5 health potions and some information on [[Iarno Albrek|Glasstaff]]. He gives them a mission: [[Quests#^obtain-relic1|Obtain Amulet of Focus]] — anything else they find in [[Goblin Gully]] is theirs to keep. The party want to know if [[Iarno Albrek|Glasstaff]] has any weaknesses/aversions and if he's linked to necromancy in any way.

> Stasia makes a remark about [[Iarno Albrek|Iarno]] being the [[Iarno Albrek|Illusive Man]], but the missions seems harmless enough.

The party will collect cochineal for [[Ronaldo of Triboar]], before clearing out [[Goblin Gully]] on their way to [[Twilight Tor]].

## Giant Boars
[[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] and [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] track down the location of the boars and immediately take a turn running to the rear of the group. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] readies a shot if the boars attack. [[Grrurika]] tries to scare the boars away with a roar, but ends up getting run over by a giant boar. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] blows four boars away and injures a giant boar with a _fireball_. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] finishes the giant boar off. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] kills the remaining two giant boars and [[Grrurika]] succeeds at scaring the remaining boars away.

The party harvests 3 + 1 vials of cochineal. [[Ronaldo of Triboar]] only requested 3 vials, so they're giving him extra.

[[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] sends his dragonflies and [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas']] sparrow, "Ska", to scout the woods within a half hour radius. They all come back with reports of enemies. The party contemplate going back to [[Phandalin|Phandalin]], but end up finding a merchant caravan on the [[Triboar Trail]].

## Ma'iq
The merchants are initially wary of [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]], but the party manage to ease their concerns. One of the merchants, [[Ma'iq the Liar|Ma'iq]] invites them to smoke shisha with him. He has a collection of ornate daggers and challenges [[Grrurika]] to a throwing competition. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] and [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] also join in, everybody putting in a wager.

[[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] ends up winning the contest, earning 5ep from [[Ma'iq the Liar|Ma'iq]] but he lets everyone else keep their things.

# Day 62 | Goblin Gully
[[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] forgets _magic missile_. 

[[Goblin Gully]]

It's a stormy day, but [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] manages to find goblin tracks. They follow them to a clearing where they see a tree with a massive hollow, cart tracks leading in. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] moves to the entrance and sees two goblins standing guard. They ask who he is and he intimidates them into following his orders. He sends Toothpick to find the "manager," and get Snotblock to fetch him a bowl of gruel.

The party sneak past the first chamber where a goblin is dancing for a swarm of skitterlings. At the next chamber, the _dining hall_, a group of goblins and two bugbears see them. The bugbears come close, suspicious. Snotblock returns with a bowl of gruel. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] struggles to convince the more intelligent bugbear and a fight breaks out.

**Combat**
[[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] restrains Snotblock with a leg lock and unloads two full rounds into one of the bugbears, killing it. [[Grrurika]] attacks the second bugbear and [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] kills a goblin. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] wipes out a gang of goblins with a _fireball_, making a racket that alerts the foes in the adjacent rooms. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] uses _magic missile_ to finish off the remaining bugbear as well as two goblins.
