## Inside the Temple
They follow the blood trail and find a priest bleeding out on the floor. The priest says he thought he saw [[Lathander]], but it turned out to be the skeletal giant elk, which gored him. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] volunteers to have his health siphoned to keep the priest alive. The priests asks to be carried to the altar where the [[Hand of Redemption]] sits.

The way to the altar is blocked by thorny brambles. [[Dorian Blackthorn]] trips and falls face first into them. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] also gets cut while carrying the priest to safety. The priest drops a bible. There's a bookmark in the bible — a damaged key to a code of some sort. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] grabs the key and brings it to the mural outside to decipher its title. She finds it's called, "Berun's Search for Knowledge."

Back inside, the priest touches the [[Hand of Redemption]] and siphons life force from it it heal himself. He stops abruptly when he notices the hand is dimming instead of having the unlimited life force it once did. The [[Hand of Redemption|Hand]] floats up and into an adjoining room that's blocked by a large stone.

Near the boulder lies a massive wheel inset in the floor. The wheel is cracked, stiff with decay and the tangle of vines. Gears connect the wheel to an aperture in the ceiling. Upon turning the wheel, a sliver of light shines through the open aperture and refracts off crystals near the altar. A set of five crystals shines a beam onto five murals.

The party use the bible to figure out the corresponding crystals to light the murals with. On rotating the last crystal into place, the large stone rolls away, opening the next chamber.

## The Second Chamber
You find yourselves on a narrow, winding path that floats above a sea of shimmering light. Along the path, you hear echoes—voices of those you’ve healed, saved, or helped throughout you journey. These voices speak of gratitude, but as the cleric progresses, the echoes shift to voices of those who were harmed by the curse, expressing pain, fear, and betrayal. As the cleric moves forward, they must face illusions of these individuals. Each illusion presents a moral challenge, such as confronting an ally who distrusts them because of the curse or meeting a spectral version of themselves who tempts them to embrace the curse’s power. These challenges test the cleric's resolve, compassion, and willingness to confront the consequences of their actions.

- Beggars of [[Neverwinter]] (Babblebell)
- Steel Wolves' companions: Clobb, Eihr, Perts, Schuul

## Trial 2: Trial of the Soul
This realm is filled with a serene dawn landscape—a place where the sun is eternally rising, casting long shadows that stretch unnaturally far. The party arrive at a crossroads guarded by three entities, each an image of [[Grrurika]], but representing a different aspect. They stand on pedestals labelled: The Tempter, The Judge, and The Healer. The cleric must confront each entity in order to pass. Her allies can volunteer to take the damage on her behalf.

[[Grrurika]] approaches the Healer first — A gentle, sorrowful figure bathed in a soft light, representing the cleric’s original devotion to healing and life. This entity offers the cleric forgiveness but warns that true redemption requires sacrifice. Each party member regains full health.

The approaches the Tempter next — A shadowy figure with eyes glowing with necrotic energy. This entity whispers promises of unmatched power and eternal life if the cleric would only submit to the curse.

Lastly, she approaches the Judge — A figure holding scales before them. This entity demands that the cleric account for their past actions and make a choice: to be judged by the weight of their sins or to judge themselves. She chooses to be judged. Each sin of her choosing to let a teammate risk death is played out before her.

### Grrurika Sins
- Killing [[Lathander]]
- Defiling the body
- Siphoning [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] to death during Mimic fight
- Choosing not to heal [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] during undead ogre fight
- Kicking PoW Hobgoblin to death in the nuts
- Did not attempt to heal [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] when he blew himself up
- Did not heal [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] when she was downed by goblins outside [[Cragmaw Castle]]

With each sin, [[Grrurika]] takes 1d6 necrotic damage. Despite her wishes, her teammates step in at time when her lack of action hurt them, forgiving her, personally. They take the damage on her behalf.

After the sins, her good deeds are also weighed, healing her each time.

### Grrurika Deeds
- Revives [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] during goblin night ambush on the way to [[Helm's Hold]]
- Heals [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] during wolf fight at [[Helm's Hold]]
- Heals [[Dorian Blackthorn]] during Reconsecration
- "*Bless*" the Dorians and [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] during [[The Arcane Convergence]]

After the judgement, the path to the final trial opens.

## The Final Trial
A pathway opens to the final chamber. It's like the Matrix's white room, except there's a cloud of death lingering in the air. At the centre of the chamber, the [[Hand of Redemption]] floats above an altar, its light is flickering as if dying out. The Hand beckons her to approach. Only [[Grrurika]] is able to enter the chamber.

Each step (5ft) towards the [[Hand of Redemption]] reduces the [[Grrurika]]'s health. Every 5ft, she looks back at the door and sees the exit is within reach, giving her the choice to continue or leave. The halfway mark is the point of no return. [[Grrurika]] recalls a memory every 5ft.

She recalls special memories of her adventures with her party, and of her time in [[The Steel Wolves]]
### The Final Choice
As [[Grrurika]] reaches the [[Hand of Redemption]], she know she has a final decision to make: Siphon her own life essence to rekindle the Hand or drain the last spark. She choosing to transfer the last of her life force into the Hand. As her vision fades to black, she sees the [[Hand of Redemption]] flicker back to life.

The party sees [[Grrurika]] sacrifice herself. The hand then emits a blinding light the clears the necrotic clouds. When the light fades again, [[Grrurika]] stands in the chamber, reborn.

Her bane is lifted and she gains the task of becoming the *Keeper of the Hand.* The [[Hand of Redemption]] is now in her care, and she can use the power in it to enhance her healing abilities.
