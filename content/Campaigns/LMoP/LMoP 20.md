## Priest's Quarters
[[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] finds a poem scrawled on the wall in the Priest's Quarters. He recognises it as [[Erynel]]'s. They also find a diary belonging to [[The Black Spider]]. It contains a note about [[Vhaeraun]], who [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] and [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] have some knowledge of.

[[The Black Spider|Nezznar]] begs for his mask. He offers information about the location of another magic item somewhere in the cave in exchange for having the mask put back on his face. He explains it's to hide himself from [[Lolth|The Spider Queen]] and is forthcoming with information now that he has been captured.

He tells the party of his plan to take the [[Forge of Spells]] in order to create magic items to help him retake [[Omphalos]] from his former House. He shares that he plans to kill his mother and create equality for drow, regardless of gender.

[[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] wants to kill him, but [[Nundro Rockseeker|Nundro]] notes that [[The Black Spider|Nezznar]] would have inside information about what's currently at [[Omphalos]] and that their goals somewhat align—or at least overlap.

The party chain [[The Black Spider]] to the Rockseekers and continue exploring the cave.

[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] scrambles into the cave to help out.

## The Caverns
The party step into the **Collapsed Cavern** and spot a couple of Bugbears arm wrestling in the cavern below. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] and [[Grrurika]] join the competition and both make small winnings. They continue on to the **Booming Cavern** and see three [[Redbrands]] and a bugbear helping another bugbear rappel down to the surf below.

**Combat**
[[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] takes a shot at a Redbrand before [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] uses *Gust of Wind* to push a couple of the others back. [[Grrurika]] casts *Toll of the Dead* on the bugbear. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] sends a *Circle of Daggers* after the same bugbear, causing it to fall to its death and be washed away by the surf. The bugbear that was rappelling drops into the water below. It runs to a pile of rocks and picks up shiny gloves, which it dons.

One of the Redbrands flees down a corridor and another gets blown out of sight by the *Gust of Wind*. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] moves her *Circle of Daggers* and finishes off the bugbear below, whose body gets trapped in a pile of rocks. [[Grrurika]] and [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] finish off the last Redbrand.
**Combat end**

[[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] rappels down to find the item the bugbears were looking for. He's battered by the waves, but manages to take the [[Gloves of Swimming and Climbing]] off the bugbear's body. The party help hoist him up the rope as the waves crash into him.

A ghost appears to the party and warns them to turn back. The ghost reveals itself as Brunda Hearthfist, a former member of Saint Nick's crew. She tells them that [[Thariel]] has lost her mind as a vengeful spirit. The party assures her they'll help [[Thariel]] rediscover herself and find peace.
