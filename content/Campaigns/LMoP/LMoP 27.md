# Day 64 | Prepping Day 1
[[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] goes fishing. Catches a tortoise and releases it. Catches an emperor crab so big, Reef has to help him reel it in. [[Grrurika]] cooks it into a chowder. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] forages for [[Wolfsbane]]. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] uses her contacts to smuggle in a poisoner's kit to make 4 doses of wolfsbane poison.
# Day 65 | Prepping Day 2
Grrurika and Barabbas dig pits outside town and fill them with pointy sticks. Dorian buys beers to use the bottles for molotovs. A fox leads Thia to more wolfsbane.
# Day 66 | Full Moon, Yo
The party finish setting up defences outside the town. Prep bonfires to light later — One is filled with [[Wolfsbane]]. An owl signals the incoming battle. Eldina builds herself a makeshift roost to set up in. [[Snotblock Dadson|Snotblock]] sets up in a tree to fire from above. 

**Combat**
10 dire wolves, 2 winter wolves, 7 werewolves in 3 waves. 8,300xp total.

Eldina fires flaming arrows into the 3 bonfires. The one with wolfsbane does well to poison the werewolves as they run down the hill. Dorian came closest to going down, but made his save against lycanthropy. Everyone else did a good job staying at range and not getting surrounded.

>Combat lasted 7 rounds, but everyone enjoyed it. Said it was varied enough to keep it interesting. Seeing their effort put into making traps pay off was rewarding.
