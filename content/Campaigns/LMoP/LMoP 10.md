## Starting Scene

# Day 10
The party leave [[Helm's Hold]], gathering their gear at the gate. They skirt the edge of [[Neverwinter Wood]] as they head south. They set up camp for the night. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] and [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] keep watch. They hear rustling and chatter from beyond the bushes.

**Combat**
A hobgoblin hunting party surprise the team. Two rush in with shields while arrow fly through the brush. One hobgoblin wielding a greatsword points to [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] and shouts, "There's the small one. Get him!" One of the other hobgoblin retorts, "Forget the small one, there are elves here," and the archers start targeting [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] and [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]].

[[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] kills a shielded hobgoblin with "magic missile." [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] kills the other. 

[[Dorian of Galdor]]'s magic goes wild and he casts "Fireball" centred on himself. #wild-magic  No hobgoblins are hit by it. Himself, [[Grrurika]], [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]], and [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] are hit by it. [[Grrurika]] fails her saving throw and it reduces her to 0HP. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] uses his "second wind" to heal and he rushes over to stabilize [[Grrurika]]. The hobgoblin captain goes after [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]]. One of the hobgoblin archers hits [[Dorian of Galdor]] and reduces him to 0HP.

[[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] kills a hobgoblin archer with "magic missile." [[Dorian Blackthorn|Dorian B]] hits the hobgoblin captain with "shocking grasp," preventing him from taking opportunity attacks so [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] can move to safety. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] takes aim and kills the hobgoblin captain. The last hobgoblin is an archer that hits [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] with an arrow. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] heals [[Grrurika]] with "cure wounds."

[[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] grapples the archer. Instead of attacking, [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] pickpockets the grappled archer (who is still at full health). She finds two elf ears in its pockets.

The party reduced the archer to 1HP and ties him up. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] asks why he has elf ears. The hobgoblin spits and says, "Filthy elf." [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] makes him shit himself. All questions are met with rude replies. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] cuts his right ear off. [[Grrurika]] kicks him so hard in the nads — 7 dmg — it knocks him unconscious and he starts making death saving throws. #grrurika-sin  [[Dorian of Galdor]] casts "spare the dying." on him. The party struggle to figure out that slapping a 0HP enemy won't wake them up. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor's]] slaps don't wake him up so he uses "rally" to give the hobgoblin temp HP and it wakes up.

The hobgoblin answers all their questions about [[Cragmaw Castle]]. He tells them that [[King Grol]] has a new pet that he's afraid of. Also, there are roughly 20 goblins (he can't count) and some hobgoblins. There's someone else, Lhupo, who also has a pet. The hobgoblin struggles to describe the pet. The best he can do is it's a nightmare worm. He also tells them that the best entrance is from the north, through a broken wall.

The party then debate over what to do with the hobgoblin. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]], [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]], and [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] want to kill it. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]], [[Dorian Blackthorn|Dorian B]], and [[Grrurika]] are against killing prisoners of war. They decide to tie him up and let fate decide. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] isn't fully satisfied, so she pulls out a dagger, grabs the hobgoblin's tongue, and hacks it off.

The party get ready to finish their long rest. [[Dorian of Galdor]] wants to identify some of the magic items they've collected. He identifies the properties of the [[RR Collar]] and puts it on himself. He discerns some jewellery as mundane, and senses faint magic from [[Thunderclap]], although it's not enough to have any properties.

On his last use of "identify," he rolls on the wild magic table and casts "ice storm" as a level 4 spell on himself. #wild-magic  The rest of the party, who were at a safe distance away, rush to toss him [[Ruby Rax]] to buff his HP. It narrowly prevents him from dying, but he's still reduced to 0HP. [[Grrurika]] does not attempt a prayer of healing. #grrurika-sin 

The *ice storm* also pulverizes the ties-up hobgoblin. [[Dorian Blackthorn]] considers this a natural death. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] manages to stabilize himself and the whole party completes their long rest. #rest 

# Day 11
The party reach [[Cragmaw Castle]] around midday. [[Grrurika]] and [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] scout ahead. [[Grrurika]] uses her tremor sense to identify and locate all enemies in the castle. She also sense a small unmoving figure and deduces it could be [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]]. He's in a room with two large presences. 

They find the hidden entrance and step into an empty room. The rest of the party joins.

The party waits in that room while [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] and [[Grrurika]] scout the room where [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] might be. Opening the door gives away their presence. Inside is a bugbear, [[King Grol]], who is wearing a crown and there's a large wolf with him. The room has a throne and an unconscious dwarf in the corner.

**Combat**
[[King Grol]] and wolf rush at [[Grrurika]]. The bugbear shouts, "GET IN HERE!" and two hobgoblins enter behind [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]]. The hobgoblins swing at [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]], one of them hitting with the bonus damage.

The rest of the party scrambles to join the battle, but there's a bottleneck at the door. The sound of more goblins approaching come from two different directions. [[Dorian Blackthorn|Dorian B]] rushes into the next room to cast *Ray of Frost*, barricading the door. This works as the sound of goblins banging on the door is soon heard. [[Thia Galanodel]] guards the other entrance, where there's no door to barricade. Three goblins run in and she slays one of them.

[[King Grol]] hears the sound of his minions dying from the other room and bellows, "YOU DO NOT HAVE MY PERMISSION TO DIE!" The in his voice instils enough fear into the dying goblin that it stands up again half-past-dead.

Back in the main room, [[Grrurika]] is defending [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]]. She manages to kill one of the hobgoblins. This creates enough room for [[Dorian of Galdor]] and [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] to enter, [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] leapfrogging over [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] in order to rally [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]]. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] kills the other hobgoblin while [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] helps fight the big bugbear and the wolf. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] takes the opportunity to disengage and finds an adjoining room to hide in.

Outside, two goblins manage to break down the door. [[Dorian Blackthorn|Dorian B]] kills one of them but goes down. The three goblins are trying to get around [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]]. One of them exits the castle and scrambles up the broken wall, looking for a way to get into the throne room. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] kills one of the two in front of her. The one that scrambles up the wall finds itself above [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]]. It draws two daggers and leaps down towards her, but she fires her crossbow, sending the goblin flying in the other direction, out of the castle.

[[Dorian of Galdor]] tries firing a spell at the big bugbear, but he wild magics and ends up teleporting himself to the [[Astral Plane]]. #wild-magic 

[[Grrurika]] and [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] continue fighting [[King Grol]] and his wolf. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] taunts them into focusing on him [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] kills the last goblin she's engaged with and runs to [[Dorian Blackthorn]] to help him up and heal him.

Since [[King Grol]] is focusing his attacks on [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]], [[Grrurika]] manoeuvres behind him and casts "Inflict Wounds." She winds up and give [[King Grol]] a thunderous smack on his ass. The necrotic strike leaves a blackened hand print on [[King Grol]] as his body is sent flying across the room. Seeing [[King Grol]] die, the wolf and remaining goblin flee.

As soon as the battle is over, [[Dorian of Galdor]] teleports back from the [[Astral Plane]], leaving shimmering glitter floating in the air around him.

## Gundren Rescued

[[Grrurika]] rushes to the dwarf and finds it's indeed her cousin, [[Gundren Rockseeker]]. They help him up. [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] is surprised (and relieved) to see everyone there, but maintains an obviously false bravado and tells them he had [[King Grol]] right where he wanted and was about to pull off a heroic escape. He suddenly panics and asks if anyone has found his map. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] loots a key off [[King Grol]] and uses it to unlock a chest nearby. Inside is [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren's]] map, along with 3 potions of healing, and some money.

[[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] is relieved and says that [[The Black Spider]] also knows the location of [[Wave Echo Cave]] and wants to reopen the [[Forge of Spells]] for his own gains. There was chatter that [[The Black Spider]] himself was coming to [[Cragmaw Castle]] to execute [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] in order to keep the cave's location to himself.

[[Dorian of Galdor]] finds a poem scratched into the wall. It's about him and is signed off by [[Erynel]]. [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] confirms that both [[Erynel]] and [[Iarno Albrek|Glasstaff]] were at the castle recently, but left about a day ago. [[Erynel]] was looking well and didn't seem to be a prisoner. The party is worried that [[Erynel]] has started working with [[Iarno Albrek|Glasstaff]].

[[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] asks if he can be escorted back to [[Phandalin]] so he can find his brothers and continue their mission. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] loans him a shortbow and 27 arrows. The party takes a short rest.
