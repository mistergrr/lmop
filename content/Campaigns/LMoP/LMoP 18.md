# Day 25
As they leave at the crack of dawn, an orc party returns. They found the party's hidden horses and are bringing them back to their cave at [[Wyvern Tor]].

**Combat**
[[Grrurika]] kicks off a surprise attack with *Toll of the Dead*, but it misses. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] casts *Acid Splash*, which hits three orcs. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] uses *Magic Missile* to hit two orcs. 

An orc goes for [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] but doesn't hit. Same with [[Grrurika]]. One orc does manage to hit [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]]. [[Grrurika]] kills an orc.

[[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] hits an orc with *Magic Missile* and [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] finishes it off and *Action Surges* to finish off another. The last orc tries to flee, but [[Grrurika]] kills it with *Lol of the Dead*.

**Combat end**

## To Old Owl Well
As they approach [[Old Owl Well]], they hide their horses outside the ruin walls. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] sneaks into the camp. As he enters the tent, twelve zombies wander out of the tower and start surrounding him. His necklace of hands (now rotting) mask his smell enough that the zombies don't attack him. He heads back to the party and hands everyone a hand.

The party move through the crowd of zombies and are caught in the middle when [[Hamun Kost]] steps out of the ruined tower. "What's the meaning of this?" he says.

The party explain that they've been asked to investigate what's going on here at [[Old Owl Well]]. [[Hamun Kost]] tells them he's busy doing research there and wishes to be left alone. They want more information on what he's up to, but he refuses to tell any stranger. He offers them a deal: if they can speak to [[Agatha]] and ask her the name of the wizard who built the tower here, he'd give them some spell scrolls he found here.

They promise to leave and return with the requested information in exchange for safe passage through [[Old Owl Well]]. They mount their horses and [[Hamun Kost]] watches them leave. When they are around 80ft away, [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] casts *Magic Missile* at [[Hamun Kost]] and gallops away. [[Hamun Kost|Hamun]] throws up *Shield* to block the missiles.

The rest of the party gallop towards [[Hamun Kost]] to fight him and his zombies.

**Combat**
[[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] hucks a javelin which kills a zombie. [[Grrurika]] uses *Turn Undead* which disperses more than half of the zombies. She then uses *Spiritual Weapon* to hit [[Hamun Kost]]. [[Hamun Kost]] in return knocks [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] prone.

The party manage to either kill or turn the zombies away and thump [[Hamun Kost]], who surrenders.
**End Combat**

[[Hamun Kost]] surrenders and asks the party what they want. He gives [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] his [[Robe of Melee Protection]] and a couple of spell scrolls he found in the tower. They decide to part ways unamicably.

## Back In Phandalin, At Last
The party finally return to [[Phandalin|Phandalin]] after 12 days of being away. They head to [[The Wee Inn]], where [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] is hosting a soft launch. [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] is eager to head to [[Wave Echo Cave]] in the morning.

They're celebrating and partying when a commotion breaks out. [[Redbrands]] have walked into the tavern, and are causing trouble, with [[Ziggywix Tinkerton|Ziggywix]] caught at the centre. There's also a hobgoblin standing over [[Ziggywix Tinkerton|Ziggywix]]

**Combat**
The party attack the [[Redbrands]]. [[Grrurika]] goes for the hobgoblin, but he's nimble and avoids the attacks. The hobgoblin drop kicks a redbrand into a table, which confuses the party. In the end, [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] kills a redbrand, [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] gets 2 with *Magic Missile*, and [[Grrurika]] finishes one off with *Toll of the Dead*.
**End Combat**

One of the [[Redbrands]] carries a letter from [[Iarno Albrek|Glasstaff]] that mentions an assassination plot on [[Harbin Wester]]. The party decide they don't have time to deal with it, but hand it over to [[Sildar Hallwinter]]

The hobgoblin turns out to be a friend of [[Ziggywix Tinkerton|Ziggywix]]. His name is [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd]]. He volunteers to join the party in order to atone for his past and spread good. meanwhile, [[Richtor Helmsley III]] announces he's heading off on his own path to join [[The Lord's Alliance]] and help [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar]].

They sleep at the tavern. #rest
