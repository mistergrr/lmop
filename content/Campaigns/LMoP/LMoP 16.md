# Day 19
Using the map from [[Owlbear Grylls]], the party spend the day travelling to Agatha's Lair. On the way, [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] recounts what she knows about banshees. They camp just outside the area of Agatha's lair. #rest
# Day 20 | The Banshee's Bargain
[[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] sends dragonflies to scout the lair. They return saying they see no sign of anyone. They go to the alcove that is Agatha's lair and [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] creeps inside. A chilling cold runs down her spine as she realizes she's being watched. [[Agatha]] materializes behind [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]], saying "What fools are here to throw their lives away?"

[[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] steps forward and says, "We're here to bring you gifts in the hopes you can provide us some information." She present the jade frog statuette and the jewelled comb from [[Sister Garaele]]. [[Agatha]] comments that cheap trinkets will not please her so easily, but takes the comb and absent-mindedly starts brushing her hair with it. She looks at [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] and says, "You're a beauty, aren't you? How I would love to peel your face off and wear it as my own. I, too, used to be beautiful. But that time has long passed. Oh, how I did enjoy chatting up the noblemen."

[[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] steps forwards and announces his nobility. [[Agatha]] seemingly notices him for the first time. She suddenly seems bashful. "Why, yes, you *are* nobility, somewhat. How horrid you must think I look now." She hides her face.

[[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] insists he find her beautiful and proves it with a kiss. He grabs her, and with a twirl, kisses her. [[Agatha]] grabs his head in a vice-like grip, Her clammy tongue shoots down the back of [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor's]] throat, blocking airflow. She holds him there long enough that he nearly passes out before releasing him, her tongue sliding back out of his insides.

[[Agatha]] wipes her lip and says, "I haven't been kissed like that since grade school." She then turns back to [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] and says, "Very well, I'll answer your question. I am a powerful diviner and can locate nearly any person, place, or object. But I will only answer one question, so choose wisely."

[[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] asks about the location of Bowgentle's spellbook. [[Agatha]] answers that she traded the book a couple of centuries ago to a necromancer named Tsernoth of Iriaebor. After that, it's location has remained magically hidden.

Before [[Agatha]] disappears, [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] offers her a pair of emerald earrings so [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] can ask a question. He also kisses [[Agatha]], much like [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] did. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] asks if she can locate [[Erynel]]. After providing a trinket for [[Agatha]] to conduct her divining ritual with, she informs him that [[Erynel]] is currently located in [[Wave Echo Cave]]. With that, she says, "Now leave before I take all your faces." Then she disappears.

The party leave the lair and make camp in [[Conyberry]]
# Day 21 | Locating Endurance Hill
[[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] *Speaks to Animals* and gets swallows to point them in the direction of an old settlement in the hills that could be [[Endurance Hill]]. They navigate in the mountains and manage to take cover when they hear large wings beating overhead. They eventually find the ruins of a dwarven outpost.

[[Grrurika]] reads the runes at the outpost, which mention a trial for warriors up the path. They rest in the outpost for the night. #rest 

# Day 22 | The Trial of Endurance
The party tie the horses in the shelter of the ruins of the outpost. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] rides [[Fang]]. After hiking and climbing, they reach a temple at the end of the trail. In the temple is a giant statue of [[Torm]].

[[Torm]] speaks through the statue and asks [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] what he seeks. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] explains that he's there to regain his lost strength. [[Torm]] tell him that he must prove himself through three trials in order to prove his strength, wit, and leadership ability.

## Trial 1
[[Torm]] explains that with power must come wisdom. A leader must use wisdom to lead a party.

Five warriors formed a party tasked with recovering an artifact sacred to Torm. During the dungeon expedition, their mission ended in tragedy with a TPK. The spirits linger, awaiting justice and bound by fractured memories. The players must listen, compare accounts, and find the true cause of their downfall.

Each spirit recounts their experience with each party member. All of them either blamed someone else for incompetence, or outright accused them of intentionally causing the party to fail.

The party decides that every spirit is guilty of pointing fingers at others instead of looking inwards and reflecting what they could've done differently. All five spirits are found guilty, and a spiritual flames engulf each as they are tried.

## Trial 2
Through the next doorway, the party find themselves in an arena. [[Torm]] says this is a trial to see if they can defend a relic from waves of enemies. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] has the option to use his turn to make Charisma checks in order to inspire his party members, or gather insight about a particular enemy type.

### Wave 1
[[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] stands by the relic as the last line of defence. The others set up in various positions around the arena.

The first wave are  2x [[Kobold Sniper]], 3x [[Restless Dead]]. The kobolds climb a tower and start shooting from high ground. One of them hits [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] with a crossbow bolt, while the other hits the relic.

[[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] casts *Acid Splash,* hitting all three zombies. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] follows up with an up spelled *magic missile* to each of them, hitting one twice and killing it. [[Grrurika]] speaks a *Healing Word* to heal [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]]. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] shoots a zombie dead and [[Fang]] kills another. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] finishes off the kobolds with *Magic Missiles*

### Wave 2
The enemies are the same, plus a [[Rift Strider]]. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] prepares for the round by going into stealth. [[Grrurika]] 

The [[Rift Strider]] starts by summoning three undead minions. The zombies shamble towards [[Grrurika]] and swing at her. One connects. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] casts *Acid Splash* on the group of summoned undead. One of them dissolves in a pool of acid, while the others manage to shamble on.

[[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] gets into a position with a clear line of sight to cast *Chromatic Orb* on the [[Rift Strider]]. The [[Rift Strider]] buzzes with instability and teleports forward, trying to reach [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]]. It doesn't reach him in time before it explodes. 

[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] kills a zombie with her crossbow, but it gets back up. The zombie minions go after [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] and get a couple of hits in on him. One by one, the party finishes the kobolds and zombies off.

[[Thia Galanodel|Thia]], [[Grrurika]], and [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] eat a [[Ruby Rax]] each.
### Wave 3
Same as the previous wave, but with an [[Arboreal Bruiser]] stomping into the arena.

[[Grrurika]] casts *Bless* on herself, [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]], and [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]].

The zombies rush for [[Grrurika]] again. They hit her, but she manages to keep concentration on her spell. The [[Rift Strider]] summons more undead minions. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] casts *Acid Splash* on the new group of undead minions, killing one of them for good. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] kills a couple more with *Magic Missile*. 

The [[Kobold Sniper|kobolds]] shoot at [[Grrurika]] and the relic, both hitting their targets. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] shoots a zombie dead and stealths up again. [[Grrurika]] gets her *Spiritual Weapon* out, nearly killing a kobold. The [[Rift Strider]] opens a rift and bolsters the health of the kobolds, two zombies, and the [[Arboreal Bruiser]].

One of the zombies rushes to the relic, but [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] strikes it down. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] takes another minion out with *Words of Radiance.* [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] hit the bruiser with *Chromatic Orb,* which bounces and kills a kobold. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] hits the bruiser from stealth, but it seems resistant to her bolt.

The [[Rift Strider]] calls out an order to rush the relic. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] strikes a minion down with her sword as it runs past her. [[Grrurika]] hits the bruiser hard with her *Guiding Bolt* and also hits a kobold with her *Spiritual weapon*. The bruiser slams her in retaliation, taking her  below half health. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] sounds an inspiring call and rallies [[Grrurika]], bolstering her with temporary health.

[[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] casts *Chromatic Orb* at level 2, bouncing off the bruiser and killing both the kobolds. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] kills another zombie with a critical shot and stealths up again. [[Grrurika]] swings at the bruiser, but fails to connect, but she manages to hit it in the back with her *Spiritual Weapon*. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] attacks the [[Rift Strider]] strider with *Sacred Flame* but it's still standing.

Using scorching ray, [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] hits the bruiser with a couple of rays. The bruiser roars as its arboreal body catches fire and it drops dead. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] rushes up to the rift strider and stabs it in the back. It screams and teleports towards the relic before it explodes, taking out the remaining zombies with it.

The relic is damaged, but intact as the second trial is completed.

## Trial 3
For the final trial, roles are reversed. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] must prove his combat ability in a duel with a [[Champion of Torm]] while the rest of the party have to hold up a large weight to bolster [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor's]] strength.

The [[Champion of Torm]] looks a lot like [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]], but with a greatsword in place of a warhammer and shield. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] throws his shield down and wield [[Thunderclap]] with both hands.

The fight is evenly matched, both fighters using their armour and parries to reduce incoming damage. Both use battlemaster manoeuvres to try breaking through each others defence. With the help of the party buffing him, [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] manages to get Torm's champion to kneel.

With all three trials passed, the curse lifts off [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] and his strength returns. He is granted the title, "Torm's." The party also get a boon: **Go Team, Go** — Party receives +1 to all melee and attack rolls for 1 day.

A bolt of lightning flashes down and strikes [[Thunderclap]]. The symbol of a lightning bolt is etched into its side as a magical power unlocks from within the hammer. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] feels the gaze of his ancestors looking upon him with pride.

The party spend a night's rest in the temple #rest
