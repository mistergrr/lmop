# Day 26 cont'd
The party step into the Starry Cavern. [[Grrurika]] doesn't sense anything inside the structures with her *tremorsense*. They go to the northern structure and find the door is bent shut. They find a side door and pick the lock to get in. It's an armoury of sorts. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] finds [[Dragonguard, +1]] and dons it. They continue into the next room and find themselves at the [[Forge of Spells]]. A [[Spectator]] floats above.

The [[Spectator]] shows clear signs of mental instability, first referring to itself as "god," then immediately as "Bob." It also thinks it was born yesterday. It recommends the party leave, which they do.

The party go back to the lower level of the Starry cavern and draw the attention of undead.

**Combat**
[[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] opens with *Chromatic Orb* which bounces between two ghouls. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] follows up with a *Fireball*, which takes care of all the ghouls and skeletons, leaving only a Flaming Skeleton left. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] shoots the Flaming Skeleton, but it seems resistant to piercing damage. The Flaming Skeleton throws two motes of flame but misses both. It then runs up to the group.

[[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] casts *Chromatic Orb* on the Flaming Skeleton. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] gets a shot on it, followed by a *Magic Missile* from [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]]. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] gets into melee to use his scimitar, but it also seems resistant to slashing. [[Grrurika]] leaps from the higher escarpment and is carried by a divine wind in order to bring her hammer down to finish off the Flaming Skeleton. The skeleton explodes in a ball of fire and bone shards, injuring [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]], [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]], and [[Grrurika]].
**Combat End**

# Meeting Thariel
[[Grrurika]] breaks down the door to the next structure. A wraith appears and accuses the party of trying to plunder the mine. [[Grrurika]] tries to convince her they're not here to plunder and calls [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] for help. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] tries talking [[Thariel]] up and reminding her that she was good and helped the people of [[Phandalin|Phandalin]] as well as [[Wave Echo Cave]]. They also get [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] in to prove he's a Rockseeker and convince her that they're here to reform the Phandelver Pact.

[[Thariel]] doesn't seem to be bothered about speaking to her goodness. She seems embarrassed about something. She says the forge was damaged in the fight and that it can no longer operate as it once did. And even if it did, her last mistake prevents anyone from reaching the forge to use it. The makes a deal with the party: if they can fix her mistake, she'll let them take the treasure of this room (except that which is hers) as she has no more use of material things.

The party head towards the [[Forge of Spells]], going through the armoury, [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] takes [[Dragonguard, +1]]

# Spectator Fight
[[Grrurika]] *Blesses* [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]], [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]], and [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] and the party return to the forge and file in. The [[Spectator]] seems to want to converse. It asks [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] how old he is, saying again that it was born yesterday. It also informs the party that tomorrow is its birthday. The party sings happy birthday for it and it joins in, at the end asking who Bob was. The party start attacking.

**Combat**
The [[Spectator]] starts charging up its central eye. It turns to face the last person who injures it. [[Grrurika]] is the first to act with a *Command* to "Halt." The [[Spectator]] fails its saving throw, but sacrifices an eye stalk to shake off the effect. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] casts *Scorching Ray*, hitting all three rays. The [[Spectator]] then fires a ray each at [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] and [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]], who both make their saves. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] casts *Poison Spray* and it responds by shooting her with its *Wounding Ray*, doing partial damage. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] shoots it with her crossbow and [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] gets in with his melee weapons.

The [[Spectator]] fires its central ray at [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]], who ducks out of the way just in time for the blast to go over his head, blowing open the sealed forge doors. [[Grrurika]] flanks the [[Spectator]] and hits it from behind. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] hits it with *Chromatic Orb.* The [[Spectator]] hits [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] with its *Confusion Ray* and flies between [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] and [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]]. Both of them hit it. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] in his confusion, fires at [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] but misses. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] also manages to dodge the central eye ray.

[[Grrurika]] casts *Guiding Bolt*, which the [[Spectator]] reflects at [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]]. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] finishes the [[Spectator]] off with another *Scorching Ray* attack.
**Combat End**

[[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] takes the [[Obsidian Knife]] that was sitting in the forge. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] takes [[A glass jar containing a bit of flesh floating in pickling fluid that casts Circle of Power]]

The party return to [[Thariel]] who thanks them for finally giving her peace. She and Brunda leave this plane of existence. The party claim their rewards. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] solves the **Cryptex** and opens it to find a map to the [[Library of Whispers]].

# Leaving the Mine
The party keep moving south and find themselves in the **Fungi Cavern**. [[Grrurika]] notices the dead body near a coffer and braves the poison spores to reach it. When she sees it's locked, [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] comes over to pick the lock. He succeeds and they take the loot. On the way out, he inhales some spores and is poisoned. Everyone holds their breath and runs to get through to the next corridor. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] and [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] are also poisoned by the spores.

[[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]], [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]], [[Grrurika]], and [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] drink the mystery cocktails that [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] brewed. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] becomes charmed by [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]], who suddenly starts speaking Spider and sprouts vestigial spider legs. She turns to [[The Black Spider]] and in her Mickey Mouse voice, says, "[[Lolth]] has blessed me." [[The Black Spider]] is freaked out by this and tries to flee, but is yanked on the rope that ties him to the Rockseeker brothers and falls flass on his arse.

In the corridor, undead are feasting on corpses. They turn to attack the party.

**Combat**
[[Grrurika]] uses her *Turn Undead* and vanquishes five ghouls. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] shoots a skeleton and nicks it. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] runs in and finishes off another skeleton. In the commotion, a **Grick** sneaks up on [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] and grabs her. It starts dragging her back to the **Fungi Cavern**. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] tries rescuing her and attacks it with *Scorching Ray*. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] stabs the grick with her sword, but it's still holding on. [[Grrurika]] finishes off the last skeleton, smashing it to pieces.

[[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] dashes back to help [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] and attacks the grick with help from [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]]. The grick chomps down and drops [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] before [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] finishes it off with *Magic Missile*.
**Combat end**

[[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] cradles [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] and brings her back with his last health potion. [[Grrurika]] says a prayer of healing and tops up the most injured with a little more health.

The party explore the **Assayer's Office** and the **Old Guardroom** and uncover more of the story of what happened at [[Wave Echo Cave]] 500 years ago. Low on health, spell slots, and completing their main objective, the party leave for [[Phandalin|Phandalin]]

# Back Home (Day 27)
On the journey back, [[The Black Spider]] appeals to the party to let him go, saying they could work together to kill his mother and clear [[Omphalos]] of its captors. The party, including the Rockseeker brothers, are divided about what to do. Half of them think it's better to entrust [[The Black Spider]] into [[Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar's]] hands and prison. They decide to sleep on the decision for now, holding [[The Chasm]] at [[The Wee Inn]] in the meantime.

As they enter [[Phandalin|Phandalin]], there's an announcement being made in the town square. [[Harbin Wester]] is giving a speech about "The Hero of Phandalin" and announces that [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] saved him and the town from an assassination attempt. He unveils a statue of [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] with his new title written on the plaque.

As [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] stands for his portrait to be painted, [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] shits his pants with *Prestidigitation*.
