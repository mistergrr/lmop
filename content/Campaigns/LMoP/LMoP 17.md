# Day 23 | Wyvern Tor
[[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]], brimming with excitement over his restored strength, is keen to take on the orcs at [[Wyvern Tor]]. They make their way through the mountains, dragonflies helping [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] lead the way.

As soon as they're in sight of the cave entrance to [[Wyvern Tor]], [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] scouts ahead. She spots an orc sitting as sentry. She shoots them from stealth, but the orc survives and ducks into the cave. The party regroup and find a tripwire trap leading to the cave entrance. They set up behind and above the cave mouth. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] climbs up the hill to find that the tripwire is set to sett of a rockslide. He intentionally triggers the rockslide while the rest of the party feign death screams to trick the orcs.

An orc cautiously sticks its head out of the cave to see what happened. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] hits him in the face with *Acid Splash.* [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] also shoots him.

Four orcs spill out of the cave and start attacking. One of them hits [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] with a javelin throw. Another does the same to [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]]. [[Grrurika]] hits one of the orcs. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] finishes off two of the injured orcs with *Magic Missile.*

[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] kills an orc with her crossbow. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] runs back down the hill and kills the last orc.

[[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] cuts the hand off an orc body to add to his collection. He has apparently been doing this for a while now. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] decapitates another and tosses the head into the cave, taunting anyone inside. The cave goes deep into the mountain and no one besides the party were around to hear him. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] is disgusted by this action.
## Inside the Cave
The party head into the cave, narrowly avoiding traps along the way. They eventually reach an open cavern with a campfire burning in the middle. Inside, they see three orcs and an ogre. One of the orcs is the leader, [[Brughor Axe-Biter]]. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] casts his *Wand of the Wilds* at the group. A fart cloud spawns on them, obstructing vision.

[[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] takes the [[Jar of Piss]] that [[Owlbear Grylls]] gave him and uses it to coat [[Thunderclap]]. The rest of the party remain out of sight around the corner. The orcs run out of the cloud and at the party. One orc runs to [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] and the other to [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]]. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] protects himself with his shield but [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] takes a hit. [[Brughor Axe-Biter]] runs out and at [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]], managing to strike him.

The ogre also emerges from the fart cloud and dashes to get closer to the action. [[Thia]] rounds the corner and squeezes in between [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] and the orc fighting him. She steps up to the ogre and casts *Sacred Flame,* but misses. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] shoots the orc with her crossbow.

[[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] casts *Chromatic Orb* on the ogre, which bounces and hit [[Brughor Axe-Biter]]. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] uses his *Action Surge* to kill the orc fighting him, creating a one-on-one with Brughor. The orc that [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] squeezed past turns his attention to her and slashes her in the back. The ogre also tries to smash her, but she throws an arcane *Shield* up just in time. The ogre looks confused and says, "Elf no squish?"

[[Grrurika]] kills the orc between [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] and [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] with *Toll of the Dead*. 

The ogre smashes down on [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] again, this time breaking through her arcane shield. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] is nearly dead. She hits the ogre with an *Acid Splash* then turns to the party. "Guys, there's a health potion in my backpack," she says, before taking out said health potion and drinking it.

[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] hits the ogre with a crossbow bolt. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] hits it with *Scorching Ray.* [[Grrurika]] hits it with *Guiding Bolt,* finally finishing the ogre off.

[[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] and [[Brughor Axe-Biter]] are still exchanging blows, [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] coming out on top. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] knocks [[Brughor Axe-Biter]] out by casting *Sleep* on him. They tie him up to question him.

**Combat End**

The party loot the cave and take [[Hew, +1]] off Brughor. The axe has dwarven runes, so they pass it to [[Grrurika]]. There's a dead dwarf in the corner. They wake [[Brughor Axe-Biter]] up to question him. He tells them [[The Black Spider]] is looking for any dwarf named Rockseeker and to bring any map on them to him for a reward.

The party notice the cave continues deeper. They ask Brughor what's further in, but he refuses to tell them. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] stands him up and leads him down the passage at knife point. [[Brughor Axe-Biter]] says, "Alright then, let's do this," before charging into the passage. They hear the click of a trap being triggered. A large boulder rolls down from inside the passage, killing [[Brughor Axe-Biter]]. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] manages to dive out of the way just in time.

The party have a long rest in the cave. #rest
