# Day 26 | Wave Echo Cave
The party arrive at [[Wave Echo Cave]]. Near the entrance, they find the body of **Tharden Rockseeker**. [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] wants to return the body to [[Phandalin|Phandalin]] for a proper burial, but the party convince him to help them find **Nundro** first.

They step into the cave and set off the spider webs. They burn the webs to hide their progress going forward. [[Grrurika]] uses her *tremorsense* to help lead the way. She leads them north to the **Dark Pool**. They spy the skeleton in the water and fish it out. They take the two platinum rings and [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] keeps the [[Prosthetic Limb]].

## North Barracks
They continue and find the **North Barracks** with 2 [[Bugbear]] and 3 [[Redbrands]].

**Combat**
[[Grrurika]] charges in and attacks a Redbrand. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] opens up with *magic missile* on one of the bugbears. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] sends her newly learned spell, *Circle of Daggers* at the other bugbear.

The bugbears are the first to react. One goes for [[Grrurika]] but she holds a strong defence. The other rushes for [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] but also fails to land a hit. The first Redbrand does manage to hit [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]]. Another Redbrand also manages to land a flanking hit on [[Grrurika]].

[[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] fires a shot from the back and hits the first Redbrand. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] goes for the same Redbrand and hits them. [[Grrurika]] uses *LOL of the Dead* on the Redbrand on her and also brings her *Spiritual Weapon* out on the Redbrand in the back. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] casts another *magic missile* on his Bugbear. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] also moves her *Circle of Daggers* back on her Bugbear.

The bugbears attack and miss again, unsuccessfully trying to grapple [[Grrurika]] and [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]]. The one in the *circle of daggers* tries to flee from it and [[Grrurika]] kills it with an attack of opportunity. [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] kills the Redbrand he's been shooting, and so does [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] with a one-two.

The last Redbrand tries to flee out of the barricaded door. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] convinces him to give up with a warning shot next to his head. 

**Combat end**

The Redbrand tells the party that they've been unable to take the [[Forge of Spells]] because of the undead. He also points out the general direction of where [[The Black Spider]] and Nundro are. He tells [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] that [[Erynel]] is around with [[Iarno Albrek|Glasstaff]] and that they seem to be working towards something together, but he doesn't know what. He also says that goblins and orcs were supposed to help with the cave, but they never showed up. The party take the his cloak and convince him to leave the cave.

They take a short rest and [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] takes a Redbrand hand.

## Temple of Dumathoin

They continue to the room where [[The Black Spider]] is. Listening at the door, they overhear a conversation: someone says controlling the Forge is taking too long and that they'll head out to find the hidden library.

The party barge into the room just in time to see [[Iarno Albrek|Glasstaff]] and [[Erynel]] stepping through a portal. [[Erynel]] doesn't quite recognise [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] (because he's blue) before the portal closes.

Two bugbears stand near [[The Black Spider]].

"There you are. I was beginning to wonder if you'd ever show. You are quite meddlesome, aren't you? You dismantled most of [[Iarno Albrek|Iarno's]] [[Redbrands]] and threw the [[Cragmaws]] into their own war for the crown. The [[Many-Arrows Orcs]] of [[Wyvern Tor]] haven't shown up yet — I'm guessing you had something to do with that as well?"

"All your actions have put us both at an impasse. Now neither of us has the fighting power to actually take the [[Forge of Spells]]. Why don't we make a deal? You clear the way to the [[Forge of Spells]] and leave [[Wave Echo Cave]], and I'll give you your dear Rockseeker back."

The party decide not to negotiate and attack.

**Combat**
[[The Black Spider|Nezznar]] casts *Web* into the middle of the party. He then drops magical *Darkness* over them. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] and [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] break through the web and run out of the area of *Darkness* and take cover behind the pillars. Two giant spiders and two wolf spiders descend from the ceiling. [[Grrurika]]

[[Grrurika]] also emerge into the light and attacks [[The Black Spider]]. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] finds a a spot in the light at the rear.

A Giant Spider and a Wolf Spider bite [[Grrurika]]. Her poison resistance helps her negate the poison, but she's still hurt.

[[The Black Spider]] casts an *Arcane Burst* at [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]], hitting him hard. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] throws a fireball in the middle of the mob. It kills both Wolf Spiders, a Giant Spider, and a Bugbear. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] and [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] both shoot [[The Black Spider]]. [[Grrurika]] uses a combo of [[Hew, +1]] to attack the remaining Bugbear and her *Spiritual Weapon* to attack [[The Black Spider]]. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] casts *Magic Missile* at one of the Giant Spiders, which [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] finishes off with a *Fire Bolt*.

[[The Black Spider|Nezznar]], badly injured, dashes to flee out the door. [[Grrurika]] and [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] chase after him. His hits each of them with an *Arcane Burst*, but they manage to knock him out.
**Combat End**

They tie up [[The Black Spider]] and find a key on him. They use it to unlock the door to The Priests Quarters and find [[Nundro Rockseeker|Nundro]] there, unconscious, but alive.

[[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] takes a Wolf Spider leg as a trophy, and puts on [[The Black Spider]]'s mask. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] takes the [[Spider Staff]]. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] takes the [[Tankard of Sobriety]]. They also find a potion of healing, gems, and money.

# Summary
The party explore [[Wave Echo Cave]]. They defeat [[The Black Spider]] and rescue [[Nundro Rockseeker]]. [[Iarno Albrek|Glasstaff]] and [[Erynel]] portal away in search for a hidden library.
