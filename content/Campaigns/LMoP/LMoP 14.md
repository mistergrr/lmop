# Day 17
## Starting Scene
[[Grrurika]] has nightmares of the moments she killed the Aspect of [[Lathander]]. She also sees faint lights leading her towards the [[Temple of the Dawning Light]]. [[Owlbear Grylls]] has made a sleep salve so [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] can get a restful night. #rest

# Day 18
The party wake up to a breakfast of pissfish, cooked by [[Owlbear Grylls]].

They have to cross Edal's creek. [[Owlbear Grylls]] uses a bamboo pole to vault across the 15ft. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] tries to throw [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] across, but she lands halfway into the water. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] tries to pole vault, but also doesn't make it. [[Grrurika]]'s attempt is a long jump, which doesn't get her all the way over. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] dries them all of on the other side.

[[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]], [[Dorian Blackthorn]], and [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] manage to pole vault across.

The land here is darkened and black, but not in a dead way. The trees are still healthy, but discoloured. Woody shrubs line the ground.

On the way to the centre of the blight, the ground rumbles as something big approaches. They duck down and see an awakened tree fleeing from the direction they're heading in. [[Dorian of Galdor]] jumps out to get its attention.

"What are you?" it asks.

"I'm an elf," Dorian replies.

"I've never seen a blue elf before," the tree says.

"What are you running from?" Dorian asks.

"The blight. All my friends are gone, and I'll be next if I don't get out of here soon." The tree then runs off.

## The Cradle of Life
They reach the source of the blight, which is a grove with a giant oak tree at the centre, blighted by Gulthias. The oak is leaking red sap from two knot holes. Three red flowers circle the oak, transferring magical energy towards the oak. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] senses that they're providing strength to the tree. 

The magical chaos is palpable, forming dust devils with the winds of arcane energy.

[[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] checks the surrounding area and finds no man-made traps.

### Skill Challenge
[[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] steps into the grove. The **Young Gulthias Blight** awakens and turns its attention towards him. He back back out. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] also keeps back, waiting to see what happens.

[[Dorian of Galdor]] steps into the centre of the grove and uses his necklace as a focus to channel arcane energy and stabilise the chaotic energy. #skill-success Five **twig blights** reveal themselves and swipe at [[Dorian of Galdor]], all hitting. The combined attack deal serious damage to [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]]

Two **needle blights** also shift. One fires at [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] and hits him. The other fires at [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]], who throws up an arcane *Shield*, blocking the attack.

[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] crouches behind [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] and fires a bolt at one of the **twig blights** attacking [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]], killing it. [[Grrurika]] also hides behind [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] and says a *Prayer of Healing* for [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] #grrurika-deed 

[[Dorian Blackthorn]] also steps into the grove and slams his staff down to send arcane roots towards the **Young Gulthias Blight**. He fails to overpower the dark energy and it backfires, giving him a level of exhaustion. #skill-fail

The **Young Gulthias Blight** shakes spores around, which form two new **needle blights**. It then slams some branches into the ground and the earth in a 30ft radius starts to rupture.

[[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] runs up to the nearest *needle blight* and stabs at it, narrowly missing. He then utters a rallying cry, bolstering [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] with temporary HP.

[[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] hurls an *Acid Splash* at the **Young Gulthias Blight**. It hits, but the blight is hardly fazed.

[[Dorian of Galdor]] points his energy at the **Young Gulthias Blight**, unlocking its memories of its past self. This quietens the chaos a bit #skill-success. It also settles the root eruption.

The remaining **twig blights**, swipe at [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian G]] again, but miss. The **needle blights** attack next, hitting [[Grrurika]] and [[Dorian Blackthorn]]. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] throws another shield up to protect herself from the needle attack.

[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] takes out two **twig blights** with a single shot. [[Grrurika]] says a prayer **Bless** [[Dorian of Galdor]], [[Dorian Blackthorn]], and [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]]. #grrurika-deed 

[[Dorian Blackthorn]] casts a fire spell above his staff and blows the smoke towards the **Young Gulthias Blight**, calming it down much like smoking a bee hive. #skill-success 

The **Young Gulthias Blight** sinks its branches into the ground again for another attempt at an eruption.

[[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] kills the **needle blight** in front of him and rushes to the next one. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] casts *Sacred Flame* on another, but only deals minor damage.

[[Dorian of Galdor]] get a critical success on balancing the chaos. #skill-success 

[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] and [[Grrurika]] did something.

[[Dorian Blackthorn]] casts a *Chromatic Orb* into the sky, calling thunder and mana-infused rain down over the grove #skill-success . The the warm rain fills the place, the chaos settles and the blight is cleansed
### Blight Cleansed
As the magical energies align, [[Dorian Blackthorn]] finally starts seeing the arcane flow that has perplexed him all his life. The wild magic in him starts to tick in time with the timepiece he keeps on him. He starts seeing the cogs of the universe and how even the seemingly unorderly has an order to it. His subclass is now **Clockwork Sorcerer**.

A portal to [[Mechanus]] opens up and invites him in.

Around his feet, where the blight's eruption was about to occur, a branch catches [[Dorian of Galdor]]'s eye. He snaps it of and attunes to it as a wand — [[Wand of the Wilds]]

## Onward to the Temple of Dawning Light
[[Owlbear Grylls]] (who was totally a part of the fight and wasn't forgotten about at all) thanks them for cleansing the blight. [[Neverwinter Wood]] will recover over time. He continues leading them to the [[Temple of the Dawning Light]].

As they approach the entrance, a **Skeletal Giant Elk** steps out of the doorway, blood dripping from its antlers.

# Summary
[[Dorian of Galdor]] and [[Dorian Blackthorn]] complete [[The Arcane Convergence]], cleansing them of the wild magic that banes them. [[Dorian of Galdor]] receives the [[Wand of the Wilds]] and [[Dorian Blackthorn]] becomes a **Clockwork Sorcerer**. The party then continue to the [[Temple of the Dawning Light]]
