# Day 62 | Looting Gully Mine
The party try to fight off the statue as it goes after Dorian. The statue knocks Dorian out, but [[Grrurika]] revives him. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] runs to take the [[Ear Horn of Hearing]] from Dorian and offers it to the statue. The statue takes the horn and returns to its former position.

[[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] and [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] climb the ladder in this room to the trapdoor. They pick the lock and find themselves in [[Lt. Trike|Lt.Trike's]] room. He's just as surprised as they are to find the hidden trapdoor.

**Loot**: 4gp, 1,415sp, 1,334cp; [[Potion of Water Breathing]], [[Perfume of Bewitching]], [[Potion of Watchful Rest]]

They find **Toothpick** among the dead and the goblin dancer in a catatonic state from being swarmed by butterflies. They chuck [[Lt. Trike]] into the pit and use a javelin to bar the door.
# Hex Crawling
**Pathfinder**: [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]]. **Cartographer**: [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]]. **Scouts**: [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] & [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]]. **Quartermaster**: [[Grrurika]].

Amidst the heavy rain, Dorian leads the party on the safest route through grasslands. They find [[Ma'iq the Liar]] camping among the foothills and set up next to him. Grrurika cooks a fantastic salted boar stew and they share some with Ma'iq, who gifts them a **kitty doll** (with pins sticking out of it).

> Hex crawl received well. This is a good system, with someone drawing in a map as they go.

The next day, they come across a 3 day-old battlefield with ogres and barbarian casualties. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] loots a #tarot-card from the corpses. His fake eye sees *his* likeness on the card. [[Barabbas Tarot Cards]].

They also see a meteor shower, horde of barbarians hunting elk, and supply caches that tell Grrurika they're on path to finding followers of [[Marthammor Duin]].
# Day 64 | Twilight Tor
The first villager they meet is [[Boburin “Burin” the monk]]. He gives them a quick lay of the town. Barabbas and Eldina head to Star Mart to replenish ammo. **Thori Starforger** tells them he can't spare any supplies as the village is preparing for battle. Barabbas puts a down payment to reserve bolts after the battle. Thori gives him a pickaxe as collateral.

Dorian and Thia meet **Vedana the astronomer** and all three elves have a bitch session about other races over tea. Vedana also complains about being trapped here for 80 years, eating fish daily. They share boar stew with him and he lets Dorian use his home to have a hot shower.

Grrurika meets **Helga Löderr**. Helga promises to get Burin to take them to the [[Library of Whispers]] if they help defend the town from a possible werewolf attack in three days.
# Summary
The party toss [[Lt. Trike]] in the pit. They reach [[Twilight Tor]] and prepare for [[Full Moon, Yo!]] before Burin can take them to the [[Library of Whispers]].
