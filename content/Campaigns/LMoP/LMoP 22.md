## Quest Boarding
The party take [[Ronaldo of Triboar]]'s Quest. The meet him in the [[The Wee Inn]], where he tells them he has run out of red paint, and only the red from the cochineal bugs found in the [[Neverwinter Wood]] will do. The problem is, there is a family of aggressive boars around. He promises a group painting of the party after.

[[Gundren Rockseeker]] needs the party to talk to the gnomes in [[Gnomengarde]] to broker an agreement. Shared ownership of the [[Forge of Spells]] of they can help get it running again. He's up for creating a new Phandelver Pact.

The party go to see [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] in the townmaster's hall. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] and [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] wait outside. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] staring at the painting of himself when they enter. [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] makes him shit his pants. [[Harbin Wester|Harbin]] salutes [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]], his saviour. [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] tells them that there a spree of vandalism going on through the town. All he knows is it happens at night and he's the target. He tells them the only thing he can offer as payment for finding the perpetrators is his sheriff badge (worth 20gp). The party are shocked that [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] is broke.

When [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] helps to clean up some of the graffiti, they discover that magical red paint has been used. They suspect [[Ronaldo of Triboar]] and go back to investigate him. As a gauge of what he thinks of [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]], they start gossiping in front of [[Ronaldo of Triboar]], who joins in, complaining that [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] is keeping the painting of him all to himself when [[Ronaldo of Triboar|Ronaldo's]] art should be appreciated by everyone. They ask him about the red paint, to which he responds, "Is not his pure crimson." But he does note that the vandal has steady hands.

[[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] opens up about what happened at the assassination attempt of [[Harbin Wester|Harbin]] — it was her who killed the assassin, not [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]]. The party want to confront [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] about it and are sure that [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] is the vandal — [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] and [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] thought she confessed when she didn't.

The party asks [[Ronaldo of Triboar]] if red paint was easy to acquire. he says many people use paint, such as the sign maker in town and even [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]], whose hands are currently stained. [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] immediately confesses the the crime, caving under no pressure at all. She did it for the fun of it, but feels justified know that she's heard [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina's]] story.

The party think [[Richtor Helmsley III|Richtor]] is dirty — that's he's using town funds for his statue, but also that he's gambling or something which would explain why he's broke. They also think [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] might be blackmailing him.
## Milly Milner
The party make way for [[Gnomengarde]], stopping by [[Phandalin#Umbrage Hill|Umbrage Hill]] on the way so they can buy health potions. As they approach, they see the windmill being attacked by three flying **Manticores**. The beasts are complaining of hunger.

**Combat**
[[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] fires off a chromatic orb from afar, hitting one manticore. It fires tail spikes back at her, one hits and poisons her. Another manticore fires three tail spikes at [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]], All three hit and he goes down straight away. The third manticore makes a _Trumpeting Howl_, which frightens [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] and [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]]. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] fires two shots, then action surges and fires two more at the first manticore to get hit. It goes down. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] shoots a second manticore and [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] brings it to the ground with a spell.

The manticores try to flee. The one that howled manages to get away, but [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] manages to finish of the other with a _Chromatic Orb_
**Combat End**

[[Milly Milner]] is thankful for their help. She had been hounded by the manticores for the past 3 hours. She gives the party **4 health potions** as thanks and brews a pot of tea. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] grabs a tail spike to use as a poisoned dagger. She also helps them harvest 1 vial of poison and 1 [[Vial of Manticore Blood]]
