# Day 60 | Gnomengarde
The party arrive at [[Gnomengarde]]. They meet Thimbleweave as he's harvesting giant mushrooms. He gives them the low-down on what's been going on: Two gnomes, Orryn and Warryn have disappeared without a trace. [[King Korboz]] has also been inflicted with a sudden bout of insanity and taken [[King Gnerkli]] as prisoner. They're locked away in the royal chambers. (The party think it's a sex thing).

They offer to bring some mushrooms in for him and head into the caves. They go to the kitchen first and try to get information on what's going on. The mood shifts as soon as the disappearances are mentioned. None of the gnomes answer questions head-on and advise the party to find [[Fibblestib and Dabbledob]], although that means possibly running into Factoré.

The party get suspicious and suspect foul-play. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] sneaks into the pantry to look for signs of foul play, possibly involving food. All she finds is green mushroom bread.

They continue on and find a room with parked Barrel Crabs. [[Eldina Tosscobble|Eldina]] wants one.

## Factoré
Further on, they stumble across Factoré, who is testing her auto-loading crossbow platform. She immediately opens fire on them. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] leaps onto a crossbow and is swung around as the platform rotates. [[Grrurika]] ends the fight by casting _Hold Person_ on Factoré. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] carries her off the platform and puts her in a timeout corner. He also removes the crossbow strings. When the spell ends, Factoré talks to herself about the improvements she wants to make to the auto-loading crossbow, mainly protections to the user to prevent the same thing from happening again. [[Dorian of Galdor]] grabs her book and forces her to pay attention to him. She doesn't know much about what is going on and suggests they speak to [[Fibblestib and Dabbledob]].

## Mimic
The party find a room full of kegs. [[Grrurika]] inspects each of them one-by-one, finding mushroom wine inside each. One of the kegs turns out to be a mimic. The party get into a fight and [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] finishes it off.

The next room is guarded by a couple of gnomes who were under strict orders not to let any shapechangers pass. It was [[King Korboz]]' last instruction before he locked himself up. With the help of [[Ziggywix Tinkerton|Ziggywix]], they convince the guards that they're friends and are allowed to cross the bridge to the next section of caves.

They reach the inventor's workshop where [[Fibblestib and Dabbledob]] are arguing about how to best fix [[King Korboz]]. Fibblestib proposes a "sanity ray," while Dabbledob thinks that's dumb, and wants to build something called a "straitjacket" instead. The party interrupt and ask how they can help. The gnomes need parts to make their inventions, but the autognomes in the store room have gone haywire and need "resetting." The gnomes promise to let [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] go through the _Magick of Gnomengarde_ spellbook if they help shut down the autognomes.

The party recommend letting them speak to [[King Korboz]] first, before trying the inventions. They suspect that the king isn't really the king and ask for information only [[King Korboz]] would know, like how the two kings met.

## Talking the King down
The party go to the Kings' bedroom door and speak to [[King Korboz]]. He insists he was attacked by the rug his mother gave him while he was in the thrones room. The party check to make sure [[King Gnerkli]] is doing alright before they go to the throne room to investigate. They meticulously cast spells and poke everything they can find in the thrones room to check for mimics. They conclude that the mimic culprit was the one they had killed in the ale room. [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd|Barabbas]] goes back to drag the mimics carcass to the kings' door, while [[Dorian of Galdor]] brings the now slightly frosty rug from the thrones room.

They verify that [[King Korboz|Korboz]] is indeed [[King Korboz|Korboz]] by asking him for the story of how he and [[King Gnerkli|Gnerkli]] met. Satisfied with his response, they come to an agreement with him. He opens the door and releases [[King Gnerkli]] from his bonds.

The party broker a partnership between [[Gnomengarde]] and [[Gundren Rockseeker]]. The gnomes will send [[Fibblestib]] and [[Dabbledob]] to help repair the [[Forge of Spells]]. They provide the party with a sealed letter of the agreement.

## Autognomes
Before the party leave, they decide to help [[Fibblestib and Dabbledob]] with their autognome problem. They head to the storeroom and begin combat. During the fight, one autognome self-destructs. However, [[Grrurika]] manages to shut two of them down while [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] and [[Dorian of Galdor|Dorian]] shut down one each. As thanks, [[Fibblestib and Dabbledob]] give the party a [[Wand of Pyrotechnics]] and let [[Thia Galanodel|Thia]] study their spellbook.

# Summary
The party help [[King Korboz]] regain his sanity by defeating the mimic that attacked him and ate two of his people. They also broker a partnership between [[Gnomengarde]] and [[Gundren Rockseeker|The Rockseekers]], pledging to send [[Fibblestib and Dabbledob]] to help repair the [[Forge of Spells]]. The party will return to [[Phandalin|Phandalin]] to receive their payment of gemstones.
