# Elven HOA of doom
The bylaws buff elves and debuff others
### Buffs elves
- Ignore cover
- rerolls
- multiattack
- spells add additional targets
- circle magic
- pause all buffs for domain expansion
### Debuffs other
- spells randomly change targets
- spells have chance to alter to a funny element (e.g.. fire to gingerbread)
