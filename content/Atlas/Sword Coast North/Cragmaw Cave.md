# Background
Cave once used by [[Cragmaws]] is now a logger's camp. Timber needed for **charcoal**, mine supports, houses, bridges and wagons. Also charcoal-burning camp for iron production.

Trees are clear-cut. Potential for druid/dryad/nature anger. Coppice possible as solution. Timber burned in air-starved earth mounds.

- 5–7 tonnes wood : 1 tonne charcoal
- 8–16 tonnes charcoal : 1 tonne iron
- 40–112 tonnes wood : 1 tonne iron

# Quests
- Loggers report hearing axes in the forest at night, but no one is there.
- Charcoal kilns are being sabotaged, threatening iron production.
- A druid demands the loggers adopt sustainable forestry—or face awakened trees.
- Ore caravans are piling up because there's no charcoal to run the furnaces.
- An abandoned charcoal pit hides the entrance to an old dwarven ventilation shaft leading into forgotten mines.
