#settlement/town
45mi. west from [[Phandalin|Phandalin]] via road. 22mi. direct.

Small town near High Road. Between mines in mountains and coast from which **ore barges** go to ships. Abandoned many years because a wizard's tower went awry, paralysing all lookers. [[Dagult Neverember]] **reopened** the High Road to travel, tearing down the tower. **Working town** once again. All who live there owe allegiance to [[Dagult Neverember]].

Used to send ore from [[Phandalin|Phandalin]] to [[Neverwinter]]
