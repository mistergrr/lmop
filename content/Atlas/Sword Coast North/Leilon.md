## (LIE-lon)

**Location**: Sword Coast North
**Government**: Lord Pelindar Filmyra. Member of [[The Lord's Alliance]]. Firm ally of [[Waterdeep]].
**Population**: 3,000
**Population Diversity**: Human 80%, other 20%
**Languages**: Common, dwarfish, creole
**Resources**: Copper, nickel, silver
**Fun Fact**: Abandoned many years because a wizard's tower went awry, paralysing all lookers. [[Dagult Neverember]] tore down the tower. All who live there owe allegiance to him.

Mining town along High Road. Between mines in mountains and coast from which **ore barges** go to ships. **Lacks walls**, unlike most northern towns. A wooden palisade atop an earthen bank shields the landward side, but the wall has **no gate**.

The mines east are rich in **copper, nickel and silver**. The mountains are honeycombed with mine shafts and tunnels, including several that open up into the town itself, and some that go very, very deep. 

Used to send ore from [[Phandalin|Phandalin]] to [[Neverwinter]]. **No proper harbor**. During good weather, a dozen massive ore **barges** are loaded in the shallows, then poled and rowed out to unload their cargo on waiting **transport ships**. Even in the best weather, the operation is tricky.

# Places of Interest
## The Knight's Goblet
Caters to travellers' trade. Clean, boring, and overpriced. Proprietor roasts whole boars in the hearth. Serves hearty, large, nutty-flavoured loaves of bread with large slabs of the meat.
## The Orc's Tusks
Favoured by locals. Crowded, cheaper than the Goblet, and friendly. Taproom is dominated by an orc's skull with large tusks upon which patrons hang amusing or embarrassing items.
## The Sword of Leilon
Old, cosy. Warren of small rooms. Guests often get lost and blunder into each others rooms. Built on the site of an earlier inn where Leilon's defenders used to gather. That inn burned down due to misadventure, but the name hearkens to those days of local glory.
## Manyclaws Alley
Only dangerous spot in town. Reputed to be haunted by the ghosts of some trolls.* The monsters guard treasure that still lies buried in vaults long forgotten.
