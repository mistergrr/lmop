#landmark/temple
Location of [[The Trial of Endurance]]
5x8 Hex = 40 hexes

# Points of Interest
- (B3) **Skybridge** – A natural stone arch connecting two peaks; trapped or home to harpies.
- (C3) **Collapsed Dwarven Outpost** – Abandoned watchtower with ancient dwarven script talking about a "trial for the worthy." Contains runes that point towards the temple’s location.
- (C5) **Hunter’s Camp** – Remains of a lone ranger or hermit; (DC12 Survival) killed by bear or mountain lion.
- (C6) **Orc Encampment** – [[Orc Trouble]]
- (C8) **Luminous Caves** – Bioluminescent fungus and strange crystals; minor dungeon crawl or resting spot.
- (D1) **Endurance Hill** — Location of [[The Trial of Endurance]]
- (D5) **Howling Crag** – Wind-blasted cliffs; sound confuses direction, may hold a hidden cave entrance.
- (D6) **Avalanche Path** – Dangerous terrain with a high risk of triggering a rockslide; might reveal a hidden stair.
- (D7) **Giant Eagle Roost** – High peak nest; climbing challenge. [https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#giant%20eagle_xmm]
