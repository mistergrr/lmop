# Background
A flash flood washed away a collection of farms in 1426 DR. The resulting gully that opened revealed veins of tin. The area was converted into a mine. By 1477 DR, much of the minerals were depleted and the mine was due for shutdown.

Before that happened, a goblin horde swept through the mine, killing the inhabitants. Among the lost was a wizard named Flangdarf, who happened to be visiting to broker a trade of the old mining equipment.

Goblinoids now use the mine as a base. They attack passing travellers and take their loot.

The main entrance to the pit is a cave now overgrown by a huge tree.

# The Rooms
## 1. Main Entrance.
A huge **tree** has grown over the entrance. 
- DC 17 PER to spot **2 Goblin Sharpshooters** on watch in tree (high ground adv. + disadv.). Mounted on **Heavy crossbows** with **makeshift shields** (3/4 cover) bolted into tree. Unable to reposition.
	- **AC** 20, **HP** 10. **ATK** Heavy bolt: +5; 100/400ft.; 5 (1d10) piercing. **S** -1 **D** ++3 **C** +0 **I** +0 **W**-1 **CH** -1

## 2. Skitterling Nursery.
Stairs lead down to a Grand Hall. Smell of **wet fur and urine**. **Chew toys** scattered about. 
- **1 Skitterling swarm, 4 Skitterlings** around a **fire** are being sung to by a **goblin dancer**.

## 3. Grand Hall.
40ft wide chamber with **columns** lining walls. **Relief carving** of a huge dwarf provides entry to (area 4) (via mouth).
- **2 Bugbear warriors**, **4 goblin warriors and 1 goblin gang** eating **meat** and drinking **grog** around cooking pot and **dining tables**.
- **2 Goblin Sharpshooters** from area 4 fire into this room (3/4 cover).

## 4. West Bridge Room.
Leads to the **bridge** (area 5) that spans the **gully** (area 6). **Goblins** fire into any battles in grand hall (area 3) and otherwise guard the bridge.
- **2 Goblin sharpshooters**, **3 goblin warriors**
- **Treasure**: Mining equipment, coal, tin ore.

## 5. The Bridge.
A rope **bridge** 20ft spans an open **gully** (area 6). 40' to top, and 80' drop. **Rain** obscures the bridge. Built recently, but its supports are **stone** carved with fading dwarf **motifs**. Quarter speed to cross. Only single file.
- **2 Bugbear Warriors** cross bridge to abduct.

## 6. Gully.
120' deep with bridge (area 5) 80' up. Stream bed with **reeds** growing. **Broken scaffolding** lines the walls, DC 15 Acrobatics or collapse. Long-decomposed **bodies**** of miners and goblins. DC 12 PER to see treasure at bottom
- **Treasure**. 2x 10GP **Hematite**, 1x 10GP **Banded Agate**.

## 7. Empty Chamber.
**Climbing rope** going up, **spiral stairs** going down. Another relief **carving** of dwarf (mouth-hole is hand-sized). Opposite wall has "see/smell/speak no evil" drawn with **elf** faces.
- **Puzzle**: Press eyes, insert fingers into nostrils, and insert hand into carving. Reveals trapped door to Secret Chamber (area 9). **DC 13 Investigation** check to find button in both eyes and nostrils.
## 8. Boss' Chambers.
**Climbing rope** from (area 7) anchored via ring in the floor. Shut **door** to private chambers (area 9) in the rear wall. A **Hobgoblin Lieutenant** carrying a **key** is barking training orders at **4 Hobgoblin Pikemen**.
- **Lieutenant**: 116 HP, +3 dmg buff
	- Loot: Key
- **4 Hobgoblin Pikemen**:

## 9. Private Quarters
Large comfy **bed**. Hidden (DC 18) **trapdoor** (DC 15) leads to Secret Storage (area 11). Locked **chest** (DC 15 or key on Lieutenant).
	- **Chest contents.** 4gp, 1,415sp, 1,334cp; [[Potion of Water Breathing]], [[Perfume of Bewitching]], [[Potion of Watchful Rest]]
## 10. Secret Chamber
Thick layer of **dust** coats entire room. **Door** from (area 7) is **trapped**. **Skeletons** of dwarves and one human. 
- **Trapped door**. Heavy rock. DC 15 DEX save; 1d10 bludgeon.
- **Human Skeleton**. [[Amulet of Focus]], 3 **Potions of Healing**, **Letter** of the deceased.
> _(This note is covered in dirt stains)_
>The goblins haven’t found us, yet. They’re probably too stupid to even notice the door, let alone figure out the puzzle to open it. In any case, the dwarves have erected a trap to squash any unlucky bugger who might manage to come through.
>Curse those blighted green shits.
>I hope someone managed to get an emergency call out. I don’t want to die in this shithole.

## 11. Secret Storage
**Stairs** lead up to another room. **Ladder** leads up to Private Quarters (area 9) via locked **trapdoor**. **Rusty elf statue** on back wall. **Plaque** reads, "Let them listen." 
- **Trapdoor**. DC 15 to open.
- **Statue**. Holds [[Ear Horn of Hearing]] to its ear and a **sword** in its other hand. Attacks anyone who takes the horn.
	- [Shield Guardian](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#shield%20guardian_xmm)

## 12. Barracks
**Spiral stairs** lead down into barracks. **Weapons and armour** flank rows of soiled **bunk beds**. Smell of sweaty socks and farts. **Door** to (area 12) is barred from this side. **Sign** says, "Bad News." **Bugbears, hobgoblins, and goblins** reside in this room.
- **3 Bugbear warriors**, **2 hobgoblin warriors**, **1 goblin witch** **5 goblin minions**.
- **Treasure**: Weapons & armour.

## 13. The Pit
**Sloshing** sounds from below. Narrow **spiral stairs** lead 50' down to **alcoves** above water (15'). Water bucket **winch** from top.  **Chunks** of stone line the stair and alcoves near the bottom, appear finely **carved**. One recognizable as a stone humanoid **hand**, broken off at the wrist. A **Basilisk** swims in the water  — _Petrification_ can be cured at [[Twilight Tor]] for favour + 100GP diamond.
- **Alcove**. **Stone dwarf statue** holding a [[Horn of Blasting]].
- [[Basilisk]]. **AC** 15, **HP** 130. +50% dmg
	- Sluggish (cold blooded). 1 attack instead of 2.
	- **ATK** Petrify: DC 12 CON; 30ft; 2 targets; Restrained (save on turn end), subsequent fail = petrified. **or** 
	- Poison Fumes (R5-6): DC 12 CON save; 15' cone; 10 (4d4) poison or half **or** Bite: +5; 6(1d6+3) piercing plus 5 (2d4) poison.
