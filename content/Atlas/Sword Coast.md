# Wildlife
- Elk, lesser fen deer, and moose.
- Huge herds of shaggun (shaggy bovines, buffalo) range the central plains and hills, often competing with domestic cattle for pasturage. Provide food and tests of courage for the [[Uthgardt]].

**Far north**
- Reindeer travel in thundering herds (hunted by tundra barbarians).
- White-coated mountain ram in mountain. 

**Small animals**
- Rabbits, squirrels, red foxes, mice, bats, water rats, prairie dogs, beavers, weasels, and ermines.

**Common bird life**
- Game birds like the pheasant, grouse, quail, duck, and geese
- Song birds of all shapes, colours and sizes.

**Fish** are a diet staple of many northern towns.
- Shalass, found primarily in the Dessarin river network
- Szorp, a brown, trout-like denizen of the Delimbiyr and Unicorn Run rivers.
- Each spring, salmon run up the Mirar, [[Neverwinter]] and Dessarin rivers to spawn.

**Coast**
- Seals and sea lions, sea otters and dolphins.
- Though their breeding grounds are farther south, pods of whales are a common sight in the northern ocean.

**Predators**
- The northern mountain ranges are home to the agile red tiger or “snow cat.”
- Wolves are nearly everywhere
- Bears are common in all forested or mountain areas, while white polar bears stalk the Uttermost North.
- Fierce sharks and orca (killer whales) patrol the seas.

Also home to many  monsters.
