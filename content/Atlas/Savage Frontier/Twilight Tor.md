# Description
Fishing & mining hamlet. Simple wooden wall perimeter. 
>The surrounding hills keeps the village in a perpetual **twilight zone**.

**Government**: Led by priestess dwarf **Helga Løderr**
**Population**: 109 permanent residents. 40-60 transient miners/travellers. 
**Population Diversity** Dwarf (75%), Human (10%), Other (15%)
**Languages** Dwarfish, Common
**Resources** Fish, ale, mining supplies
**Fun Fact** The dwarfs here love playing pranks on each other.
# Background & History
[[Berun's Hill]] and Twilight Tor — *Mrinolor* and *Anaurdahyn* in dwarfish — southernmost and northernmost tors of [[Starmetal Hills]]. Splendid **view** of the [[Dessarin Valley]] to north and east. Both hilltops long used by followers of [[Marthammor Duin]] to **worship** him on moonless nights and to **observe** passing travellers and caravans on the Long Road.

The skies have been bringing **observers** since ancient times. Elves of [[Illefarn]] and ancient dwarfs once had observatories here, and even the long-vanished Golden Eagle tribe of the [[Uthgardt]].
- Elven **tower** ruins.
- Dwarf **holds**.
- [[Uthgardt]] **stone circles**.

In 1351 DR, 50 priests established the [[Vault of the Lost Wayfarer]] at the heart of [[Berun's Hill]]. The  **tomb** has long been the talk of legends in the North.

Twilight Tor started as a **fishing** village to feed those trying to reclaim the vault. Now home to miners, scholars, astrologers, and diviners. 

The dwarf use the hills to worship [[Marthammor Duin]] on dark, **moonless** nights. They love playing **pranks** on each other. Also follow every conceivable public holiday and **festival**. Primarily throw a [[Simril]] festival, with **stargazing** at night and various games & celebrations.

# Notable Locations
## Twilight Tor Chapel
Stone chapel 60ft tall, vaulted hall. Functions as **town hall & brewery** (often combining both practices). **Squat** doors. 28 monks who run brewery and chapel. **Alderman & priest**: Shield dwarf female, **Helga Løderr**. **Beds** provided (donations welcome). #landmark/temple 
- **Offering** survival supplies to [[Marthammor Duin]] grants advantage _Survival_ & _Nature_ checks 24 hours.
- **Adjoining Alehouse**
## Telescope
Gifted by a scholar from [[Candlekeep]] who said, "On one [[Simril]] evening, it will show a traveller a **portent** which could change history." Town tradition to allow all travellers to use telescope during [[Simril|the Winter Festival]]. Managed by **Vedana the Astrologer**. #landmark
## Tied Laces Tavern
**Waterwheel** on river mills rye. No inn, **diner-only**. Salted **fish-** or **elk-based** dishes. Run by **Runa Loseynsgrog** #landmark/tavern
- Onion rock lobster + honeycakes
- Swordsea hake carrots + oats
- Thistle-boiled flailfish + oatloaf
- Plumleaf-spiced herring + goat milk
## Star Mart: Miner's Exchange & Smithy
Located on road to **mine**. Miners come and go. Run by **Thori Starforger**. While the treasure of [[Berun's Hill]] is said to be legend, a group of dwarves are still secretly looking for it. 
## Rubber Chicken Fish Exchange
**Boathouse** on lake shore. Nets in water. Buys and sells fish. Run by **Reef Tiderunner**.

# Rumours
_Six rumours: two truths, two half-truths, two false_
1. Legend of a dwarven **tomb** lies under [[Berun's Hill]]'s, rich in **golden armour** and treasures. No dwarfs of today know any more than the legend.
	- **Golden Armour**: [[Set of Light]] piece.
	- Some of the miners are getting close to finding the **treasure** of [[Berun's Hill]].
2. On moonless nights, a long-forgotten princess sings **songs** over Lake Glorfindar.
3. On one [[Simril]] evening, the telescope would show a traveller a **portent** which could change history.
4. Some cave mushrooms sing if you listen closely. These give restful sleep.
5. "The bridge is haunted by the spirit of a tax collector. Avoid using it."
