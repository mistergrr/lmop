# Description
Strategically located where the Long Road intersects the Evermoor Way. Gathered armies have often set forth from Triboar to meet orc hordes.

Two good smithies and Skulner Wainwright, a wagonmaker famed throughout the North. Skulner’s latest project is the “rolling cog,” a massive wagon that can double as a barge.

 **Problems:** Triboar’s friendly rivalry with nearby [[Yartar]] occasionally erupts into violence when a gag goes too far.
