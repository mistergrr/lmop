#landmark/temple
![[Longsaddle-Abbey.jpg]]

**Abbey**, built by **dragonborn** priests of [[Bahamut]] near [[Longsaddle]]. **Gothic**.

Located **outside protection zone** of [[Longsaddle]]. Locals **accept** it, but put faith in the magic of the [[Harpell|Harpells]] instead. Run by metallic dragonborn **Shedinn Grrrmmballhyst** — rare outsider.

Study of **draconic history**, celestial law, and **moral philosophy**. Martial techniques emphasising precision, control, and minimal force.
