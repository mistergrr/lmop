A range of knolls that runs parallel to the Long Road west of [[Longsaddle]] and has been the target of several meteor showers in recent millennia. The hills are haunted by ruthless barbarian tribes, giving others little reason to visit the area.

Deep in the Starmetal Hills, a group of dwarves devoted to [[Marthammor Duin]] (the dwarven god of explorers, travelers, and outcasts) established the village of [[Twilight Tor]] on the shores of a cold, crystal-clear lake known as *Lake Glorfindar*. Few but the dwarves know of this place. Travelers of good will are welcome, and the stargazing is excellent.

# Geographical Features
The northernmost of the hills was *Twilight Tor*, a name shared by a nearby village on the banks of Lake Glorfindar. The southernmost was *Berun's Hill*. Both provided commanding views of the *Dessarin Valley*, to the north and to the east respectively.

The [[Library of Whispers]] is located somewhere here.

# 1d12 Encounters
## 1. Other
1. **2d10 Elk** (or reindeer). Not hostile and flee if attacked. **Treasure**. 1% chance per elk of pure gold antlers (250 gp).
2. **2d10 Horses**. Not hostile. Flee if attacked. **Treasure**. Can be tamed and resold.
3. Meteor shower. **Treasure**: Alien tech or iron.
4. Mammoth carcass.

## 2. Battlefield
**Corpses** of 3d10 [[Uthgardt]] barbarians and 1d4 hill giants. 1d12 days ago. Scavengers present if under four days ago.

## 3. Bandits
Bandit captain toughs (*rolled 14*), wearing cloaks and mounted on riding horses. 

1d4 | Attitude
-- | --
1 | Ambush
2 | Frontal attack
3 | Ask for toll (100gp+)
4 | Shaking down civilians

**Treasure**. Each carries a **pouch** with 1d10 x 10 sp. Captain's pouch: 2d10 gp and 1d6 gems worth 100 gp each.

## 4. Orcs

1d4 | Attitude
-- | --
1 |  Prisoners in tow
2-4 | Looking for new home

Prisoners are 1d4 + 2 strongheart halfling commoners belonging to the Woodhew clan. Oldest is feisty old gardener Ollie Woodhew. Orcs set fire to homestead and captured them as they tried to flee. One of the orcs even broke Ollie's favourite walking stick.

**Treasure**. If escort Woodhews back, the halflings reunite with family who avoided capture. Reward: **+1 blowgun**.

## 5. Uthgardt Barbarians
Tribal warriors and an Uthgardt shaman. If twenty or more, add berserkers and a chieftain (berserker with 90 hit points). If daytime, they are hunting wild game; night time, camped.

Use werewolf stats for chieftain and berserkers; chieftain 90 HP. Also give Grey Wolf chieftain 1d4 (_rolled 3_) wolf companions.
