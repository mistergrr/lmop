# Narrative Arc 2 | Tier 2 (5-8) | The Illusive Patron
>**BBEG**: [[Iarno Albrek|Glasstaff]] 

- A messenger, [[Tom Hagen]], hires the party on behalf of a mysterious patron — [[Iarno Albrek|The Illusive Man]]
- He promises them their deepest desires (and money) in exchange for their services.
## The First Job
Retrieve [[Amulet of Focus]] ^obtain-relic1
 
## Prepare the Stage

Use best ideas here
- Exploration — knowledge gathering
- Point towards Hero Transition
- As fun as possible
## 5. The Hero Transition (Break into Act 2)
The [[Iarno Albrek|Illusive Man]] promises to help locate [[Erynel]] in exchange for their services.
## 6. Promise of the Premise
- [ ] Gather the Next Two Relics 
	- Three relics, each associated with a different demonic entity. These relics are scattered across various dangerous locations.
	- [ ] 500gp worth of brimstone ^obtain-relic2
	- [ ] Hellish heart ^obtain-relic3
## 7. A Major Victory (Midpoint)
- [ ] Acquire [[The Angels' Tome]] ^obtain-tome
	- Location: [[Omphalos]]
	- Fragments of copies have been in circulation, but this is the most complete collection.
	- The tome's name is kept secret until the party acquires it (skill check to ascertain the name/purpose). But it does give off creepy vibes. Save this for last. If they read the book (written in infernal), they find out its purpose.
## 8. Bad Guys Close In
Thieves and assassins are trying to steal the tome.
## 9. All is Lost
The [[The Angels' Tome]] is stolen or given to the messenger. Either way, whoever takes it gives it to the [[Iarno Albrek|Illusive Man]].
## 10. Dark Night of the Soul
They discover that their patron was [[Iarno Albrek]] all along and he plans on using [[Erynel]] as a sacrifice to bring his daughter back from hell.
## 11. Finale (Break into Act 3)
The party heads back to the [[church]] to stop [[Iarno Albrek|Glasstaff]] to rescue [[Erynel]] and prevent whatever he's summoning.
