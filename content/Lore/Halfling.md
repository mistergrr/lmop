# Homeland
The Mootland. **Gifted** by an emperor as a joke after having a particularly delicious halfling dinner. Originally barren, but the halflings have green thumbs. Now rolling green **hills** with pockets of beech, elm, oak. **Tobacco** country. The capital is Schitt's Creek.
# Pantheon
Worship the human god, [[Helm]], as protector. Nowhere has [[Helm]] ever mentioned halflings, let alone saving them.

Other gods worshipped **casually**. 
- Marjoram — Goddess of home & hearth — Pie Week devoted to her
- Nicotiana — God of tobacco — _Pouch of the Grass_ is endlessly full
- Comfrey — God of farming
- Hyacinth — Goddess of fertility & birth
# Theft
No such thing as theft—only "borrowing without asking." Villages are extended families.
# Cooking
Renowned cooks. Most royal palaces have halfling chefs.
# Tobacco
Produce 90% of global supply. Fogmaker Red, Ava Prime Blend, Fuminator. Late-summer to early-autumn is harvest time. Big trade season. Town of _Gipfel_ in _Greenleaf Hills_ is tobacco capital.
# Altern Forest
To the north. Halfings stay away. Big folk enter, few return. Survivors wail with **madness** until put down. Cause unknown—**ghosts** of a **forgotten religion**, perhaps. Ancient religions are evil by modern standards. Blood harvested by ghosts fertilize the region. No such thing as a free blessing—**sacrifices** needed.
