Created by the god [[Corellon]], the first elves could change their forms at will. Lost this ability when Corellon cursed them for plotting with [[Lolth]], who tried and failed to usurp his dominion. When Lolth was cast into [[The Abyss]], most elves renounced her and earned Corellon's forgiveness, but that which he had taken from them was lost forever.

No longer able to shape-shift at will, the elves retreated to the [[Feywild]]. Over time, curiosity led many of them to explore other planes of existence.

Have pointed ears and lack facial and body hair. Don't sleep — instead enter a trance to rest. In that state, they remain aware of their surroundings while immersing themselves in memories and meditations.
## High Elves
Infused with the **magic** of crossings between the [[Feywild]] and the Material Plane.
### Sun Elves 
Aka **gold elves**, value excellence in all things. Keen sense of history, especially of the accomplishments of elves. Magical instruction is easy to acquire. Many day-to-day tasks are solved or made easier through routine magic. Intellectual people known for their mages, scholars, and seers.
### Moon Elves
Value wandering and the discoveries of the open road. Meet strangers with open arms. Easygoing, fluid people who value the ability to adapt to change. Many leave elven settlements for cosmopolitan cities inhabited by folk of all species.
## Wood Elves
Carry the magic of primeval forests. Known by many other names, including wild elves, green elves, forest elves, copper elves. Grounded, practical people. Value the forest and uncultivated nature over the glittering spires of elven cities. **Humble** about elven accomplishments.

Strictly **carnivorous** — won't eat flora of the sacred forests due to covenant with god. Blessed with keen eyesight and a talent for **archery**. Deep knowledge of the woodlands and their affinity with the fey enable them to conceal themselves effortlessly within their own domains.

Black, pupilless eyes; curved horns; and pointed ears. The elves of Lorwyn exhibit stark differences physically as well as culturally from their Shadowmoor counterparts.
### In Lorwyn
Horns are stubby like a goat's or curved like a ram's, but always ridged. Pointed ears are long and slender.

Value beauty above all, though what can seem arbitrary. Some assume authority over the land's other peoples, using their fallen empire to support their claims.

Blame the fall of their empire on outside threats, especially the Phyrexian invasion (see ["Lorwyn-Shadowmoor's History"](https://5e.tools/book.html#lfl,0,lorwyn-shadowmoor's%20history,0)). Though the invasion was the empire's death knell, its people's self-importance and unwillingness to collaborate with other species had already led to the empire's decline.
### In Shadowmoor
Horns are long, wavy, and covered with a rough, thorny texture. Pointed ears usually longer than Lorwyn counterparts.

Protect beauty, though definition of "beautiful" can be abstract. Many believe beauty is gained or lost in accordance with one's actions; Consider heroism and self-sacrifice the highest forms of beauty.

Bloomseekers are Shadowmoor elves who study and explore Lorwyn. Each one carries a [Reliquary of Twilight](https://5e.tools/items.html#reliquary%20of%20twilight_lfl) (see the introduction). Alongside Safewrights—Shadowmoor elves who venture across their realm in search of beautiful things—Bloomseekers are among the most trusted and revered members of their society.

# Aging
Can live for 2,000 years.
- **Mature**. Extreme alien beauty makes others uncomfortable. Tasks requiring concentration have 50% chance to fail.
- **End of Life**. Grow smaller once old as magic wanes and compresses.
