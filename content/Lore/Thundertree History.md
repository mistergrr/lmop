Small, quiet logging hamlet of about 90 folk. A good trail linked it with [[Neverwinter]], and all of the choice timber cut here was floated downriver to the shipyards, housebuilders, and carpenters. There was only a pavilion to sleep in and not much of interest to buy except some small pelts from local trappers.

A local ranger, Ansal Helmsley, served as informal leader. Ensured sustainable felling. This careful method was met with grudging approval from elves living nearby in Neverwinter Wood.

During the founding of the village, Thundertree was defended from monsters by the hero Palien of Neverwinter, who would be honoured with a statue in the town square.

When [[Mount Hotenow]] erupted in the Year of Knowledge Unearthed, 1451 DR, it devastated the village. In the wake, ash zombies drove off survivors.
