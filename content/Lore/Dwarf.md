_Dawi_
# Background
**Meticulous** planners. Always **seeking** lost halls of their empire. **Lifespan**: 400+ years.

Every dwarf learns **combat** from a young age. Train to **defend** against [[Underdark]]. Armies "_throngs_" built to **endure** — lean, efficient, disciplined. **Forage** to supplement rations. Can **carry** more equipment than other races. March with own **provisions**.

Don't glorify war. Fight for kin & hold, honour & vengeance. Reforge & repurpose **spoils** instead of taking trophies. **Record** origin, time & place seized, final use.

# Pastimes 
Deep comradery and time-honoured **traditions**. **Structured**, meaningful. **Feasts** of thick stews, roasted meats, and stonebread. Ale is a test of **endurance** — drinking contests. Usually start happy, but turn **sombre** with tales of lost settlements and kin. Reflection and grim lamentation.

Dwarves find joy in goblin hunts — sport and duty. 

Stone-hauling, axe-throwing, anvil-heaving.
# Womenfolk
Dwarf women are born at a rate of only one or two per every ten births. They're as strong as the men — some have even ruled as queens, though it's usually **temporary** as tradition dictates a suitable husband is found.

Daughters are seen as gifts from <'insert god> and their presence is seen as a mark of good fortune. Role is not simply to bear heirs, but to **uphold traditions** that keep the people strong.

Scarcity makes **suitors** ardent & ambitious. Brides wed into clans, bringing fortune to her kin. Grooms' fathers pay **dowry** equalling the **bride's weight**. Hefty brides bring more fortune to clans. Brides are **feasted** for weeks before the weigh-in.

Dwarf women **rumoured** to have beards. This is **false** — Mandela-effect. Due to the **scarcity** of women, nine out of ten dwarfs have beards.
# Brewmasters
One of the loftiest disciplines in society. Respected artisans. Stout due to "quality control." Unmatched constitution. **Trollbrew** is most sought-after.

Engineers guild experimenting with ale-powered combustion.

# Gold & Gems
Love for gold & gems is from tradition. It's a measure of a hold's **prosperity** and a clan's **endurance**. "Gold Lust"/"Gilded Fever" can inflict miners — uncontrollable desire to hoard. Specific **names** for different gold.
- Bryn — Exceptionally bright & lustrous. Most prized
- Galaz — Ornamental & decorative. Used for fine craft
- Gorl — Aged, deep yellow. Found in ancient treasure hoards
- Konk — Ruddy, reddish. Mistaken for inferior metal
- Ril — Freshly mined. Untested
- Frorl — Lucky. Believed to bring fortune
- Kurz — Unlucky. Used in dishonourable trade
# Runes
Dwarfs have no wizards, sorcerers, or even warlocks. Such are the domains of lesser races. Unpredictable & dangerous. Do not wield magic, but **bind** it. Taught and gifted by <'insert god>. Craft magic items & talismans.

Learned over **centuries**. Runesmiths dedicate their lives. Apprentices wait decades to inscribe their own. Runelords are the masters.
## Anvils of Doom
Used by the greatest runelords to forge **masterpieces**. Most **lost** to time. Many destroyed with hold. Others hoarded by runelords who have **forgotten** the rites.

## Death Practices
Proper burial important. Lost remains or desecrated tombs create wandering spirits. Proxy burial possible through ritual.
## Afterlife
Souls pulled down into earth to feasting halls of ancestors. Carved by [[Moradin]], who watches from his high table. Those who die in shame or dishonour **serve** the halls until debts repaid. A greater chamber reserved for souls of **slayers**, who fight forever.

Judged by wealth (prosperity is sign of wisdom & diligence), deeds in battle, achievements in peace (crafts, holds built, legacy). Ancestors may speak on behalf if properly **honoured**.
