# Theme
Lawful Evil, corruption, abuse of power
# Defining Features
Red Cloaks
# Goals
- **Establishing control in new territories** to create safe bases of operation where [[Iarno Albrek|Iarno]]’s agents can move freely.
- **Gathering intelligence, magical artifacts, and rare components** necessary for the resurrection ritual, often by extortion, theft, or intimidation.
- **Suppressing or eliminating opposition**—whether local leaders, rival gangs, or meddling adventurers—that might threaten [[Iarno Albrek|Iarno]]’s plan.
- **Recruiting new members and allies** to bolster their ranks and influence, especially those susceptible to power or magic.
