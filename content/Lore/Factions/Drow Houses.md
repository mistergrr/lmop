# Great Houses
## House Baenre (First House)
**Ruler:** Matron Mother Quenthel Baenre  
**Edge:** Religious legitimacy + elite infrastructure + prestige
- Seat of Lolth’s “approved” authority in the city.
- Controls major religious and political influence through priestesses.
- Strong but stretched thin after Underdark upheavals.
- Relies heavily on intimidation and tradition to maintain dominance.
## House Barrison Del’Armgo
**Ruler:** Matron Mother Rilyn Del’Armgo
**Edge:** Military strength + disciplined leadership
- One of Baenre’s oldest rivals.
- Strong martial tradition; often second in raw military power.
- Historically positioned as the “backup First House candidate.”
## ## House Xorlarrin
**Ruler:** Matron Mother Zeerith Xorlarrin (and senior archmages)  
**Edge:** Arcane supremacy
- Elite wizard house tied deeply to Gauntlgrym / duergar alliances .
- True power is arcane, not political.
- Often operate semi-independently, which limits their political dominance.
- Known for being patient, calculating, and expansionist.
## ## House Mizzrym
**Ruler:** Matron Mother Ilivara Mizzrym 
**Edge:** Political manipulation + espionage
- Specialists in infiltration, poison, and intrigue.
- Not the strongest militarily, but extremely dangerous in destabilising rivals.
- Often acts as kingmakers rather than kings.
- Capable of triggering house collapses indirectly.
## ## House Faen Tlabbar
**Ruler:** Matron Mother Malice Faen Tlabbar  
**Edge:** Brutal military aggression
- Aggressive, expansionist, and openly violent.
- Less subtle than Mizzrym, more direct than Xorlarrin.
- Known for internal ruthlessness and battlefield strength.
- Historically fluctuates between rising and crashing due to overreach.
