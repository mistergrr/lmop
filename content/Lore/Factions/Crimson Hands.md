The gang’s name came from their calling card: a bloodstained fist with a middle finger up, left imprinted on their victims—sometimes branded into their targets and sometimes painted on them in red ink or blood. It was a symbol of dominance, a reminder that those who crossed them would be left beaten and bloodied. Jargen pioneered his crew with his adopted brother, Malrik Bartun, who gave him the codename “Chalkline”, as Jargen would often be the reason his targets would be left outlined on a street corner. Jargen formed the Crimson Hands with the following members:

- [[Jargen “Barabbas” Crudd]]
- Malrik “The Aristocrat” Bartun (Human, Second in Command): A former street urchin turned Crimson Hand Underboss, Malrik ran the Crimson Hands operations with Jargen like a well-oiled machine. He was clever, charismatic, and had an instinct for sniffing out golden opportunities for the Hands to get to work. Due to being adopted by a Waterdhavian noble in his later teenage years, Malrik developed into a refined person of influence in high society with his adoptive father. Malrik would be in the room for important conversations with prestigious clientele, while Jargen would observe and strategize in the shadows. He saw Jargen’s talent early during their time together in the orphanage in Mintarn, and were inseparable even over the distance of Mintarn and the mainland Waterdeep. Malrik never fought unless necessary—but his true skill was in making people disappear.
- Varla Endren (Drow, Crew Muscle): A deadly fighter and the gang’s muscle. Varla had a reputation for finishing fights before they even began, using speed and brute strength over careful precision like her boss and mentor Jargen. She had a personal code: no loose ends. Jargen respected her, and Varla claimed Jargen as the grandfather figure she always wanted. She often referred to him as “Gramps” due to the significant age gap between them both.
- Durgosh the Grin (Orc, Armsdealer): Durgosh was the crew’s logistics man, running shipments of illicit goods through Waterdeep’s hidden tunnels and back alleys. His permanent grin came from his slave-master when he was a child. Being part of the Crimson Hands was a way to gain vengeance on his oppressor from the upper class all those years ago, and out of loyalty to Jargen and Malrik, he remained as a faithful smuggler for the crew.
- Kessel “Ratfinger” Brookes (Tiefling, Scout, Spy and Informant): A small man with big horns and even bigger ears to match, Kessel was the gang’s eyes and ears on the streets. If something was happening in the Waterdeep underbelly or in Mintarn taverns, Kessel knew about it. He always had the latest gossip, the best hiding spots, and a dozen escape routes planned in advance for when he and the crew were on jobs.
