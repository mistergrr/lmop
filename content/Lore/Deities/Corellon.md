*Creator of the Elves*, *First of the Seldarine*, *Coronal of Arvandor*

Corellon Larethian is the leader of the elven pantheon, and the very race of elves is said to have sprung from his blood during his many battles with [[Gruumsh]] of the orc pantheon. He embodies the highest ideals of elvenkind, serving as a powerful war deity when needed (his longsword, *Sahandrian*, is said to be forged from a star) and protecting his elven people with the careful hands of an artisan.

Corellon created the elven race and is the lord of the non-evil elven deities, collectively known as the **Seldarine** (“fellowship of brothers and sisters of the wood”). The Seldarine respect and honour him, and he maintains peaceful contact with the leaders of the dwarven, gnome, and halfling deities, as well as Faerûnian nature and good-aligned deities from that pantheon.

Corellon counts [[Lolth]] (once his consort) and [[Vhaeraun]] among his enemies, having banished them from the Seldarine after they led an attack against their kin. His other enemies include beings such as **Bane**, **Cyric**, **Talos**, **Malar**, and the deities of the orcs and goblinoids.

# Dogma
The elves are sculptors and wardens of magic’s endless mysteries. Bring forth the beauty that endures, and let the spirit gambol unfettered. Seek out new experiences and new ways.  
Ward against those who would destroy what they cannot create. Commune with the natural and mystical worlds. Be ever vigilant against the return of the banished darkness, and be strong in heart against the corruption of the Spider Queen.
