*The Masked Lord*, *the Masked God of Night.*
## Portfolio
Thievery, trickery, surface raiding, male drow empowerment, stealth, and subterfuge. Followers tend to have the *Darkness* spell with an extended duration.
## Symbol
Black mask with a single eye hole.
## Favoured Weapon
"Shadowflash" (short sword)

# Lore
Vhaeraun is the son of [[Lolth]] and [[Corellon]] Larethian (making him brother to **Eilistraee**, the dark elves’ goddess of redemption). While his sister seeks reconciliation with the surface and a return to beauty and song, Vhaeraun is more ruthless: he wants drow to rise to power, but without [[Lolth]]’s matriarchy or isolation.

## Dogma
The shadows of the Masked Lord must cast off the tyranny of the [[Lolth|Spider Queen]] and forcibly reclaim their birthright and rightful place in the Night Above. The existing drow matriarchies must be smashed, and the warring practices of twisted [[Lolth]] done away with, so that the drow are welded into a united people, not a squabbling gaggle of rivel Houses, clans, and aims. Vhaeraun will lead his followers into a society where the drow once again reign supreme over the other, lesser races, and there is equality between males and females.

# The Church of Vhaeraun
## Core Beliefs
1. **Male Supremacy (or at least equality):** They reject [[Lolth]]’s matriarchal hierarchy. In their vision, males can and _should_ hold leadership, priesthoods, and positions of authority.
2. **Surface Raiding:** Unlike Lolth’s cult, which focuses inward on endless intrigue, Vhaeraunites aggressively raid surface settlements for wealth, slaves, and power. They see the surface world as ripe for exploitation.
3. **Stealth and Subterfuge:** They value cunning over brute strength. Assassins, rogues, spies, and illusionists are their favoured agents. Followers tend to have the *Darkness* spell with an extended duration.
4. **Unity (of a sort):** Vhaeraun seeks to reunite drow with surface elves — not out of love, but to forge a new, stronger elven race under his influence. Contact and marriage with other elven races is encouraged.
