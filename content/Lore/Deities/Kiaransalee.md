*Lady of the Dead, the Revenancer*, *the Vengeful Banshee*
## Portfolio
Undead, vengeance.
## Symbol
Female drow hand wearing silver rings.
## Favoured Weapon
"Cold Heart" (dagger)

# Lore
Kiaransalee is portrayed in some legends as a fierce female clad in silver and translucent veils, and in others as a banshee. In either version, her hands bear many glittering silver rings, an image recognized as her symbol.

Drow regard Kiaransalee as the patron of vengeance because she is said to have died and returned from death to seek revenge, bringing with her an army of the dead. Different communities of her worshipers have varying accounts of who killed her and why, though the murderer is typically depicted as having features associated with creatures the drow loathe. Followers of Kiaransalee pay little attention to these specifics, as all the stories could be true—she is believed to have returned from death repeatedly.

It is vengeance that most strongly resonates with the drow, being a necessity in the life of every ambitious dark elf, often more than once. The undeath aspect of Kiaransalee is of less interest to the general populace, but those who practice necromancy turn to her for guidance and protection from the undead. Some of her most devoted followers seek the secret of attaining undeath themselves. Kiaransalee favors these individuals by returning them as undead—not as powerful liches, but as banshees, revenants, or wights.

Drow believe that Kiaransalee was driven mad by the repeated experience of returning from death as a deity. Her followers aren’t discouraged by this perception. Despite her madness, her actions are shaped by a deep and devious cunning—a trait the drow value more highly than sanity itself.
