*The Platinum Dragon*, *Fizban the Fabulous*, *Marduk*, *Xymor*

**Chief deity** of the metallic dragons. The Platinum Dragon. Dwells in the Seven Heavens of [[Mount Celestia]], but often wanders disguised as venerable human male in peasant robes. **Accompanied** by seven golden canaries — (polymorphed ancient gold dragons).

**Massive platinum dragon**, roughly **180ft (55 m)** long. Scales “tougher than any shield” and a faint blue sheen. **Eyes glow** with shifting blue light.

**Seldom interferes** in affairs of mortals, though makes exceptions to help thwart the machinations of [[Tiamat]] the Dragon Queen and her evil brood. Worshipped for dedication to **justice and protection**. As a lesser god, he has the power to grant divine spells.
## Favoured Weapon
"Bite" (heavy pick), claw
## Symbol
Platinum dragon's head in profile.
## Colours
Platinum, silver, pale blue.
# Suggested domains
Life, War
# The Seven Virtues
1. Mercy
2. Truth
3. Resolve
4. Compassion
5. Honour
6. Judgment
7. Sacrifice
# Dogma
- **Stand as a shield** to those who cannot defend themselves.
- **Act with honour**, even when it is costly.
- **Show mercy** to those who seek redemption.
- **Punish cruelty**, but never with malice.
- **Uphold truth**, even when lies would be easier.
