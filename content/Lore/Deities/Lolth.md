*Spider Queen*, *Queen of the Demonweb Pits*

Considers herself the true god of drow, though others of drow pantheon disagree. **Cruel** god who encourages her followers to backstab and betray each other, revelling in their **treachery**.

Can appear in the form of a giant black widow with crimson eyes, **True form** is that of a human-sized, exquisitely beautiful female drow.

Influence permeates every aspect of drow society—from politics and religion to warfare and family structure. Recently, folk unassociated with the Underdark have heard her call, guided by trails of spiders to join her aboveground cults. Disciples proudly wear **web-patterned** clothing and practice **blood sacrifice** on unwilling creatures. Followers aren't permitted to pray to other gods apart from [[Kiaransalee]] and [[Selvetarm]], Lolth's two allies.

Lolth's faith permits only women to rise to the priesthood. In the [[Underdark]], drow priests of Lolth rule the noble houses of drow cities like [[Menzoberranzan]], where they wield absolute power. Some of these cities' residents despise the tyranny of Lolth's organization, fleeing to the surface or to drow communities that serve more lenient gods of the drow pantheon.

Demanding mistress. Above all else, she demands sacrifices of treasure and blood. Screams of victims echo through her lightless temples. Altars heaped with skulls, stripped of flesh by giant spiders overhead.

When one of Lolth's worshipers shows great promise, Lolth summons them to the [[Demonweb Pits]] for a test of faith and strength. Those who fail the test are transformed into [[Drider|Driders]], but those who succeed become *Yor'thae*, Chosen of Lolth. Yor'thae exercise divine mastery over spiders and carry out Lolth's machinations.

Clerics of **Lolth** pray for spells either after waking from trance or before retiring to it. They are always female. Lolth requires regular homage from her clerics: submission in prayer, plus offerings. Her aid demands sacrifices—traditionally, the blood of drow faithful or captured foes—spilled with a spider-shaped knife whose eight descending legs are blades.

In exchange for devotion and sacrifice, Lolth bestows signs of her favour: great success in surface slave raids, the sudden and inexplicable death of a rival house’s matron, or the birth of an heiress beneath auspicious omens.

# Origins and Transformation
Once known as *Araushnee*, spouse of [[Corellon]] Larethian. Banished to [[The Abyss]] after attempting to overthrow Corellon alongside **Ghaunadaur**, **Malar**, and many other fell deities. Fashioned an arachnid form for herself and took her current name. Spiders of all kinds are seen as omens of Lolth, especially in the Underdark. Often sends [[Yochlol]], her signature demons, as messengers or spies.

The Weaver of Destiny bore her lover two children—**Eilistraee** and [[Vhaeraun]]—before she betrayed him and attempted to invade Arvandor, alongside **Ghaunadaur**, **Malar**, and many other fell deities. For her crimes, Araushnee was banished to the Abyss in the form of a spider demon, where she took the name Lolth.

## Silken Stewards
Certain disciples of Lolth don't always kill their victims, putting them to sleep with *Lolth's Sting* (chapter 3 DMG). Victim awakes cocooned in sticky webs, suspended from ceiling of a dark temple or underground room. In a matter of hours, a swarm of tiny creatures begins to wriggle in the victim's chest. The last feeling the victim experiences is a hair-raising, tickling sensation that quickly turns to agony as baby spiders chew their way out of the ribcage, killing the host.

Lolth's worshipers who practice this method of spider-egg incubation call themselves the *Silken Stewards*. The practice was pioneered by House Auvryndar, one of Menzoberranzan's ruling noble houses. The drow of this house found that implanting spider eggs into Humanoid hosts and performing a ritual to Lolth over the body caused the eggs to hatch in mere hours, rather than weeks. A *Lesser Restoration* spell cast on the host kills the spider eggs, ending the threat.

Silken Stewards provide other disciples of Lolth with a steady supply of spiders used as mounts, trackers, sentries, and front-line forces in service to the Spider Queen. The Stewards raise all sorts of arachnids: Spiders, Giant Spiders, and even Phase Spiders. Silken Stewards wear gloves made of the finest spider silk, and their clothes bear brooches depicting the stylized image of a curled-up spider inside a white spider egg. High-ranking members oversee the implantation of eggs and the accompanying rituals. Middle-ranking members capture Humanoids to use as hosts. Lesser-ranking members rear the hatched spiders and train them.

Followers of Eilistraee are among the Stewards' greatest foes, particularly Eilistraee's militant orders. They lead raids into the Stewards' hatcheries, killing spiders, freeing captives, and curing infested hosts. Silken Stewards are ever vigilant for Eilistraee's adherents infiltrating their ranks. Underdark-dwelling followers of Silvanus also oppose the Stewards, viewing the Stewards' methods of spider incubation and domestication as a blasphemous affront to the creatures' natural life cycles.
