**Role:** Melee Grunts

- **Description:** Shambling undead animated by foul energy. Their decaying forms are easy to destroy but can overwhelm in numbers.
- **Abilities:**
    - **Claw Swipe:** Basic melee attack with low damage.
    - **Undead Fortitude:** Potential to rise again after being reduced to 0 HP (standard zombie trait).
    - **Weakness:** Vulnerable to radiant damage.
- **Tactical Purpose:** Provides straightforward melee threats and an opportunity for divine characters to shine with Turn Undead.
