Leader of the [[Dead Rats]] in [[Neverwinter]]

# Description
A one-eyed wererat leader. 

# Backstory
He claims he lost his eye in a falling-out duel with the halfling [[Toytere]], the [[Luskan]] gang's leader. ln truth, Rsolk is following [[Toytere]]'s instructions to establish a foothold in [[Neverwinter]]. Together, the gang leaders intend to build a criminal empire along the Sword Coast.

# Drive
The gang is working to usurp control of the [[Sons of Alagondar]], which it is using to establish a trade alliance with [[Valindra Shadowmantle]] and [[Thay]]. Ultimately, the [[Dead Rats]] plans to eliminate the rebels and remake [[Neverwinter]] in [[Luskan]]'s image, albeit even more unstable.
