Leader of the [[Dead Rats]] in [[Neverwinter]]

# Description
**Class:** Warlock (Great Old One – with a pestilence-themed patron)  
**Appearance:** Cloaked in rags and leather, face hidden behind a rat skull mask. His eyes glow blue in the dark. His skin is pocked, stretched, and patchy with white fur.  
**Voice:** Raspy, whispering like wind through a tunnel. Constantly sniffs or twitches.  
**Tactics:** Ambushes, traps, and minions. Never fights alone. Always plans an escape route.

# Backstory
Born into a hard life on the streets, Rsolk often found shelter in the [[Blacklake Sewers]], living among the rats. He contracts wererat lycanthropy after being bitten by a rat. He tried to fight the curse at first, but grew to enjoy the power it gave him. He now spreads this power, recruiting a gang to carry out his plans.

[[Dagult Neverember|Dagult]] is clearing the slums that Rsolk grew to relish. Rsolk sees the gentrification as a fraudulent shift in [[Neverwinter]]'s true identity. Rsolk claims he serves "the true will of the city’s underbelly."

# Drive
- **Short-term:** Assassinate [[Dagult Neverember]] to star sowing the seeds of chaos in the city. He intends to overthrow the civilised world and to spread chaos and pestilence.
- **Long-term:** Ascend as a "Rat-God" through dark rites. He’s begun transforming, making permanent the hybrid form of the wererat lycanthropy and controlling swarms.
