# Mission:
Put an end to the [[Dead Rats]] gang.
# Location: 
[[Blacklake Sewers]]
[[sewer-2]]
# Setting the Scene
- **Atmosphere:** Describe the oppressive stench, ankle-deep muck, and flickering torchlight. Water drips constantly. Strange echoes make direction hard to judge.
- **Hazards:** Narrow walkways, broken ladders, and sudden drops. Make simple navigation a challenge—perfect for Perception and Acrobatics checks.
- **Signs of the Gang:** Graffiti (a rat skull), chewed corpses, discarded syringes, torn scraps of black cloth. Maybe an ambush site with blood trails leading deeper in.

# Encounters
- **Scouts/Lookouts:** The Dead Rats use wererats or mutated thugs who can scurry through small pipes. Consider giving them sewer-enhanced abilities (bonus to Stealth, immunity to disease).
- **Swarm Trouble:** Giant rats or swarms that pour out of holes in the walls to delay the player.
- **Puzzles/Obstacles:** A collapsed tunnel or a locked sluice gate. Require creative use of tools, spells, or brute force to continue.

## Act III – The Hidden Lair
- **The Gang’s Base:** A dry chamber built into the sewer system, repurposed from an old storm shelter or underground crypt. There’s crude furniture, stolen goods, alchemical tools, maybe even a shrine to a dark power (Ratrick, god of disease).
- [[Rsolk]] resides here, conducting his experiments to remove the human form from the wererat curse.
- **Options for Drama:**
    - The gang is working for a higher power.
    - The gang is protecting something or someone worse (e.g., something sealed in the sewers).
    - One of the gang members tries to defect and help the player.

## Act IV – Escape or Confrontation
- **Interrogation First:** Rsolk lets the player walk into his lair willingly—interested, not threatened. He offers a twisted deal.
- **Trap-Laden Showdown:** [[Rsolk]]’s chamber is ringed with traps, retracting bridges, and loose masonry. He fights while sending minions to flank.
- **Transformation Mid-Fight:** If bloodied, [[Rsolk]]’s transformation accelerates. He sprouts extra limbs, or controls swarms directly.
- If captured, the characters will be forced to turn into wererats.
